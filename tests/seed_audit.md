# Audit of the orchestrator’s seed findings

17 Oct 2026, quant review subagent. Seed scripts were run unchanged apart from paths, on `seed/data`, with `data/raw/macro/psx_KSE100_hist.csv` (Oct 2016 on) standing in for the missing `/tmp` KSE100 file. All numbers below come from those runs unless marked "claimed".

## Data problems behind every test

1. **29 of 108 fundamentals names have no prices.** These include HUBC, OGDC, ATRL, CHCC, ISL, SEARL, GAL, IPAK and ECOP. They were ex-dividend on 16 Oct, so the screener lists them as `HUBCXD` and the fetch asked for that symbol. The value test ran on 71 names, not 115. The same bug is in `scripts/refresh_prices.py`: `data/prices_raw/HUBCXD.csv` is empty.
2. **Days with no trade drop their returns.** `pct_change(fill_method=None)` followed by `fillna(0)` zeroes the move after any no-trade day. That is 50,060 stock-days, mean absolute move 3.7%. Mostly illiquid names (KOHP -4.18 log points, ITANZ +4.84).
3. **The corporate-action fix cuts both ways.** 10% bonuses stay in as fake losses (MEBL 22 Aug 2022 -9.5%, SYS 2021 -6.3%). The MEBL 15% bonus day is zeroed. Large ex-dividend drops are zeroed (EPCL 8 Sep 2021 -12.8%), so dividends are kept in price and `valtest.py` then adds DY again. Drops on stocks under Rs10 are never fixed (2,205 days below -10.5%).
4. **The 3-month lag is short for some names.** In Sep to Oct 2026 announcements, 17 of 73 June-FY names in the sample first posted annual results between 1 and 8 Oct, after the formation date.
5. **Survivorship.** All prices are ALLSHR as of 16 Oct 2026. Fundamentals are picked on current liquidity. Both push levels up and favour cheap names that later re-rated.

## Value test, the rerating question

Terciles are formed inside each cohort, so ranking against the cross-sectional median on the same date changes nothing: the ranks are identical. The market move from about 3.5x to 9x P/E does not enter the spread directly. I ran three checks:

- **Cohort fixed-effects regression** (year fixed effects) of 12m return on within-cohort rank: 24.8 pts with DY, 13.4 pts price only, t 0.6 to 1.2.
- **Without the 2023H2 to 2024H2 cohorts:** 27.7 pts over 5 cohorts. The correlation of the spread with the cohort market return is -0.22. Value did not just ride the rerating, and it was negative in 2023H2 and 2024H1.
- **Sector-neutral ranks:** E/P+DY -1.3 pts, E/P -9.2, ROE/PB +20.3 (t 0.64). With a beta control: 12.2 pts (t 0.52).

Verdict: in this sample, value is mostly a sector bet and cannot be told apart from zero.

## What changes in our beliefs

- **Deep value goes from works to unproven.** The headline needs the non-payer bug, dividends counted twice, an exact 3-month lag (at 4 months the spread is 4.8 pts) and sector tilts. Keep E/P+DY as a screen, ranked within sector. It is not a proven edge.
- **Rate cuts help, but the evidence is 3 episodes, not 15 events.** The Dec 2025 cut did not work. The first-cut rule rests on n=2.
- **The CPI-turn trigger is essentially one episode (Nov 2022 to Apr 2025).** Smaller CPI declines before that were followed by negative 12m returns.
- **Sector momentum is weak:** t about 1.3 after allowing for overlap, and nothing at a 12m lookback.
- **The momentum and operator "fails" verdicts stand, with a different meaning.** These signals show no edge over random entry, not a reliable loss. Pump-chasing is a little worse than random. The claimed -14.9% is unverified.
- **Fix the data first.** Map XD symbols to their base names, build total-return prices adjusted for bonuses, use actual announcement dates, and forward-fill across no-trade days.

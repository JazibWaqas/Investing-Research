## 2. The game you want, and the honest version of it

Buying something today to sell next week, again and again, loses money on the PSX in ten years of our data. We tested forty versions of it (buy the top gainer, buy the 7% day, buy three up days, and so on). All of them lost to the costs of buying and selling, and most did worse than picking a random stock. Trading every day turned Rs50,000 into about Rs15,000 in three months in the worst version, mostly in fees and small losses.

What does work is shorter than a year but longer than a few days: buy a stock that has a reason to move within weeks, write the exit down before you buy (a target where you take profit, and a level where you admit you are wrong), and let it run. That is the game below. It recycles money every few weeks, not every few days.

The reason right now is the 14 December rate decision. Inflation fell to 9.6% and oil fell 20%, so the State Bank may hold or cut. The stocks that gain most when rates start falling are cement, appliances and car makers, because their buyers borrow. They tend to move before the decision, not on the day.

## 4. How to run it

1. Place limit orders in the morning. If MARI does not fill in two days, pay up to Rs730. If others do not fill in two days, tell me and we look again.
2. When a stock reaches its "take profit" price, sell all of it, or sell half and keep the rest with your exit moved up to your buy price, so the rest cannot lose.
3. If a stock closes below its "get out" price, sell the next morning. Do not wait for it to come back.
4. Profit that comes out goes into the next setup, not back into the same stock.
5. Every gain is taxed 15% at the source, and every buy and sell costs about 0.25%.

# Catalogue of winning situations on PSX: recognise, join, milk, leave

Written 18 Oct 2026, prices to 16 Oct 2026. One section per situation type, then a decision procedure ("the reflex"), then a check of it against PRL and MACFL in 2026. Hindsight is used on purpose: the cases are winners chosen after the fact, and every exit was chosen after seeing the path unless a test is cited.

Evidence: `tests/winners_population.md` (382 liquid runs), `knowledge/winners/`, `knowledge/situations/`, `tests/winners_2026_postmortem.md`, `tests/winners_case_control.md`, `tests/BASE_RATES.csv` and the tests it cites, `knowledge/playbooks/short_term.md`. PRL and MACFL arithmetic: `data/prices_raw/`, raw closes, no corporate action in the window.

Strength tags: **[many]** a population test with n of 100 or more, or eight or more agreeing cases; **[few]** three to seven cases; **[one]** a single case, still valid if the situation can recur. Most cases sit in two or three regimes (2019 to 2021, 2023 to 2025, 2026), so even [many] means fewer independent draws than the count (lesson 16).

## What holds across every type

- **Winners are rare against their denominator [many].** Of 1,558 first 252-session highs in liquid names, 25.6% gained another 50% within 126 sessions, from 6% (2017, 2019, 2022) to 38% (2024).
- **The regime sets the supply [many].** Six-month doublers were 2.5 to 2.8 times as common with the 6M T-bill below the policy rate. 23 of the 34 runs of 5x or more began within three months of a market low.
- **Nothing here is a day trade [many]:** over 1 to 10 days no public signal paid after costs.
- **A 20% trail is too tight for most winners [many].** From the first 252-high it made a median 1.01x on 248 finished winners; multi-year runs 1.14x at 20%, 1.42x at 40%; shorter runs 0.92x at 20%, 0.78x at 40%.
- **Locks and gaps.** The limit was 5% to March 2020, 7.5% to 5 Jul 2024, 10% since; lock days may not fill and gaps go through stops (`HOW_TO_THINK.md` section 10).

## 1. Regime turn (the index)

**Recognise.** A hike cycle ending with the external account fixed: CPI past its peak, the rate held, the rupee stable, an IMF staff-level agreement or dated Board, an index higher low. Or a shock with a backstop: the index down 12% to 22% in weeks with the IMF and rate path intact.

**How often.** Turns in Sep 2019, Mar 2020, Jun to Jul 2023, Jun 2024, May 2025, Mar 2026. Bears in 2017 to 2019 and 2021 to 2022: 4% of liquid names rose over 21 to 27 months.

**Paths.** +35% from the 16 Sep 2019 hold to January; +61% from the Mar 2020 low to December; +190% from Jun 2023 to Dec 2024; +58.6% in six months after the Jun 2024 cut; +13.3% in two sessions in May 2025, a weekend gap.

**Join [few].** The second signal, not the headline: the 2019 hold, 3 or 12 Jul 2023, thirds in March 2020, the 9 May 2025 Friday close. Board approval was not the low in any of three IMF cases. Buying 5% below a shock's pre-event close won 78% at 20 sessions (n = 9, median +4.2%, worst -7.7%).

**Exit.** The regime reversing: a forced hike, a step devaluation, a stalled review. A 10% index trail is a placeholder; in May 2025 it sold the low.

**Failure twins.** The 3 Jul 2019 approval-day buyer was down 17.6% in six weeks; after the 14 Jul 2022 agreement the market fell five more months. The rupee and rates were still moving against it. **Liquidity:** any size. **Cases:** situations 01 to 06, 12.

## 2. Sector tide (the leader of a regime wave)

**Recognise.** Inside a regime turn a whole sector rises; its strongest liquid name closes above its 252-day high on three to five times its base value within weeks of the trigger (LUCK 30 May, UBL 6 Jul, MEBL 21 Jul 2023). Peers rose a median 44%, the name beat them in 91% of 238 episodes; foreigners were net sellers in 70% of 110 runs, local individuals buyers.

**How often [many].** 142 of 382 library runs (37%): 31 starts in 2019, 18 in 2020, 27 in 2023, 22 in 2024, 11 in 2025, 7 in 2026. 26 of 34 five-baggers began Oct 2022 to Nov 2023.

**Paths.** Multi-year, median 3.3x in 387 sessions, after a dead stretch of 3 to 14 months; the first 252-high at a median 1.40x with 5.4x of the peak ahead. Pullback depth is what differs:

| case | multiple | worst pullback | stop that held | late entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MEBL 2022 | 7.65x | 19.7% | no 20% to 40% stop fired; Rs100k to Rs649k | at 3.2x: 2.55x |
| UBL 2023 (not KMI) | 10.6x | 20 to 30% (Mar 2026) | 25% for 664 sessions | at 5x: 1.4x to 1.7x |
| LUCK 2023 | 6.6x | 19.8% | 25 to 30% | Oct 2024: 2.2x |
| MARI 2023 | 5.3x | 15% | 25%, out on the third limit-down close: 3.4x | at 3.3x: 1.17x |
| FFC 2023 (KMI from Dec 2025) | 9.5x total return | 15% | 25%+ | at 3x: 2.3x; at 7x: 1.15x |
| DGKC 2023 | 6.7x | 18 to 23% | 30%: +225% (20%: +15%) | at 3x: 1.54x |
| NBP 2023 (not KMI) | 13.45x | seven of 15%+ | 30%: 6.9x (25%: 1.0x) | Oct 2025: 1.04x |
| PSO 2023 | 4.5x | 35%, 30%, 35% | 40% only | at 3.15x: about 1.0x |
| GHNI 2023 | 9.9x | four of 27 to 34% | 40%, or 25% with re-entry | at 5.25x: 1.3x to 1.5x |

**Join.** The leader's first 252-high after the trigger [many]. Late [few]: at about 3x of the start six of seven policy waves still paid, at 4x to 5x mostly under 2x, at 7x or more under 1.2x.

**Exit [few].** A trail about 1.3 times the name's deepest pullback: 25% for dips of 15% to 23%, 40% for 33% to 35%; for bank and cement leaders, 25% until the index itself falls 20%. Event exits: a signed deal, directors' sales, the third limit-down close after a 90% blow-off. Most stops fired in five market breaks (Covid 2020, 26 Dec 2023, 26 Nov 2024, 8 May 2025, 2 Mar 2026).

**Failure twins.** Laggards that only tracked the index (MCB, ABL, BIPL); breakouts that never ran; the derating: SYS fell 31% from 7 Jan 2026 while trailing EPS rose 18%, at 25x against a 10% bill (situation 10). The end-tell is the multiple, not the earnings, doing the rising.

**Liquidity.** Mostly H tier (over Rs20m a day, so Rs500,000 is at most 2.5% of a day and far less for the leaders); L-tier names (KOHC, FLYNG) need the wider stop. UBL, NBP, BOP, BAHL and TRG are not KMI; use the eligible leader (MEBL, FABL, EFERT, LUCK). **Cases:** the files above plus FCCL_202304, BAHL_202211, BOP_202305, PIOC_201904, PIOC_202212, FLYNG_202308; `_policy_sector_waves.md`, `_rate_cut_and_shock.md`.

## 3. Crash reclaim

**Recognise.** After a market crash of 20% or more, a liquid name closes above its pre-crash or 252-day high within about three months, on several times its base value; in 2020, USD earners and policy-backed sectors.

**How often.** 106 library shock rebounds (28%): 14 in 2018, 18 in 2019, 12 in 2020, 9 in 2025, 30 in 2026 [many]. Median 1.71x in 77 sessions, mostly under a year; 37% of the gain left at the first 252-high. The single-name cases are [few].

**Paths.** TRG from 2.09x (1 Jul 2020) to 12.7x, with a 33% dip; SYS from 2.03x (10 Jun 2020) with 16% dips, 15.5x by 2026; AVN 1.54x to 5.5x through a 42% drawdown; PIOC +85% from 2x (23 Apr 2020); INIL +30% from its 12 May 2025 reclaim.

**Join.** The reclaim, never the high just before the crash: TRG's 3 Jan 2020 high lost 21% to 42%, SYS 27 Nov 2019 returned 0.89x to 1.06x, PIOC 10 Mar 2020 lost 24%, MUGHAL 3 Jan 2020 lost 25%. Late: TRG at 3x made 2.5x to 3.4x, at 5x 2.1x at best, at 10x under 1.04x.

**Exit.** TRG needed a 35% trail or a close 5% under the 100-day average (5.1x); AVN 40%; SYS 25%; INIL 20% to 25%. **Failure twins.** Rebounds that were only beta: INIL's 19 Aug 2025 high (28% of the gain left) lost 3% to 16%; TELE's 16 Oct 2025 high lost 10% to 15%. The tell: more than about 70% of the move made and no cause beyond the market. **Liquidity:** H or M by the reclaim. **Cases:** TRG_201906, SYS_201906, AVN_201908, PIOC_201904, NRL_201907, INIL_202505, TELE_202505; situation 02.

## 4. Ignored windfall, then a trigger

**Recognise.** A quarter so large the trailing P/E drops to about 2 (PRL 2026, ILP and FABL 2023, ATRL 2022), and the price does nothing for one to eight months because the market reads it as one-off. Then a datable trigger: the IMF agreement (3 Jul 2023), or a spread turn, volume and policy (PRL, 7 to 9 Jul 2026; refinery policy approved 21 or 28 Jul, sources disagree, unverified).

**How often [few].** Four episodes: ATRL, FABL and ILP in one 2023 wave, and the 2026 refiners as one. The library has 18 liquid results-jump runs (2022: 2, 2023: 4, 2025: 3, 2026: 9).

**Paths.** ATRL waited eight months, fell 26%, then 7.6x (open); FABL 5.5x in 39 months through 22% and 27% falls; ILP 2.8x while EPS fell, then -45%; PRL lagged ten weeks, then +155% from 9 Jul.

**Join.** Not the filing: the first 252-high after the trigger, with 95%, 83%, 66% and 65% of the log gain ahead (ATRL, FABL, ILP, PRL). Late: ATRL 10 Oct 2023 made 4.3x on a 40% stop, its 23 Dec 2024 chase lost 28%; FABL at 3.7x made 15%; PRL after the policy approval (about Rs59) made 81% to 16 Oct.

**Exit.** On price, since the earnings will not repeat: FABL 25% (3.3x); ATRL 40% or stop and re-enter (about 5.3x of 7.5x; 30% left at 2.8x); ILP 25% (2.0x, right). Refiner vetoes: the crack cap (from 20 Aug 2026), a windfall tax, a rights issue.

**Failure twins.** The filing-day buyer (ATRL -22% on a 25% trail). Of 408 EPS-jump events since 2021 the 20-session median was +0.9%; 21% of 2026's fell 15% within 20 sessions. No trigger, no trade. **Liquidity:** H or M. **Cases:** ATRL_202211, FABL_202303, ILP_202211, situation 07, `_results_jumps.md`.

## 5. Earnings inflection that repeats, and the fast reply

**Recognise.** Operating profit turning from a low base or loss and repeating for two to four quarters, often in a supply-limited sector: SAZEW EPS 0.41 to over 100, GAL -1.61 to over 30, GHNI loss to profit, AIRLINK under 1 to over 3, FFC doubled on its gas network. The fast reply: operating profit up 40% or more, finance cost down, and the price answering within three sessions on volume (FCEPL +10% on 24 Apr 2026; MACFL 12x volume on the 28 Apr board-meeting day).

**How often [few].** Four large runs (2022 to 2023 starts) and two fast replies (2026). Base rate weak: 31.6% of 408 EPS jumps reached +50% within 126 sessions against 27.0% for other results.

**Paths.** SAZEW 43.8x over 830 sessions after an 18-fold profit was ignored for three months; GAL 15.4x with a 53% crash inside; GHNI 9.9x through pullbacks of 18% to 34%; AIRLINK 7.2x as P/E went from 7.5 to 19; FCEPL 2.0x and MACFL 6.3x in 2026.

**Join.** After the confirming quarter or the first 252-high after it: SAZEW 31 Jul 2023 (30% leash, +2,250%), GAL 17 Nov 2023 (30%, +318%), GHNI 30 Apr 2024 (40%, 2.66x), AIRLINK 27 Oct 2023 (1.42x on 20%). Late paid in SAZEW (4.3x, +606%) and GAL (3.2x, +90%), not GHNI at 5.25x or AIRLINK in Dec 2024 (-16%). Fast reply [one each]: FCEPL 28 Apr, +38%, open; MACFL 30 Apr, +154% on a 25% trail; after MACFL's locks, +9% or -5%.

**Exit.** A 30% leash, re-entering on the reclaim of the old high (GAL's 14 Jan 2025 re-entry added 55%); half off when the P/E has doubled (AIRLINK); for the fast reply a 25% trail and no adds after locks.

**Failure twins.** One-quarter spikes (GAL Mar 2023, then three weak quarters); other income (NETSOL, Rs1.47bn against Rs0.20bn operating profit, -14% in 20 sessions); one quarter in a falling year (INIL, FY profit -59%); paise of profit (TELE, -22% in five sessions); a shock the same week (FCEPL's February filing, -25%). The difference: repetition, the operating line, no shock. **Liquidity:** L or M at the start, liquid by the entry date. **Cases:** SAZEW_202210, GAL_202212, GHNI_202301, AIRLINK_202211, FFC_202306, FCEPL_202602, MACFL_202603; twins NETSOL_202305, INIL_202505, TELE_202505.

## 6. Dated ruling or regulator decision

**Recognise.** A named regulator and a date change one company's cash flow: the NTC anti-dumping duty (ISL, 19 Jan 2017), OGRA's revenue requirement (SSGC, 1 Oct 2024: a 23.45% return against 16.60%). Volume ignites after it.

**How often [one each].** No library label; single-event policy rows in BASE_RATES run from -5.3% to +31.3% at 20 sessions.

**Paths.** ISL 2.5x in 82 sessions, a double top, then -31%. SSGC Rs9.95 to Rs49.52 in nine weeks to 30 Dec 2024; 0.47x of that peak now.

**Join.** Before or at the ruling, only the named beneficiary: ISL 24 Nov 2016 (+49% on a 25% trail, +90% selling on directors' sales); SSGC on the first 10x volume day, 23 Oct 2024 (+294% on a 20% trail), 5 Nov still +135%. Once in the price: ISL under 15% or a loss; SSGC on 6 Dec, -10%.

**Exit.** Directors' sales (ISL, 14 Feb 2017); a 20% trail (SSGC, 7 Jan 2025, Rs39.24). **Failure twins:** steel names without a protected product (MUGHAL, KSBP, BCL); SSGC's own Nov 2023 high (-22%); the volume rule alone (n = 788, 60-day median -3.4%). **Liquidity:** H or M. **Cases:** ISL_201610, SSGC_202308, situation 09.

## 7. Deal with a process

**Recognise.** A filing that starts a dated sequence with a price: intention and offer, a delisting resolution, a privatisation schedule, regulator gates, a fixed swap ratio.

**How often.** One case per sub-kind. Measured: after an intention n = 54, median -1.2%, worst -64%; after the offer n = 24, +1.1%; sponsor delistings n = 8, 75% won, median +64%; eleven takeovers since 2021 took a median 302 days to payment and traded 13% under the offer a month after.

**Case by case.**
- PSMC: bought after the 19 Oct 2023 delisting resolution (Rs221.39), sold into the Rs609 floor: 2.75x to 3.2x; January buyers lost 14%.
- PIAHCLA: 26 Sep 2023 on a 30% stop, 3.75x; it peaked two weeks before the bid, then -39%.
- PIOC 2025: intention-day buyer 1.73x blended (61.64% filled at Rs478.43); day-before-offer buyer +1.4%.
- AGL: the Rs39 bids were not the cap; a 30% leash made +176%.
- PTC: each regulator gate gave a leg; a 40% leash from 3x is +159%, open.
- FFBL: once the swap ratio was fixed you held the acquirer. MLCF, the acquirer, topped three days before its offer document, then -44%.

**Generalisation [few].** Buy the first unpriced step, sell into the decisive one, price a partial offer as a blend, never pay above the last offer. **Failure twins.** No price (KEL 2022, 0.93x); a control sale after a 24% run (CLOV 2017, filing buyer -21%); unnamed "interest" (TPLT, 82% of the gain already made); a narrow offer (EPCL, every entry since 12 Feb 2026 down; buy only under Rs27). The difference: a price, a payer and a date. **Liquidity:** mixed; most are not KMI (PSMC, PIAHCLA, AGL, PTC, CLOV). **Cases:** those named, situation 11, `_deals_and_sponsors.md`.

## 8. Sponsor buying

**Recognise.** A controlling sponsor buying in the market more than once after an equity injection, each purchase disclosed within days (ASL 2026, Rs9.78 to Rs13.78).

**How often [one].** Buy-backs explained nothing (LUCK 6.6x and KOHC 4.5x against a cement peer median of 4.6x). Insider clusters n = 37, median -0.4%; sponsor buys n = 5, -8.7%; single insider sales, 21-session median -3.1%.

**Path, join, exit.** ASL 1.98x from 19 Mar to 9 Sep, up 10% the day before the first disclosure. The 9 Apr disclosure close (Rs10.97) made 1.63x at the peak, 1.38x on 16 Oct; a 20% trail never fired. Add only on a new disclosure; sell when the sponsor stops and crowd volume exceeds its buying. **Twins:** the first-252-high buyer (+11%, then a loss). **Liquidity:** H. **Cases:** ASL_202603, LUCK_202301, KOHC_202301, situation 08.

## 9. Thin-float ignition

**Recognise.** A flat name under Rs1m to Rs5m a day, a first day of 10 to 40 times volume, 8 to 13 limit closes while its sector is flat or falling, a PSX query answered "no information", the buyer named only after the price tripled (QUICE, CRTM), an earlier false start in the same stock.

**How often [many, against].** 59 liquid "no visible cause" runs and 387 in the thin list, every year. The base rate is negative: volume with locks, median -11% to -14% under a 20% stop, win rate under a third, worse than random entries; news-less operator volume, 60-day median -8.5% (n = 675); after a PSX letter that follows a 15% rise, -7.5% at 60 sessions (n = 302).

**Paths.** QUICE 4.6x in 24 sessions; CRTM Rs21.54 to Rs67.53 in 12 limit closes while textiles fell; TPLT doubled, fell 51%, tripled; PREMA 5x, then half gone in ten sessions; JVDC peaked four weeks before its EPS jump.

**Join [few].** Small, on the first volume week: QUICE 8 Dec 2025 (Rs10.55) 3.1x on a 20% stop; CRTM 3 Mar 2026 1.89x, after the disclosure 1.57x; TPLT 11 Dec 2025 1.32x. Late lost: QUICE after the disclosure -21%, CRTM 9 Apr 0%, TPLT on its 7 Jan lock day 0.82x, PREMA after 2x -7% to -27%.

**Exit.** A 20% trail, the first lower-limit close, no adds after the buyer is named, a KMI deletion as a hard exit (TPLT, 5 Jun 2026). **Twins:** QUICE Aug 2024 (-39%), CRTM Jun 2025 (flat seven months), TPLT Feb 2025; nothing at entry separates them.

**Liquidity: the binding constraint.** CRTM traded about 2,600 shares a day by Aug 2026, so Rs50,000 was a quarter of a day. Rs50,000 to Rs100,000 at most; Rs500,000 is not tradable. **Cases:** QUICE_202306, CRTM_202409, TPLT_202502, PREMA_202311, NRL_201907, `_operator_and_unexplained.md`.

**Traps in every type.** Rights at par into a high price (UNITY, -84%); a temporary profit plus equity raised near the top (MUGHAL); value in one man or unlisted asset (TRG, -57%); re-rating on flat EPS (CPHL); wrong library labels.

## The reflex

For a name starting to move today. Stop at the first check that fails. Sizes and stop floors are policy choices, not test results.

1. **Can I own it and get out?** KMI All Share now, no announced deletion (`data/index_changes.csv`); the position under 5% of the 60-day median day's value (a tenth for thin names); no one-day move beyond the limit (an unadjusted corporate action).
2. **What is the regime?** IMF status, rate path, T-bill against policy, rupee, any shock in three months. In a hike-cycle bear take only a deal with a fixed price.
3. **What is the dated public cause, and which type?** Sector together: 2. Crash reclaim: 3. A quarter: read the operating line; P/E near 2 with no reaction is 4, a repeated or fast-answered operating jump is 5. A regulator: 6. A priced deal step: 7. A sponsor's purchases: 8. Nothing, thin: 9. No type: it is one of the three in four breakouts that fail; pass.
4. **Where in the move?** Buy only the type's confirmation (table). Stand aside above about 3x the wave's base for types 2 and 3, after the locks or disclosure for 5 and 9, after the decisive step for 7, after any 90% in three weeks.
5. **Exit and size, written first.** Stop = the larger of the type floor and 1.3 times the deepest pullback since the base, at most 40%, plus the type's event exit. Size = the smaller of the type cap and 12% of the book divided by 1.5 times the stop, so a gap to 1.5 stops costs 12%: 32% at a 25% stop, 20% at 40%. If the book is full, replace the holding furthest into its move (untested; momentum ranking failed in the 2026 postmortem).

| type | confirmation entry | stop floor | size cap | evidence | odds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 regime turn | second signal | regime reversal | the core | few | shock buy 78% at 20 sessions, n = 9 |
| 2 sector tide | leader's first 252-high after the trigger | 25% | 33% | many, few regimes | 25.6% of breakouts run |
| 3 crash reclaim | pre-crash high reclaimed within about 3 months | 30% | 25% | few | 37% of gain left at the high |
| 4 ignored windfall | first 252-high after a dated trigger | 25% | 33% | few | EPS jumps alone +0.9% at 20 sessions |
| 5 inflection, fast reply | after the confirming quarter; first unlocked session within 3 | 25% | 33% | few; fast reply n = 2 | same |
| 6 dated ruling | at the ruling, or the first 10x volume day after it | 20% | 25% | one each | volume alone -3.4% at 60 days |
| 7 deal | first unpriced step, blend 15%+ above price | the decisive step | 25% | one per kind | after intention -1.2%, n = 54 |
| 8 sponsor | first disclosure close | 20% | 15% | one | clusters -0.4%, n = 37 |
| 9 thin ignition | first volume week | 20%, first lower limit | 10%, a tenth of a day | many, against | -9% to -14%, win under a third |

## Would this have caught PRL and MACFL in 2026, and when

In-sample: the reflex was written after both paths were known, and free capital on the entry days was not simulated (S3 held MEBL, IPAK and TPLT on 9 Jul; check 1 would have sold TPLT before its 5 Jun deletion). Rupees are for a Rs50,000 book, before about 0.5% cost and 15% capital gains tax.

**PRL: yes, bought 10 Jul, still held.**
- 21 Apr: Q3 EPS 15.78 against -4.09, close Rs38.84. KMI since Jan 2023, Rs165m+ a day. Type 4: list, do not buy. It lagged the market for ten weeks.
- 7 to 9 Jul: 15.6m and 48m shares against 1.7m. On 9 Jul the first close above the 252-day high, Rs41.85, 1.66x the 2 Mar low (Rs25.20): the confirmation, under 3x.
- Pullback since the base 12.1%, so the 25% floor; size 32%, Rs16,000, bought at the 10 Jul open, Rs41.85.
- Peak close Rs105.39 on 2 Sep; Rs80.93 on 10 Sep, 23.2% under it, so the 25% trail held by 1.8 points; Rs106.95 on 16 Oct, a new high. Marked 2.56x: Rs16,000 is Rs40,890, a gain of **Rs24,890** (Rs255,600 per Rs100,000). Open into the 20 Oct results. A 20% trail would have sold on 10 Sep at Rs80.93: 1.93x, Rs14,940.

**MACFL: yes, bought 30 Apr, sold 27 Aug.**
- 28 Apr: 3.75m shares on the board-meeting day, 12 times normal: a watch. 29 Apr, 08:47: Q3 EPS 1.83 against 1.03, operating profit Rs99m against Rs43m (PSX doc 275609); about Rs0.45 of EPS is a tax credit, but the operating line more than doubled. Type 5, fast reply. KMI, Rs8m a day. Locked at Rs26.69.
- Entry: the first session to open below the limit, 30 Apr at Rs27.00, 1.38x the 9 Mar low (Rs19.60). Pullback since the base 12.4%, so a 25% trail; size 32%, Rs16,000; no adds during the ten limit closes.
- The 25 Aug close (Rs69.28) was over 25% under the 19 Aug high close (Rs92.56); sold at the 27 Aug open, Rs68.99: 2.56x, Rs40,880, a gain of **Rs24,880** (Rs255,500 per Rs100,000).
- It missed Rs56.54 (28 Aug) to Rs123.87 (14 Oct): the 2 Sep results (Q4 EPS 0.28) gave no type, so no re-entry, which also avoided the 15 and 16 Oct lower limits.

Together about **Rs49,770** on a Rs50,000 book, 64% of it in the two from July to August. The breakout rule got neither: MACFL's first 252-high (15 Jun) had a third of the gain left, and S3 was crowded out of PRL on all 26 signal days. The reflex is earlier because it reads the cause before the chart. It would have held QUICE, CRTM and JVDC to 10% positions and kept TPLT out after 5 Jun, but it has not been run over every 2026 mover; only live scoring can test it.

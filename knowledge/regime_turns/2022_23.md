# The 2022-23 regime turn

Written 19 Oct 2026, prices to 16 Oct 2026. The tables show what was public at each week's close; (hindsight) marks later data. Indexes are the repo's KSE100 and KMI All Share total-return series. "Liquid" is the daily table's rule (median 60-day traded value above Rs5m; 50 to 56 names here). Breadth uses adjusted closes against each name's own 50 and 200-session averages.

## Short version

The scare was real: SBP net reserves fell from $8.6bn (22 Jul 2022) to $2.9bn (3 Feb 2023), ratings were cut through February, the rupee was released on 25 to 26 Jan (USD/PKR +5.6% in one day, 228 to 283 by March) and the SBP made eleven hikes from 7% to 22%. The KSE100 traded between 38,342 and 46,602 for 14 months, then rose 190% by 16 Dec 2024 and 4.35x by 30 Dec 2025 (174,473); on 16 Oct 2026 it is 4.15x the low. Four bounces failed or fizzled first. What set the real one apart was not the 50-day average, volume, foreign buying, the rupee or the bills. It was breadth (over half the liquid names above their 200-session average within a week, nine or ten at 252-highs) and money arriving: an IMF staff agreement with $3bn of bilateral support.

**Dating the low.** 23 Jun 2023 (KSE100 40,065) is the reference, as briefed. The lowest rupee close was 17 Jan 2023 (38,342, 4.3% lower) and the lowest in dollars was 5 Apr 2023 (138.2 per index point; 23 Jun is 1.2% above). Since January each rupee low was higher (38,342, 39,687, 40,065), a base that kept rising under a currency that fell 20%.

## 2. Price structure of the low

- **Tests.** The 38.3k to 40.1k floor was tested four times after December: 28 Dec (39,279), 17 Jan (38,342), 4 Apr (39,687), 23 Jun (40,065), the last three each higher. 28 of the 29 closes at or under 40,100 since 1 Dec came before 23 Jun.
- **Held.** The week to 30 Jun (two sessions) was the first to hold: 26 Jun (41,437) is the lowest close since and the index has not closed under it. The first full week was 3 to 7 Jul.
- **First close above the 50-session average:** 26 Jun, 41,437 against 41,257 (KMI 20,421 against 19,964), also above the 200-session average. On its own it meant little: the index had crossed on 20 Apr too.
- **Index volume:** 122m shares on 26 Jun (1.66x the 50-day average), 138m on 27 Jun, 170m on 3 Jul (2.17x), 245m on 4 Jul (2.99x, the peak).
- **First 252-highs in liquid names:** 27 Jun, BIPL (6.3x its 20-day volume, on a takeover offer) and KOHC (1.4x); on 3 Jul LUCK 1.5x, MLCF 0.5x, HUBC 0.9x, ATRL 1.6x, PIOC 0.7x. The volume was in the market, not the stocks.
- **Index confirmation:** the KSE100 closed above its prior 252-high on 3 Jul (43,899 against 43,677), the KMI on 11 Jul.

Only FB1 and FB2 were false in price: both lost their lows within six months. FB3 and FB4 were early, not wrong: their lows held and they returned +4% and +12% in 12 weeks and +50% and +75% in a year, but against a bill paying about 5% a quarter they were poor trades (FB3 was 7.5% under water in dollars by 23 Jun).

## 5. What the reflex would have done, and what it earned

Signals are read at the close, entries at the next open. 23 Jun plus one week has no session (28 to 30 Jun closed), so the first signal is 3 Jul. Reflex: check 1, KMI index, liquid. Check 2, regime: staff agreement signed, Board dated, budget passed, CPI off its peak, rupee steady four months; the bills and reserves ($4.1bn) added nothing. ## Caveats

- Six turns, three of them shocks, one chosen by me: "turns sharing it" counts are small by construction (lesson 16) and 12-month results are in-sample.
- The low is a choice; with the January rupee low or April dollar low every 12-month figure is better.
- EPS pairs are from an aggregator, consolidation unverified. Only BIPL (doc 211567), ENGRO and HUBC (211088, 211161) filings were opened.
- The Eid closure of 28 to 30 Jun, the 29 Jun SLA date and the January "cap" removal rest on data gaps, the seed file and general knowledge; the rupee jump is measured (ES 86).
- Breadth is over 50 to 56 names, so a point is half a name; ENGRO and PSMC are missing from the adjusted panel.

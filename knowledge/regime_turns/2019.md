# The mid-2019 turn: the KSE100 low of 16 August 2019

Written 19 Oct 2026; extends situation 01 with weekly detail. **Knowable** means the number existed on that date; **hindsight** means it needed later data. Index is `kse100.csv` closes (its open, high and low columns look shifted a day and are not used). Breadth is the share of liquid names (41 to 44, the daily table's `liquid` flag, over Rs5m a day) above their 50- and 200-session average. *Drawdown* is the fall from the 24 May 2017 peak (52,876); a *252-high* is a close above every close of the prior 252 sessions; a *cutoff yield* is the rate the Treasury paid at a bill auction.

## 1. What the turn was, in short

The low was 15.1% below the day after the staff-level agreement (SLA, 12 May: 33,900 on 13 May), 17.6% below the Board approval (3 Jul: 34,897) and 12.8% below the 16 Jul hike to 13.25%, whose statement said "the adjustment related to interest rates and the exchange rate from previously accumulated imbalances has taken place". All were knowable; none was the low. The index then rose 19.9% in eight weeks (34,476 on 11 Oct), peaked at 43,219 on 13 Jan 2020 (+50.2%), fell to 27,229 on 25 Mar 2020 (Covid, 5.3% under the August low) and was +40.1% on 13 Aug 2020, twelve months on.

## 3. The price structure of the low (hindsight for the count, knowable for the days)

- **Tests.** The close of 28,765 was not undercut for seven months. Retests: 30 Aug (29,672, +3.2%) and 3 Sep (29,810, +3.6%), both holding. The first week that held (no close below the low, higher weekly low) was 19 to 23 Aug, +9.0%; the next week gave back 5.4% but its low was higher.
- **Volume.** The four up days 19 to 22 Aug ran 1.25, 1.76, 1.67 and 2.63 times the 20-session average; the 30 Aug retest 1.04. Volume followed the low.
- **First close above the 50-session average:** 16 Sep (31,929 against 31,771), 31 days on, the day of the hold, on 0.85 times volume. **Above the 200-session average:** 5 Nov (35,358, 1.49 times). **First index 252-high:** 16 Dec.
- **First 252-high in a liquid name: ILP on 15 Nov**, 91 days after the low, on 4.4 times volume. No liquid name set one in the first eight weeks; 18 thin ones did (first PMRS 19 Aug). Twenty-two liquid names set one within six months.
- **Other lows:** the first liquid 252-high came 22 days after Mar 2020, 9 after Jan 2023, 4 after Jun 2023. In 2019 the gate was late because the bear was deeper (33% below the 252-session high at the low, against 8% to 18% in 2023).

## 8. Limits

- One turn. `kse100.csv` is labelled total return in INDEX_NOTES but its levels match the headline price index (unresolved; situations INDEX caveat).
- The liquid flag trails 60 sessions, so names that rose into liquidity enter late; breadth uses 41 to 44 names, delisted ones until their last trade.
- Flows are from scstrade.com (NCCPL is blocked from this machine); "other organisations" is undefined there. Sector medians use 1 to 7 names.
- MPS quotes: SBP PDFs of 20 May, 16 Jul and 16 Sep 2019 (https://www.sbp.org.pk/assets/document/publications/MPS-May-2019-Eng.pdf, and the Jul and Sep equivalents), read 19 Oct 2026. The Article 370 date, the holidays and the CPI release dates are not in the data files.
- Every 12-month result contains the Covid crash, unrelated to the 2019 turn.

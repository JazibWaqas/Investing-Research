# What PSX practitioners watch: documented signals and tests

Written 17 October 2026. Evidence cutoff: 16 October close and publications available by 17 October. This is a map of claims, not a finding that any signal earns excess return. The ranking below is a judgment from accessible 2025 to 2026 strategy reports and commentary, not a frequency count of all brokers. Public reports or attributable commentary were found for Topline, AHL, JS Global, AKD, Foundation, Taurus, KTrade and Ismail Iqbal. Direct strategy text for Intermarket, Optimus and Alfalah CLSA was not found, so no house view is assigned to them. Forum claims are labelled as anecdotes.

## 1. What institutions say moves the market, ranked

1. **Rates, inflation and the return on cash.** [AKD's 2026 strategy](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/akd-securities_pakistan-strategy-kse100-setting-new-benchmarks-activity-7412213227784171520-hpJ7), published around January 2026, put further monetary easing at the front of its bull case. [Foundation's 2026 strategy](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/foundation-securities-pvt-ltd_fsl-pakistan-strategy-2026-activity-7402676268762275840-XUBG), published December 2025, expected flows away from less attractive debt and property returns. [JS Global's 3 January 2026 report](https://www.jsglobalonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/JS-Global-Pakistan-Equity-Outlook-2026.pdf) tied valuations to the preceding rate cuts and inflation path. The rate is now a two-sided risk: [SBP held at 11.5% on 14 September 2026](https://www.sbp.org.pk/DFMD/pr.asp), after April's hike. Test changes in expected T-bill yields, not just the MPC headline, because the market sees auction rates first.

2. **IMF, external financing and the rupee.** [JS Global, 3 January 2026](https://www.jsglobalonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/JS-Global-Pakistan-Equity-Outlook-2026.pdf) credited the IMF reform anchor for macro stability and flagged delayed external financing as a risk. [Topline's 2026 outlook, reported by Nukta on 10 November 2025](https://nukta.com/pakistan-stock-market-surge-2026) listed ratings, Eurobond/Sukuk issuance and circular-debt cleanup among its triggers. This factor works through reserve confidence, PKR expectations and the discount rate. The repo's earlier test found IMF staff-level-agreement timing weak; do not reverse that conclusion from a broker narrative.

3. **Earnings, cash dividends and valuation relative to bonds.** [JS Global's 2026 outlook](https://www.jsglobalonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/JS-Global-Pakistan-Equity-Outlook-2026.pdf) said earnings growth plus dividend yield would contribute materially to its target. [Ismail Iqbal's 2026 outlook](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/iisec_pakistan-outlook-2026-activity-7408898167230021632-Pqr3) described a change from broad rerating to earnings delivery. [AHL's 27 December 2025 strategy summary](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ali-najib-64881b44_ahl-strategy-cy26-activity-7410543928887746560-jeSM) expected a slower, more selective year. Earnings quality and consolidated EPS matter more than index targets, which were forecasts made before 2026's shocks.

4. **Policy at the sector level.** Topline's same outlook named LNG deferral, gas and power debt, PIA privatisation, a new NFC formula, Reko Diq and FY27 budget relief. [AKD's 2026 strategy](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/akd-securities_pakistan-strategy-kse100-setting-new-benchmarks-activity-7412213227784171520-hpJ7) stressed E&P and OMC cash collection. These are distinct, dated policy bets. A refinery diesel crack cap, gas tariff or OMC margin notification changes earnings before it changes the index. Track the actual gazette or PSX filing, not the rumour.

5. **Oil, geopolitics and the current account.** Topline and KTrade were cited on elevated crude as an October sentiment risk in the [17 October 2026 Dawn market wrap](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037721) and [16 October Express Tribune wrap](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2635095). [JS Global's January report](https://www.jsglobalonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/JS-Global-Pakistan-Equity-Outlook-2026.pdf) had already named India and Afghanistan border risks. Oil has opposite sector effects and an indirect import-bill effect, so a single index beta will blur the mechanism.

6. **Liquidity, flows and index composition.** [Foundation's December 2025 strategy](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/foundation-securities-pvt-ltd_fsl-pakistan-strategy-2026-activity-7402676268762275840-XUBG) pointed to trading liquidity and movement from other assets. [NCCPL describes daily FIPI and LIPI publication](https://www.nccpl.com.pk/ur/uin). [Taurus's December 2025 investment outlook](https://taurus-sec.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Pakistan-Investment-Outlook-2026.pdf) charts mutual-fund equity allocation, investor counts and historic asset-class returns, useful context but not proof of who led a particular move. Compare foreign, mutual-fund and broker-house net buying to later returns, with same-day mechanical correlation removed.

The ranking is conditional. In October 2026 oil and politics can dominate for a week, while the long-run allocation decision still runs through rates, the IMF anchor and earnings. Topline's 2026 target, JS Global's target and AHL's target differed, a reminder that agreement on drivers is not agreement on prices. Weekly wrap phrases such as “profit-taking”, “value hunting” and “lack of triggers” are retrospective labels unless tied to a measurable variable.

## 2. How informed capital actually appears in the record

Sponsor stakes, related companies, directors and broker accounts are observable in different places. [PSX regulation 5.6.4](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/themes/psx/uploads/PSX-Regulations-November-24-2025.pdf) requires prompt disclosure when a director, CEO, executive, substantial shareholder or spouse trades the company's shares. Regulation 5.6.3 lets PSX demand an explanation of unusual price or volume. The [PSX announcement board](https://dps.psx.com.pk/announcements/psx) displayed such requests for Noon Sugar and Dewan Sugar on 13 October 2026. An unusual-movement letter is a question, not an adverse finding. Associated-company purchases and sponsor buybacks can change ownership concentration and free float; they are not evidence of illegality by themselves.

The strongest documented case of pre-event positioning is the [SECP complaint of 7 August 2025](https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Press-Release-SECP-Files-Criminal-Case-on-charges-of-insider-trading.pdf). SECP alleged that a listed company's secretary shared a planned sponsor buyback and delisting with relatives and an associated-company CEO. Accounts accumulated shares before disclosure and sold after the announcement. SECP did not name the issuer or defendants in its public release, and the complaint is an allegation, not a conviction. It establishes a concrete alleged mechanism: information passes through a corporate officer, related accounts buy during the non-public window, then exit to public buyers after the filing.

In [March 2024](https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Press-Release-March-6-SECP-files-criminal-cases-against-individuals-involved-in-stock-market-manipulation.pdf), SECP filed four complaints involving sponsors and brokerage management for alleged price manipulation in three listed shares during 2019 to 2021. It described high-priced cross-trades, cancelled buy orders and misuse of client accounts. Names were withheld in the release, so assigning them would be invention. The named [Zakir Hussain Somji, then HMB investment AVP, was convicted in June 2025](https://www.secp.gov.pk/media-center/press-releases/first-ever-conviction-for-offence-of-insider-trading-in-the-history-of-pakistan/) for trading around his bank's investment decisions, based on 2014 to 2016 trades. This is an institutional-information case, not a company-results leak. [SECP's FY2018 and FY2019 reports](https://www.secp.gov.pk/document/annual-report-2019/?wpdmdl=38692) recorded manipulation and insider-trading enquiries, while its [January 2025 clarification](https://www.secp.gov.pk/media-center/press-releases/federal-investigation-agency-fia-seeks-details-of-cases-of-insider-trading-and-market-manipulation-from-securities-and-exchange-commission-of-pakistan/) said 27 criminal complaints since 2008 had been shared with FIA. Counts are not convictions.

Broker house accounts are legal market participants, not an insider proxy. [AKD Securities' PSX profile](https://dps.psx.com.pk/company/AKDSL) lists investment in securities among its businesses. [NCCPL's MTS eligibility rules](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/files/?file=244118-1.pdf) tie leveraged eligibility to liquidity and impact cost. The older “badla” story is a historical warning about leverage, not the current MTS rule. [SECP's daily market reports](https://www.secp.gov.pk/document/daily-stock-market-report-dated-january-28-2026/?wpdmdl=63540) publish MTS turnover and open interest. A rising broker-proprietary flow before results or policy news is a testable hypothesis; public aggregate flows cannot identify whose trade used non-public information.

## 3. Calendar and technical habits

Practitioners watch the [published SBP MPC calendar](https://www.sbp.org.pk/m_policy/mp-comm-semi.asp), with the next meetings on 26 October and 14 December 2026, fortnightly T-bill auctions, monthly PBS CPI and PAMA, weekly SPI, monthly fertilizer offtake and cement despatches, the June budget, and result seasons in February, April, August and October. Actual PSX board notices supersede last year's date. Dividend entitlement, book closure and ex-date must be separated. KMI and MSCI review dates come from their notices; an assumed November inclusion trade is unverified. Ramadan, Muharram and year-end window dressing remain unverified seasonal effects.

Local technical conventions include round-number KSE-100 levels, support and resistance zones, high-volume breakouts, circuit-lock sequences and sector volume leadership. [JS Global's July 2026 technical note on KSEAlert](https://ksealert.com/Research/single/355518/js-pakistan-market-technical-outlook-jul-30-2026) shows that a broker publishes chart levels. A [May 2026 retail breakout screen](https://www.reddit.com/r/FIREPakistan/comments/1stptdr/built_a_screen_that_catches_psx_breakouts_on_day/) required twice the 20-day volume average, but that is an anonymous trader's rule, not validated institutional practice. PSX's [circuit-breaker page](https://dps.psx.com.pk/circuit-breakers) shows actual stock-specific limits, and the [2025 rulebook](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/themes/psx/uploads/PSX-Regulations-November-24-2025.pdf) describes the phased move from 7.5% toward 10%. Each convention is a hypothesis. A level drawn after a move or a volume surge in an illiquid name can be selection bias.

## 4. Stocks to investigate or avoid

Prioritise company-specific PSX non-compliance notices, repeated account-filing or AGM extensions, adverse auditor opinions, weak cash conversion, persistent related-party receivables, dilutive rights issues, opaque buybacks and sponsor selling into a promotion. The [13 October 2026 PSX notices](https://dps.psx.com.pk/announcements?type=cdc) separately flag Hira Textile and Standard Worldwide for specific rule breaches; the generic risk-warning footer on every PSX company page is not such a flag. [SECP's 2024 manipulation cases](https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Press-Release-March-6-SECP-files-criminal-cases-against-individuals-involved-in-stock-market-manipulation.pdf) justify checking cancelled-order and cross-trade patterns where data permit. Small free float and repeated upper circuits increase exit risk. Penny price alone proves neither fraud nor a poor return. Forum language about “operators”, including [retail discussions](https://www.reddit.com/r/FIREPakistan/comments/1d7ihl6/a_stupid_guide_to_investing_in_the_pakistan_stock/), is an anecdote until matched to filings or enforcement.

## 5. Signals to test

Use announcement timestamps, adjusted prices and a same-period placebo in the same names. Report n, independent episodes, mean, median, win rate and worst case. Sector and market controls matter, and a result known only after close belongs to the next trading day.

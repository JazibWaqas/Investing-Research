Keep the cap as a ceiling, tighten initial satellite bets to 10–12%, and add a sector/factor cap around 35% until correlations are measured.

**Overall verdict, about 150 words.** `PICKS.md` identifies the right risks but repeatedly converts weak evidence into immediate orders. The rate curve and oil shock support a tight-regime watch, yet September inflation eased, the six-month bill yield dipped at the latest auction, and the IMF staff agreement is already public. Monday's three limits pay near Friday's closes for corrected expected returns well below the quoted bill yield. The discretionary MEBL and FFC substitutions may be good businesses, but the research has not measured whether they beat FABL and ENGROH. The event list is better as a set of conditional research questions than as deployable cash: two result dates lack notices, GHGL's ex-date removes dividend carry, NETSOL's normalised EPS remains unidentified, and EPCL's offer pays only a fraction of shares. Rebuild the initial core as diversified KMI exposure, preserve small event limits, and score every rejected order against its mechanical alternative. The forecast book can survive being wrong about one print; it cannot survive confusing a plausible story with an earned edge.

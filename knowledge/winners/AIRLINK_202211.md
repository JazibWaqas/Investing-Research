## How the move unfolded

The first 12 months were a trap. From Rs30.93 the stock lost 42% to a close of Rs18.09 on 23 Jun 2023 while EPS was weak (Mar 2023 quarter 0.42, Jun 2023 0.01). The first real sign was 17 Jul 2023 (Rs27.73, 4.4m shares). On 26 Oct 2023 a new 252-day high at Rs37.29 on 6.6m shares; 30 Oct results EPS 2.06 against 0.95; Rs71.13 on 8 Dec (2.3x the start), then -25% to Rs53.51 by 22 Dec. Rs62 to Rs75 through Apr to Jun 2024, then a straight climb: Rs106 on 25 Jul, Rs143 on 6 Sep. A pause of ten weeks at Rs127 to Rs145. The last leg, 2 Dec to 23 Dec 2024, +51% to Rs215.53, with 8.7% to 10% daily rises; the peak was 2 Jan 2025 at Rs222.89 and EPS for the Mar 2025 quarter fell to 1.35. The price then traded Rs146 to Rs180 in H1 2025 and was Rs118.54 on 16 Oct 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

First reasonable entry: 26 to 27 Oct 2023 on the new high, bought at Rs36.21 the next close; the 30 Oct results confirmed it. A 20% stop sells on 20 Dec at Rs51.49 (1.42x, about Rs41,000 net on Rs100,000); a 25% stop is stopped on 8 Apr 2025 for a later entry: from 19 Apr 2024 (Rs75.16) 2.22x (+Rs121,000), exit Rs164.38. From 13 Aug 2024 (Rs137.68) 1.21x. From 6 Dec 2024 (Rs197.85) -16% and from 20 Dec 2024 (Rs215.53) -20%. Rs100,000 was about 500 to 2,800 shares against 740,000 to 9m a day of volume, so exit was easy.

The library's start date should not have been bought: the stock lost 42% in the next seven months. A reader who waited for the EPS jump bought at Rs36.21, 1.17x the library's start price and 11 months later, and missed only the drawdown.

## The trap

The earnings jump was real but the multiple doubled, and the last move of Dec 2024 came with no news and a "no information" reply. The earnings quality is unchecked: revenue fell 19.55% in FY25 while profit rose 2.65% (ProPakistani, via search), and cash conversion on assembly working capital was not looked at. Twin: SYS 2025 to 2026 (situation 10), where a true story failed on the multiple.

## The if-then rule

If quarterly EPS prints at 2x or more the year-ago quarter, twice in a row, in a KMI name on a new 252-day high, buy at the next close and hold on a 25% stop; take half off when the trailing P/E has doubled from the entry and stop adding when the move needs a new multiple.

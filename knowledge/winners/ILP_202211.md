# Winner: Interloop (ILP), November 2022 to May 2024

Written 2026-10-18. Type: results jump, then a wave. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/ILP.csv` and `data/panel_close_adj.parquet` (bonus and dividends adjusted; raw Rs56.17 at the library start is Rs33.47 adjusted), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv` (unverified against filings), PSX documents 210173 and 221919 (scanned, read by eye), `data/index_members/`. Simulations: `tests/out/winners_results_jumps_sims.txt` (method in the ATRL case).

## Dates and regime

Library start 22 Nov 2022, but the real low was 22 May 2023 (raw Rs47.17, 16% under the library start). Policy rate 16% (Nov 2022) rising to 22% (26 Jun 2023). CPI 38% in May 2023. USD/PKR 286: Interloop sells hosiery and apparel abroad, so the rupee's fall raised its rupee earnings. KMI All Share 19,801 at the low; 34,944 at the peak (1.8x against ILP 2.8x).

## What was knowable, and when

1. 18 Oct 2022: first-quarter EPS 3.54 (library: against 1.92). 14 Feb 2023: a loss quarter, EPS minus 0.27.
2. 18 Apr 2023, 12:19: third-quarter EPS 6.84, the highest in the file (the Mar 2022 comparison is not in it). The stock rose 3.2% to Rs49.02 on 1.1m shares. Trailing EPS about Rs13.9 against Rs29.21 adjusted, a P/E near 2.
3. 9 Jun 2023: a Rs3 interim dividend and a 50% bonus (doc 210173), a lock-up of 7.5%. 3 Jul: IMF session, another lock. Dozens of director and executive trading disclosures from December 2022 (50 to the peak; the file does not classify their direction).
4. 22 Sep 2023: the first 252-day high (adjusted Rs40.13). 30 Oct: Q1 FY24 EPS 4.31.
5. 1 Dec 2023: acquisition of 64% of Top Circle Hosiery Mills, USA, completed (doc 221919), a lock-up and Rs72.

## How the move unfolded

The 18 Apr filing did nothing: the stock slid another 4% to the low on 22 May. The bonus and dividend (9 Jun) turned it, the IMF day added a second lock, and it was only 1.27x the low by 3 Jul. It paused through August and September (the first 252-day high came on 22 Sep at 1.43x, about 66% of the log gain still ahead). It accelerated on 31 Oct to 2 Nov: three locks in a row, +24%, then Rs72 and the Top Circle lock on 1 Dec (2.4x). It stood still until a lock on 23 Feb 2024, peaked at Rs84.08 raw on 24 May 2024 (2.8x of the low) and lost 18% in five weeks. Quarterly EPS after the April 2023 peak was 4.28, 4.31, 2.72, 2.95, 1.80, then 0.24 for Sep 2024, so trailing P/E went from 2 to about 5.6 at the peak. After 28 Oct 2024 (the 25% stop) the stock fell to Rs43.84 on 8 May 2025 and then ran to Rs106.43 on 31 Aug 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

- Buying the 18 Apr 2023 filing (Rs29.23 adjusted, next open): worst close 4% under cost in the first 20 sessions, plus 21% at 60. With a 25% trail, out on 28 Oct 2024 at 2.04x, plus 103% net: Rs50,000 to about Rs101,000, Rs200,000 to about Rs405,000. A 40% trail left on 9 May 2025 at plus 59%, after a 45% drawdown from the high.
- Buying the 22 Sep 2023 first high: plus 47% with a 25% trail (Rs50,000 to Rs73,700). The 31 Oct results: plus 37%.
- Joining late: after the 1 Dec 2023 acquisition lock, minus 16% (25% trail, worst close 17% under cost); after the 23 Feb 2024 lock, minus 14%.
- What kept you in: not earnings (EPS never regained 6.84) but the lock sequence and the market wave. What should have taken you out: a 25% fall from the highest close, which came on 28 Oct 2024 at Rs59.57, still 2x the entry. Here a wide stop would have been worse than a tight one.

## The trap

The "jump" was the best quarter in the file, and every later quarter was lower, so paying for growth was wrong: the price rose because the market's multiple went from 2 to 5.6 and because of two corporate actions (a bonus, an acquisition). The loss quarter of Dec 2022 shows the earnings were lumpy. Insider-trade filings in volume (50) carry no direction here. Failure twins: exporters whose Q3 FY23 peak was followed by a flat or falling stock; not measured here.

## Which questions would have caught it

A1 and A4 (policy peak and IMF trigger), A5 (rupee: an exporter's windfall), B1 (a peak quarter, not a run rate), B6 (bonus and sponsor behaviour), B10 (KMI snapshot), C1 and C4 (locks and breakouts), C9 (stop at 25%).

## The if-then rule

If an exporter files its best quarter at a P/E near 2 in a market at its low and the price does not move, wait for the dividend and bonus or the market trigger, buy the first 252-day high or the first lock, and use a 25% trail: the earnings will not keep rising, so the exit must be on price. One case in a four-case 2023 wave; untested outside it.

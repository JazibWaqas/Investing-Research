## How the move unfolded

A flat first six months (0.9x to 1.07x). The 252-day high came on 7 Nov 2023 (1.28x, Rs14.9), 1.5x on 1 Dec (Rs18.9), 2x on 31 May 2024 (Rs23.5), 3x on 29 Nov 2024 (Rs35.2), 4x on 19 Mar 2025 (Rs47.5), 5x on 1 Sep 2025 (Rs59.5), peak Rs61.05 on 30 Sep 2025. The pullbacks were between 16% and 20% (Jul to Sep 2023, Dec 2023 to Mar 2024, Jun to Aug 2024, Dec 2024 to Jan 2025, Mar to May 2025, May to Jun 2025); none passed 20%. 8 upper locks.

After the peak: 0.95x at 10 sessions, 0.86x at 20, 0.84x at 40, 0.91x at 60 (Dec 2025), 0.64x at 120 (the March 2026 Hormuz shock), 0.87x at 250.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax. Rs200,000 was under 0.1% of a day's turnover throughout.

- 8 Nov 2023 (first 252-day high, Rs15.4, 1.32x): 4.04x ahead. A 20% leash held until 2 Mar 2026 at Rs46.8, +210%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs308,000; 30% held until 9 Mar at +161%. Worst close against entry about 0%.
- 4 Dec 2023 (1.5x): +138% (20% leash). 3 Jun 2024 (2x): +102%.
- 2 Dec 2024 (3.3x, Rs35): +24% (20% leash, 2 Mar 2026) to +41% (40% leash). By then 1.62x was ahead.
- The 20% leash worked for the same reason it failed elsewhere: the stock's own pullbacks never reached 20%.

## The trap

Mistaking a quiet compounder for a lagging stock: it compounded 5.3x in 30 months without one 20% drawdown from a high. And extension: a buyer at 4x (March 2025) had 1.6x ahead and a 16% pullback in the first six weeks. Failure twins: cement names with weaker balance sheets or loss quarters (DGKC's -13 and -4 quarters, the library's lagging peers).

## Questions that would have caught it

A1 (rate direction: the cuts started June 2024, but the stock had already doubled), B3 (return on new capital: the sector was adding capacity), C4 (confirmation), C9 (a 20% leash fits a stock whose pullbacks are 16% to 20%), C10 (nine cement runs are one cycle).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a low-volatility sector name closes at a 252-day high while the sector basket does too, buy and hold on a leash just wider than its largest pullback so far (19% in Sep 2023, so 20 to 25%), and add on the 3x only if the stock still has a quarter to quarter earnings trend.

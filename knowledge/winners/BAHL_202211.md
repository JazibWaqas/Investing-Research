# Winner: BAHL, 2022 to 2025

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: price and volume panels (adjusted multiples used, because raw prices fall on dividends and bonus issues and are not comparable; the raw start was Rs53.50 against an adjusted Rs41.09), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/corporate_actions.csv`. EPS for BAHL is not in `quarterly_eps.csv`; the profit and dividend amounts are unverified. Not KMI.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 15% at the start, 22% from Jun 2023, 11% by May 2025. KMI All Share 3.19x and KSE-100 4.0x from start to peak; bank peers 3.3x (ABL) to 8.5x (NBP) in the same window. BAHL was in the middle of the sector wave, at 5.3x, and was the steadiest.

## What was knowable and when

Almost nothing was dated. The filings in the run are results (nine-month, annual, quarterly), a stream of interim-dividend notices (BAHL issued interim-dividend notices three times a year, for example 29 Apr, 12 Aug and 24 Oct 2024; amounts unverified), corporate-briefing sessions (7 Nov 2022, 22 Nov 2023, 22 Nov 2024, 14 Nov 2025), "disclosure of material information" on 22 Sep 2023, 22 and 26 Mar 2024, 8 Aug 2024 and 6 May 2025 whose text I could not retrieve, and an application on 19 Apr 2024 for a further issue of shares other than by rights. No PSX price query, no company reply, no sponsor filing. The first sign was price: a close above the previous 252-day high on 25 Jul 2023 at 1.15x the start, after a range of 0.87 to 1.05 times the start.

## How the move unfolded (adjusted multiples of the start)

Eight flat months, range 0.87 to 1.05; a dip to 0.87 in Jun 2023 (about -14% from the Feb level). Then 1.21 on 31 Jul 2023, a pullback to 1.07 in Sep, 2.0 by 7 Dec 2023, 2.8 on 8 Jul 2024, -20% to 2.32 on 5 Sep 2024, 3.34 on 29 Nov 2024 (a +9.6% day on 22 Nov, 4.1m shares), a plateau at 3.4 to 3.5 from Feb to May 2025, then 4.3 in Aug, 5.28 on 21 Oct 2025. Two pullbacks of 15% or more in 35 months; the worst was 20.6%. After the peak: -16% in 20 sessions and -31% by March 2026, a recovery to 0.79 of the peak in April, 0.70 on 16 Oct 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Entry at the 25 Jul 2023 new high (bought next close, Rs53.22 raw): at that point 4.6x was still ahead, 92% of the log gain. With a 20% trailing stop the exit was 16 Feb 2026 at Rs173.28: 3.69x, about Rs267,000 net on Rs100,000 (1,879 shares); a 25% stop sold on 2 Mar 2026 for 3.24x; a 30% stop on 9 Mar 3.17x. Entry on 26 Apr 2024 (2.25x still ahead) made 1.6x, and on 22 Nov 2024 1.23x. By contrast a 20% stop on the library's own start day was hit on 19 Jun 2023 at 0.86x and the reader then missed 6x: with a slow starter the signal is the new high, not the low. Exit liquidity was never a problem (about 0.4% of a day's volume).

Late joining paid until mid-2025. The 20% stop kept about 80% of the multiple from the entry (3.69x against 4.62x at the peak): unusually clean for this set, because the drawdowns were small.

## The trap

Boredom. There was no event to look for, and the first year looks like a range. The opposite trap: assuming the 20% stop a reader uses on thin names suits a bank; a bank with 20% worst drawdown is stopped only by a real reversal.

## Which questions in QUESTIONS.md would have caught it

A1 (a rate regime that raises bank earnings), B13 (growing, not stable), B3 (earns on new capital), C4 (confirming trend), B11 (priced in), C9.

## The if-then rule

If a liquid business with dividends and no news prints a new 252-day high after a flat year, and its sector is rising on a rate or earnings regime, then buy at the next close and hold on a 20% to 25% stop from the running high; do not demand a story. Check the KMI-eligible sector peers first, since BAHL itself is not buyable.

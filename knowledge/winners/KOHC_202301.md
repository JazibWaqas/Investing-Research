# Winner KOHC, from January 2023: buy-backs, a flat year, and a stop that cost the whole move

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, price panels (split-adjusted, dividends excluded), `data/announcements.parquet` (titles only; the PDFs were not opened), `data/macro/`. Prices before the 5-for-1 split credited 25 Aug 2025 are quoted in old rupees.

## Dates and regime

Library start 17 Jan 2023 (Rs121.52), library peak 3 Oct 2025 (4.5x, 668 sessions), price high 23 Dec 2025 (4.4x of the 22 Feb 2023 close), 16 Oct 2026 at 3.0x of that close. Same regime as LUCK: policy rate 16% going to 22%, cut cycle from 10 Jun 2024, KMI All Share up 264% to the peak. Median traded value Rs4.8m at the start (tier L), Rs5 to 70m on busy days. Maximum drawdown inside the run 24%.

## What was knowable, and when

- 23 Jan 2023: material information. 21 Feb 2023: EOGM resolutions. 22 Feb 2023: public announcement of a buy-back. 13 Apr to 3 Aug 2023: daily purchase filings; 3 Aug "completion of purchase period of buy-back of 5,000,000 shares"; 11 Aug cancellation; 16 Aug final report.
- 16 Oct 2024: public announcement of a second buy-back. 18 Feb and 9 to 11 Apr 2025: purchase filings; 14 Apr "completion of purchase period of buy-back of 12,000,000 shares"; 22 Apr final report.
- 7 Aug 2025: EOGM on the share split; 25 Aug the five-for-one credit.

Prices paid and any size beyond the share counts in the titles: unverified.

## How the move unfolded

Rs121.52 on 17 Jan, Rs137.73 on 22 Feb (buy-back announced), a first 252-high at Rs168.24 on 6 Jun 2023 (94 sessions in; 79% of the log gain ahead), a high of Rs189.8 on 2 Aug, then a 20% shakeout to Rs151.8 on 31 Aug. It recovered to Rs241.77 on 8 Jan 2024 and then went flat: Rs211 to 250 from February to August 2024, eight months of nothing while the second buy-back had not yet been announced. The next leg was explosive: Rs303 on 7 Oct 2024, Rs411 on 28 Oct (+35% in three weeks, 16 times normal volume), straddling the second buy-back announcement of 16 Oct. Then another pause: a Rs446 high in late 2024, a 24% fall to Rs338 on 8 May 2025, and the final leg to the old-rupee equivalent of Rs612 (Rs122.31 post-split) on 23 Dec 2025. Thirteen cement peers made 2.9x to 9.7x (median 4.6x) from 17 Jan 2023 to 22 Dec 2025; KOHC's 4.6x is the median.

Reasonable recognition points: 22 Feb 2023 (buy-back), 6 Jun 2023 (252-high), 16 Aug 2023 (first programme finished, price back at Rs172), 16 Oct 2024 (second programme).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Entries and what a stop did to them (price-return, Rs100,000, no dividends or costs):

| entry | price | 20% stop | 30% stop | held to 16 Oct 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 22 Feb 2023 | 137.73 | Rs110,000 (31 Aug 2023) | Rs304,000 (6 Mar 2026) | Rs302,000 |
| 16 Aug 2023 | 172.46 | Rs202,000 (30 Apr 2025) | Rs243,000 | Rs242,000 |
| 16 Oct 2024 | 305.44 | Rs114,000 | Rs137,000 | Rs136,000 |
| 28 Oct 2024 | 411.09 | Rs85,000 | Rs102,000 | Rs101,000 |

The library's stop from the first 252-high (Rs168.24, 6 Jun 2023) at 20% exited at 0.90x. So the first reasonable entry was fine, and what kept you in was the stop width: the run had five pullbacks of 16 to 24% (August 2023 was exactly 20.0%), and only a 25% to 30% stop rode them. The reasonable exit was the 30% trailing stop in March 2026 (3.0x of the February 2023 entry). Joining late paid until about the second programme: an October 2024 buyer, 1.75 years after the start, made 1.36x; the buyer after the 35% October jump made nothing.

## The trap

Two. First, the label: buy-backs here were frequent and small (5m and 12m shares), they did not prevent five pullbacks of 16 to 24% or a flat first half of 2024, and the name merely matched its peers. Second, the stop: on an L-tier name that swings 20% inside a bull run, a tight stop sells the middle of a 4x. The failure twin of this type is MLCF, where the buy-back preceded an acquirer top.

## Questions that would have caught it

A1 and A4 (a rate peak and cut cycle behind the sector), C1 and C2 (type: buy-back, no base rate), C4 (the 252-high was confirmation), C9 (exit: stop width is the whole decision), D5 (a placebo: the sector median did the same), B9 (tier L, size for the exit).

## The if-then rule

If a mid-cap KMI name in a rate-cut sector wave runs buy-back programmes, hold it as a sector position with a 30% trailing stop (not 20%) and add on the second programme only while price is above the first-programme high; never read a buy-back as protection against a 25% pullback.

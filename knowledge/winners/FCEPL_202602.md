## How the move unfolded

The February results gave nothing: the stock slid to Rs65.46 in the shock (25% under the 18 Feb open on the adjusted close, within 20 sessions). A bounce to Rs78 by April left it where it began. The 24 Apr filing ended that: two limit days in a row (Rs85.87, Rs94.46), a pause at Rs92 to 99, then a climb on rising volume to Rs115.05 on 11 May. It fell 10% the next day, drifted between Rs103 and Rs128 until August, jumped 10% on 24 Aug and peaked at Rs156.44 on 1 Sep (2.5x the low, 2.0x the library start). It fell 17% to Rs129.85 by 12 Oct. At 24 Apr 82% of the move was still ahead in price terms (66% of the log gain); at the 11 May high 36% (34% of the log gain); at the 21 Aug filing 21%.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

- The February filing (Rs83.35): worst close 25% under cost; a 20% trail sold on 10 Mar at minus 22%; 25% and 30% trails held and showed plus 59% at 16 Oct. This is the entry that failed within 20 sessions.
- The 24 Apr filing, first fill after the two limit days: Rs94.46 on 27 Apr (limit price, fill uncertain) or Rs96.25 on 28 Apr: plus 41% and plus 38% at 16 Oct with a worst close of 2 to 4% under cost and a 17% drawdown from the high. Rs50,000 became about Rs70,000 (open). Not selling is the reasonable choice: 20% to 30% trails were not hit.
- The 11 May first 252-day high (Rs115.79 after a minus 9.7% day): plus 15%, a 14% drawdown. The 21 Aug results (Rs129.34): plus 3%.
- What kept you in: the second filing (3.33 against 0.30) re-confirmed the cause; the drawdowns were 10 to 17%, inside any trail from 20%. The exit would be a close 20% under the highest close (Rs125), or weak Q3 results; the executive turnover is the live caution.

## The trap

The February filing looked the same on the tape as the April one (a good quarter, small positive move) and lost 25% to the war. The Q4 EPS jump was a low base; only the April quarter combined operating profit growth and a financing-cost cut. A reader who bought the 11 May high (plus 10% on 7.8m shares) bought a day before a minus 9.7% day. Failure twins: 21% of 2026 EPS-jump events fell 15% or more within 20 sessions (`tests/out/winners_results_jumps_twins.txt`).

## The if-then rule

If a liquid KMI name files a quarter in which operating profit rises 40% or more and finance cost falls, and the stock closes up 10% on the filing after a shock low, join within two or three sessions even at plus 20%, with a 25% trail, and add nothing at the first 252-day high. Do not buy a low-base quarter's filing on its own. One case; the only one of the eight where the filing itself shows the cause.

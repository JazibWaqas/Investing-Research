1. 30 Apr 2025, 15:12: Q3 FY25 EPS Rs0.28 against minus 0.09, net income Rs96m against a Rs30m loss, revenue Rs2.5bn against Rs2.7bn (EPS file). The stock fell 3.0% to Rs6.82. Trailing EPS was negative, so no P/E.

4. 17 Sep onwards: 62m shares on 17 Sep, then 17m to 108m a day through October. First 252-day high on 16 Oct (plus 10%, Rs11.34).
5. 29 to 30 Oct: PSX asked about the unusual movement; Telecard replied it was "not aware of any matter" that explained it (doc 264339).

## How the move unfolded

The filing did nothing, and the shock took the stock to a new low in six sessions. The rebound was fast (Rs5.60 to Rs8.22 by 26 May, 1.47x the low), followed by a 15% pullback to Rs6.97 on 23 Jun. It rose slowly through July and August to Rs8.95 on 17 Sep, accelerated on volume in September (about 46% of the log gain still ahead at 17 Sep), and exploded on 9 to 23 Oct: plus 11% on 9 Oct (the reaction to a loss quarter), plus 10% on 16 Oct, volume of 100m shares, Rs13.26 on 23 Oct (2.4x the low). It fell 18% in a week, gained 10% on 17 Nov, made a last high of Rs13.06 on 7 Jan 2026 and Rs7.11 on 16 Oct 2026.

## The trap

The label made it look like a results winner. It was not: EPS of Rs0.28 is a few paise of profit, the year ended with a loss quarter, and the October stock reacted to a loss by locking up. The cause was never disclosed. QUESTIONS B9 and C7 apply: operator-type volume without news has a measured 60-day median of minus 8.5% (win rate 35%, n = 675, `tests/seed_audit.md`). Here the run came with 100m-share days, so a late buyer in the last week (16 to 23 Oct) was buying the end. Failure twins: the entries from 16 Oct lost money at the trails used.

# Winner case: United Bank Limited, January 2023 to February 2026

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/panel_close_adj.parquet` (a 2-for-1 sub-division on 23 Jun 2025 is adjusted; raw Rs94.57 at the start is Rs47.29 on today's shares), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/macro/`, situations 04 and 06; ProPakistani 17 Jan 2023, Profit and Nukta on Silkbank (search snippets, not opened in full). Simulations as in `MEBL_202212.md`.

## Dates and regime

Start 17 Jan 2023: the KSE-100 fell 3.5% to 38,342 that day on a delay in the IMF review, the lowest in 30 months (ProPakistani). Policy rate 17% on 23 Jan, 22% from 26 Jun, held until the first cut on 10 Jun 2024; 1,000bp of cuts to 12% by 27 Jan 2025 and 10.5% by 15 Dec 2025. Staff-level agreement on a $3bn stand-by on 29 or 30 Jun 2023; KSE-100 +5.9% on 3 Jul (situation 04). The KMI All Share rose 3.85x from the start to the UBL peak. UBL is a conventional bank: never eligible.

## What was knowable and when

Filings: results and dividends on schedule; a new President and CEO appointed on 3 Apr 2023; "Material Information" on 1 Jun and 12 Sep 2023, 1 and 7 Nov and 3 and 26 Dec 2024, 11 Mar and 23 May 2025 (content not opened). Silkbank shareholders approved amalgamation into UBL on 26 Dec 2024, and the SBP sanctioned it effective 11 Mar 2025; reports disagree on the swap ratio. Sub-division of shares in June 2025. One report puts 2025 profit at Rs130bn against Rs75.8bn in 2024; another gives different EPS for 2024, so earnings are unverified here. The quarterly EPS file has no UBL rows.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

The first reasonable entry was the 6 Jul 2023 close above the 252-day high, three sessions after the agreement. Rs100,000 became Rs634,000 on a 20% closing stop (exit 3 Mar 2026), Rs578,000 on 25%, Rs522,000 on 30% and Rs624,000 on 40% (still held). The stops fired in March 2026, after a fall of 20 to 30% from the peak. Entry on 8 Dec 2023 at 2.0x returned 3.6x to 4.4x; on 23 Sep 2024 at 3.05x, 2.4x to 2.9x; on 4 Apr 2025 at 5.0x, 1.4x to 1.7x. Joining late kept paying until the last year. Liquidity never limited Rs50,000 to Rs200,000.

## The trap

Buying the 17 Jan 2023 low: five months of nothing, a 16% fall, and a 9% limit-down day on 10 May 2023 (the arrest, situation 04). The second trap is counting this as one of four winners: UBL, MEBL, LUCK and GHNI all start on the same day, so the sample is one regime episode (L16). The twin that failed to follow is MCB or ABL: 3.2x to 3.8x, about the index.

## Questions that would have caught it

A4 (IMF status: the staff-level agreement was the trigger), A1 (real rate and cut room), A6 (a cheap or rerated market: 2022 to 2025 flatters every long test), A10, B10 (not KMI: use MEBL), C4, C9, C10.

## If-then rule

If the index jumps on an IMF trigger and the strongest rate-earning large cap closes above its 252-day high within a week, buy it and hold on a 25% closing stop until the index itself falls 20%; if it is not on the KMI list, buy the Islamic equivalent.

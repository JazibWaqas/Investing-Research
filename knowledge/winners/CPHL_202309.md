# Winner: Citi Pharma, September 2023 to February 2025 (5.5x)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/CPHL.csv`, `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`, `data/macro/`. News: web search found no source for the Dec 2024 to Jan 2025 leg (summary pages gave the all-time high of Rs109.30 on 11 Feb 2025, and the company's profile as Pakistan's largest API maker, paracetamol sold to GSK). The cause is unverified. A KMI member.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 22% at the start, 12% at the peak. KMI All Share +122% over the run. The library's five-name pharma basket rose 193%, so CPHL (5.5x) moved with its sector (2.9x) and the market (2.2x), only more. Cuts began on 10 Jun 2024; pharma was a named leader of the first-cut period (situation 05).

## How the move unfolded

Fourteen months of 1.1x to 1.8x. 1.25x on 13 Oct 2023 and a 25% fall to February 2024, then the first 252-day high on 7 May 2024 (Rs29.4, 1.5x), 2x on 9 Oct (Rs40.2). Then nine weeks: 3x on 10 Dec 2024 (Rs59.8), 4x on 20 Jan 2025 (Rs84.8), 5x on 31 Jan (Rs98.4), peak close Rs106.56 on 11 Feb 2025. Daily gains were 9% to 10%; there were 8 upper locks in all, none for several days in a row.

After the peak: 0.94x at 10 sessions, 0.85x at 20, 0.95x at 40, 0.73x at 60 (13 May 2025, after the 8 May market-wide fall), 0.79x at 120.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 8 May 2024 (first 252-day high, Rs31.6, 1.63x): 3.4x ahead. A 20% leash sold on 28 Apr 2025 at Rs84.2, +167%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs265,000, with a worst close of -12% against entry; a 30% leash on 7 May 2025 at +136%.
- 10 Oct 2024 (2x): +90% (20% leash). 11 Dec 2024 (3.3x, Rs64.5): +31% (20% leash), about Rs130,000; 1.65x was still ahead.
- The exit came 76 days after the peak (28 Apr 2025), at 0.79 of its price, because the stock held up after a 20% dip in Feb to Mar.
- A Rs200,000 order was 2% of a day's turnover at the start and 0.04% in January 2025.

## The trap

Paying for a re-rating on EPS that did not move: trailing four-quarter EPS was Rs2.66 against Rs19.4 (7x) at the start and Rs3.63 against Rs106.6 (29x) at the peak, and nothing in the filings said why. The two possible readings, a sector rotation after the cuts and an unnamed buyer, cannot be separated with this data. Failure twin: a 3x entry in December 2024, where the next 11 weeks gave 1.65x ahead on paper but one sharp shakeout.

## Questions that would have caught it

B1 (one-offs stripped: Dec 2023's 1.64 was the top of the range), B11 (what is priced in: the multiple), C4 (confirmation from May 2024), C9 (the leash), C5 (do not pay for a story that looks like a doubler).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a sector wave (pharma after the first cut) lifts a name that has flat EPS, buy the first 252-day high only as a starter and exit on the first 20% close below the high; the late leg (the last 3x) had no earnings behind it, so size it at half and take the 3x as the signal to tighten, not to add.

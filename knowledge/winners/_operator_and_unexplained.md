# Operator and unexplained winners: what the eight largest teach

Written 2026-10-18 from the eight case files beside this one (QUICE, CRTM, PSMC, PIAHCLA, AIRLINK, NBP, BOP, BAHL), the eight largest episodes labelled "operator or no visible cause" in `data/winners_library.csv` plus QUICE and CRTM from the thin list. SSGC 2024 is in the library as a policy or sector wave, not here; see `knowledge/situations/09_2024_ssgc_volume_run.md`. Hindsight picks, one path each, stops tested after the fact.

## What the label hid

Only QUICE and CRTM were unexplained by their companies, and each later showed a named buyer: a brokerage (14.57m shares, disclosed 6 Jan 2026) and a sponsor-family company (9.2m shares, disclosed 26 Mar 2026), both after the price had at least tripled. The other six had causes the classifier missed: a majority-holder delisting (PSMC, filed as "other"), a privatisation (PIAHCLA), an EPS jump after the start window (AIRLINK), and a bank rate-and-earnings wave with no peer basket in the sector map (NBP, BOP, BAHL). Five of the eight are not KMI. I did not check the other 51 episodes of the type.

## Is there a shared signature?

For the thin pair, yes: a flat history, a first day of 10x to 40x volume, then 8 to 13 closes at the +10% limit while the sector or the index was flat or falling, a PSX query answered with "no information" within two weeks, and a buyer named only after a 10% or "substantial" threshold. Both had an earlier false start in the same stock (QUICE Aug 2024, -39%; CRTM Jun 2025, seven months flat). For the other six there is no shared signature: one rule per case.

## Exits that kept the move

A price fixed from outside (PSMC) is the cleanest: sell into it. A 20% stop kept about three quarters of the two thin runs. Wide stops (25% to 30%) were needed for sector waves with 28% to 39% pullbacks (NBP, BOP, PIAHCLA). Selling before the decisive event beat selling after it: PIAHCLA peaked two weeks before the Rs135bn bid and fell 39% in 13 sessions.

## The failures

Entries after the disclosure or on the spike: QUICE 15 Jan (-21%), PSMC 10 Jan (-14%), PIAHCLA 15 Dec 2023 (-26%) and 19 Mar 2024 (-19%), AIRLINK Dec 2024 (-16%, -20%), BOP 30 Oct 2025 (-14%), CRTM 9 Apr 2026 (0%). A stop too tight for a slow starter: NBP 25% (1.0x), BAHL 20% from the library start (0.86x, then 6x missed). Limit-up days may not fill, and CRTM traded about 2,600 shares a day in Aug to Oct 2026.

## The rules, one per family

Ignition on a thin KMI name: small position at the first confirmed volume week, 20% stop, no adding after the buyer is named. Process (delisting, privatisation): buy the first unpriced step and sell into the decisive one. Trend (banks, AIRLINK, BAHL): new 252-day high, wide stop, check the KMI equivalent. Base rate warning: for QUICE and CRTM the engine's analogues had medians near zero, so these were rare right-tail outcomes, not a pattern to chase.

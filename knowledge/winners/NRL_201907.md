# Winner: National Refinery, July 2019 to March 2021 (6.9x)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/NRL.csv` (no corporate action in the run: raw equals adjusted), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/macro/`. News (web search 18 Oct 2026, snippets): Mettis Global, "NRL tells PSX it is not aware of any reason that is causing its share price to increase" (14 Dec 2020). I found no source for what moved the stock after December 2020: unverified.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 13.25% at the start, 7.0% from 25 Jun 2020. KMI All Share +53% over the run (15,047 to 23,030). A KMI member. The library's two-peer refinery basket is too small to read; refiners were trading on a long-awaited refining policy, which had not yet been approved (a 2022 PACRA note still calls it "imperative"). Oil was recovering from the Covid lows.

## What was knowable, and when

- 24 Dec 2019, 26 Mar, 10 Apr and 23 Apr 2020: material information filings (bare titles). 25 Sep 2020: another.
- 25 Nov 2020: a material information filing, the day after a newspaper article saying Mari Petroleum would buy up to 20% of National Refinery in a Balochistan joint venture. NRL's filing said the venture involved National Resource (Pvt) Ltd, not NRL, and asked for a correction (Mettis).
- Price Rs161.36 on 25 Nov, Rs307.68 on 14 Dec: +90.7% in 13 sessions. 14 Dec 2020: "Clarification in response to SECP letter regarding trading price", and NRL told the PSX it knew of no undisclosed information.
- 1 Jan 2021: material information. No results-based explanation: `quarterly_eps.csv` has no rows for NRL.

## How the move unfolded

Slowly at first. 1.5x on 2 Dec 2019 (Rs142), a 55% fall in the Covid crash, then 2x on 3 Aug 2020 (Rs186, the first 252-day high, turnover Rs97m a day against Rs15m). A 30% fall from 9 Sep to 23 Nov 2020, then the explosion: 3x on 10 Dec (Rs286), 4x on 4 Jan 2021 (Rs373), 5x on 3 Feb (Rs464), peak Rs641.45 on 3 Mar 2021. Turnover Rs720m a day in December, Rs1.2bn in February. 48 upper-lock closes in all.

After the peak: 0.92x at 10 sessions, 0.87x at 60, 0.65x at 120 (Sep 2021), 0.33x at 250 sessions.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax. Rs100,000 base.

- 3 Aug 2020 (first 252-day high, Rs186): 3.4x still ahead. A 20% leash sold on 30 Sep at -7%; a 30% leash was never hit until 26 Jul 2021 at Rs448, +140%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs239,000, after a worst close of -16% against entry. A 40% leash sold on 20 Sep 2021, +105%.
- 10 Dec 2020 (3x, Rs286, after the +90%): 2.08x ahead; a 30% leash +57%, about Rs156,000. This is a fair entry because the price kept being confirmed, but at 3x it is a bet on the unexplained.
- 1.5x on 2 Dec 2019: -14% to -38% in the crash.
- The 30% leash sold at 0.70 of the peak. The reasonable exit was a stop: nothing in the data says to sell the top in March 2021.

## The trap

Joining because of a rumour the company denied. The 90% move in 13 sessions had no filed cause; the second half of the run (4x to 7x) happened with no explanation either. A reader who needed a cause would have sat out the whole thing and been right on the fundamentals. Failure twins: other 2020 to 2021 stocks with a denial and a +90% move (the library's thin list has many that fell 50%+ within a year).

## Questions that would have caught it

C7 (volume with locks: demand or an operator?), C3 (who decides: the refining policy, a buyer that denied it), C1 (type: unexplained), C9 (the loss if the story is false), B9 (float: Rs720m a day made exit easy).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If an index-member refiner makes a 252-day high on 6x turnover, size it as an unexplained mover (a quarter of a normal position) and hold on a 30% leash; add only if the price confirms after a denial, and expect the exit to come from the stop, since no cause will ever arrive.

# Winner: Ghandhara Automobiles, December 2022 to September 2025 (15.4x)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/GAL.csv`, `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`, `data/macro/`. News (web search, snippets): Profit, "Gandhara Automobile stock price goes up by 53% in a month" (Dec 2024 to Jan 2025); Profit and PakWheels on the March 2025 quarter and FY25. A KMI member.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 16% at the start, 22% in 2023, 11% at the peak. KMI All Share +229%. The library's six-name autos basket rose 122%. Imports were restricted for much of this period, and vehicle supply was tight. See also SAZEW_202210 and GHNI_202301 for the sector twins.

## What was knowable, and when

- Quarterly basic EPS: Dec 2022 0.56, Mar 2023 5.50, Jun 2023 -0.55, Sep 2023 -1.61, Dec 2023 0.09, Mar 2024 2.84, Jun 2024 5.10, Sep 2024 10.55, Dec 2024 8.23, Mar 2025 21.12, Jun 2025 31.97, Sep 2025 29.33. The single March 2023 quarter did not hold; from March 2024 four quarters in a row rose.
- 7 Dec 2023, 24 Apr, 7 Jun and 7 Aug 2024: the exchange asked about unusual movement. 13 Jun and 26 Jul 2023 and 22 Jan 2025: material information.
- 19 Dec 2024 to mid-Jan 2025: the stock rose from Rs260 to a record Rs398 on the launch of the JAC T9 Hunter pickup; orders were paused on 15 Jan 2025 (Profit). March 2025 quarter profit +303% to Rs1.64bn; FY25 profit Rs4.1bn against Rs365m a year earlier (AKD via Profit; the PSX company page lists FY24 as a loss, so one of the two figures is wrong: unverified).
- Associates: a 17% stake in Ghandhara Industries (Isuzu trucks).

## How the move unfolded

Seven months of drift at 0.8x to 1.1x. Turnover jumped from Rs4m to Rs30m (October 2023), then price: 1.25x on 13 Nov, 2x on 27 Nov (Rs81), a 29% pullback in December, 3x on 19 Apr 2024 (Rs121), 5x on 11 Jul (Rs214), 7x on 29 Jul (Rs299) on Rs957m a day. Then a 53% crash from 7 Aug to 14 Oct 2024 (the 16 Aug and 26 Nov days were -10% and -9%), a recovery by 14 Jan 2025, 10x on 15 Jan (Rs406, Rs1.9bn a day), a 29% shakeout to 23 Jun 2025, and the peak close of Rs621.24 on 22 Sep 2025. 48 upper locks.

After the peak: 0.95x, 0.93x, 0.92x, 0.90x at 10, 20, 40 and 60 sessions; 0.64x at 120 (Mar 2026), 0.81x on 16 Oct 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 17 Nov 2023 (first 252-day high, Rs58.4, 1.45x): 10.6x ahead. A 20% leash sold on 20 Dec 2023 at +4%; a 30% leash on 26 Aug 2024 at Rs244, +318%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs416,000; a 40% leash on 2 Oct 2024 at +260%.
- 28 Nov 2023 (2x): +212% to the same exit. 22 Apr 2024 (3.2x): +90% to +123%.
- Re-entering on the reclaim of the old high on 14 Jan 2025 (about Rs400) and holding to the peak was another +55% by the close of 22 Sep 2025; a reader stopped out in August 2024 needed that second entry.
- Rs200,000 was 4% of a day's turnover at the start but under 0.1% by 2024.

## The trap

Taking the single good quarter of March 2023 (EPS 5.5) as the start: it was followed by three weak quarters, and the stock moved 0.8x. The real turn was from March 2024. And the 53% crash of Aug to Oct 2024 inside the run.

## Questions that would have caught it

B1 (one-offs stripped: one quarter, then three weak), B2 (sector levers: import limits, new models), B13 (growing, not stable), C4 (confirmation after the November 2023 turnover), C9 (the leash that survives a 53% crash is not available; the cheaper answer is to re-enter).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If quarterly EPS rises four quarters in a row from a low base in a supply-constrained sector, buy the 252-day high and hold on a 30% leash; if stopped out in a crash, re-enter when the old high is reclaimed, which here cost nothing in expected return, since the second entry went from Rs400 to Rs621.

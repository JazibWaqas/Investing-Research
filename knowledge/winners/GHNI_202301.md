# Winner case: Ghandhara Industries, January 2023 to October 2025

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/panel_close_adj.parquet`, `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`, `data/macro/`; Profit on GHNI's Sep 2025 quarter and its parent's FY25 (search snippets). Simulations: entry at the signal close, exit at the next close after a closing-price trailing stop, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

## Dates and regime

Start 17 Jan 2023 (Rs90.04), the day the KSE-100 closed at 38,342. Same regime as UBL and MEBL: staff-level agreement 29 or 30 Jun, +5.9% on 3 Jul 2023; first cut 10 Jun 2024. The KMI All Share rose 3.5x to the GHNI peak; the median auto peer rose 2.7x. GHNI is a KMI All Share member.

## What was knowable and when

The company lost money for two years: quarterly EPS in the file was -10.82 (Jun 2022), 0.60 (Dec 2022), -1.92 (Jun 2023) and -3.86 (Dec 2023). Then 9.52 (Mar 2024, filed 25 Apr 2024), 11.22, 14.99 (Sep 2024), 15.43, and 38.38 (Mar 2025, filed 21 Apr 2025). The file shows revenue of exactly Rs4,801m for both Mar 2023 and Mar 2024, which looks like a data error, so the Mar 2024 quarter is unverified. Profit confirms the Sep 2025 quarter: profit Rs1.58bn, up 147%, sales up 97% on "very strong market demand", finance cost down 70.7%; industry commercial-vehicle sales in FY25 were 6,682 units against 3,257. Filings: material information on 31 May and 21 Jul 2023, 3 and 7 May 2024; PSX "unusual movement" notices on 21 Aug 2024 and 6 Jan 2025 (content not opened).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

The first reasonable entry was not the 252-high of Oct 2023 on a loss-making company. It was 30 Apr 2024, the first close after a positive quarter: Rs100,000 became Rs147,000 on a 20% stop (3 Sep 2024), Rs130,000 on 30% and Rs266,000 on 40% (31 Mar 2026). Entry on 20 Oct 2023 gave 1.10x, 0.96x and 0.87x on 20%, 25% and 30% stops and 4.20x on 40%. A 25% stop with re-entry above the old high gave 2.27x from 20 Oct 2023, out and back in on 13 Feb, 26 Apr, 11 Sep and 9 Dec 2024. Entry at 5.25x (9 Dec 2024) returned 1.3x to 1.5x. Joining late left 1.9x ahead at 5x, but a stop converted little of it.

## The trap

The July 2023 ignition: value from Rs3m to Rs67m a day on a company still losing money, then -27% in four weeks. The larger trap is the stop: the 34% drawdown of Dec 2023 to Feb 2024 sat between the 252-high and the first profit quarter, and every one of the six pullbacks ended in a higher high. The laggards are INDU and HCAR at 2.3x and MTL at 1.9x over the same window, against GHNI at 9.9x; the sector tide gave 2x to 2.7x and the earnings swing the rest.

## Questions that would have caught it

B1 (earnings power: loss-making in 2023, so the first test was the quarter), B13 (growing, not merely stable), C5 (a start-of-move feature does not pick the winner), C4, C7 (38 upper locks), C9 (an exit sized to a 34% pullback), B9, D2 (the EPS file is not clean).

## If-then rule

If a sector wave lifts a small-cap whose quarterly EPS has just turned positive, buy the first close after the result and size for a 35% drawdown; use a 40% stop, or a 25% stop with re-entry on a close above the old high, because four pullbacks of 27% to 34% would have fired a 20% to 30% stop before each new high.

# Deals, buy-backs and sponsors: what eight winners say (18 Oct 2026)

Cases: LUCK_202301, KOHC_202301, MLCF_202404, PIOC_202212, TPLT_202502, ASL_202603, CLOV_201704, KEL_202206. Selection: the largest runs the library tags as buy-back, sponsor or deal that were tradable (tier H, M or L), plus TPLT and ASL as asked, plus the two that reversed (PIOC, KEL). Thin-tier tags (SPEL, KTML, IMSL, NCML, LSECL, CASH) were skipped as untradable at these sizes. Eight cases, no base rate.

**There is no shared rule, and the filing was rarely the signal.** In five of the eight, price and volume moved before the disclosure: ASL the day before the sponsor's letter, TPLT two sessions before the takeover filing, KEL three sessions before the extension notice, CLOV on 13 times volume before the sale agreement, MLCF into its own offer document. Only PIOC priced the news on the day. In LUCK and KOHC the label explained nothing: thirteen cement peers with no such buy-back made a median 4.6x against LUCK's 6.5x and KOHC's 4.6x. The tide did the work.

**Early or late.** The reads that worked were price confirmations: the first 252-high (LUCK 4.8x still ahead, MLCF 3.0x), the first volume-and-lock day (TPLT 3.6x), the disclosure-day close (ASL 1.63x). Late entries still paid in LUCK (every date to 2024), KOHC (to August 2023), TPLT's second leg and CLOV in February 2018. They did not pay after the offer in PIOC, in MLCF after mid-2025, on TPLT's 7 Jan lock day, or at any point in KEL.

**Exits that kept the move.** In tide cases, a 25% to 30% trailing stop: LUCK's 20% stop kept 80% of the peak multiple but sat 0.2 points from firing in the run; KOHC's 20% stop sold a 4.4x at 1.10x, its 30% stop kept 3.04x. In event cases, the event is the exit: the offer window (PIOC), the offer document (MLCF), the first minus 7% day after a ten-times-volume spike (KEL), the last sponsor block (ASL).

**Failures.** PIOC fell 49% from its top after the offer; MLCF, the acquirer, 44% after topping three days before its offer document; KEL gave 0.93x on a chain with no price; CLOV's disclosure-day buyer lost 21%; TPLT's first leg lost 51%.

**One rule per case** (each in its file): buy-back at a rate peak is a tide trade; widen stops to 30% on L-tier tide names; sell an acquirer into its offer document; price the blended fill before buying a target; size thin names small and treat a KMI deletion as the exit; a sponsor that keeps disclosing is a catalyst until it stops; do not buy a control-sale filing after a pre-news run; ignore a takeover with no price.

**What generalises.** Compare the name with its peers before calling a corporate action the cause, and write the exit (a date, a tender, a stop width) before the entry.

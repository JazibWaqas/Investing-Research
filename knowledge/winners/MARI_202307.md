# Winner: Mari Petroleum, July 2023 to December 2024

Written 2026-10-18. Type: E&P leader with a discovery, a bonus and a blow-off finish. Sources: `data/prices_raw/MARI.csv` (the 800% bonus is adjusted: raw 1,530.81 at the start is 158.22 on today's shares), `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/macro/`, Nukta (24 Dec 2024, fetched) and other snippets via search (asserted). Multiples are total return; price only agrees at the peak.

## Dates and regime

- 19 Jul 2023: policy rate 22%, CPI 28.3%, 3-month bill 22.8%, USD/PKR 283, KSE 45,095. IMF Stand-By Board 12 Jul (case 04).
- 16 Dec 2024: rate 13% (cut that day, from 22% in six months, case 05), CPI 4.1%, bill 12%, USD/PKR 278, KSE 116,169; the index had passed 100,000 on 28 Nov.

## What was knowable, and when

1. 16 Nov 2023: first 252-session high at 1.12x. 11 Dec 2023: a Material Information filing (doc 222349, unread); a search report ties a Shewa-2 gas discovery disclosure in Waziristan to that date.
2. 8 Aug 2024 (14:06): results for the year to June 2024 with an 800% bonus (Nukta, asserted). In the repo's series the June quarter EPS was 21.37 against 13.19 a year earlier (+62%, one share base, unverified). Two 10% limit closes followed, on 8 and 9 Aug.
3. Nukta, 24 Dec 2024: up 198% in 2024 and about 40% in December alone; a high of Rs899 after +95% in 16 days; then a correction of over 20%. FY24 profit +38% against 17% at PPL, 7% at POL and -7% at OGDC; no circular-debt exposure; management talking of copper, gold, IT and AI with "no concrete development". The explanation for December was a premium valuation, not news.
4. 23 Dec 2024 (09:48): PSX's notice on unusual movement in the price (doc 244802, unread). 24 Dec: PSO replied to one of its own (PSO file).

## How the move unfolded

Flat for four months at 1.02x to 1.05x. The first 252-high came on 16 Nov 2023 at 1.12x (93% ahead). December 2023 added 22% (1.37x), January 1.48x; one dip of 15% (25 Jan to 12 Feb), recovered by 29 Mar. Then a long plateau: 1.8x from April to July 2024.

The results day gave two 10% closes (1.87x, 2.05x on 8 and 9 Aug) and 2.5x at September end; a limit close on 18 Sep. November ended at 2.72x. Then the blow-off: 5 Dec +9.9% (3.28x), 9 Dec (3.69x), 13 Dec (4.81x), 16 Dec +9.9% to 5.29x, Rs899.73, on median value of Rs3.3bn a day. Eleven sessions took it from 2.72x to 5.29x, 40% of the whole log gain. Then three limit-down closes, 17 to 19 Dec (4.76x, 4.28x, 3.86x); the notice on 23 Dec at 4.22x; 3.06x on 28 Jan 2025 (-42%), 4.0x in April, 3.99x on 16 Oct 2026 (price only 3.71x). To the same peak date OGDC made 3.11x and PPL 3.08x, and by 16 Oct 2026 OGDC stood higher than MARI (4.47x against 3.99x).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Ahead of each entry (log share): 1.12x on 16 Nov 2023, 93%; 1.25x on 7 Dec, 84%; 1.5x on 12 Jan 2024, 75%; 2x on 9 Aug, 57%; 3x on 5 Dec 2024, 29%; 4x on 11 Dec, 15%.

- First reasonable entry: 16 Nov 2023 (1.12x) on the breakout, or 12 Jan 2024 (1.52x), after the discovery filing. Neither needed foresight of the bonus.
- To stay in: the only dip of the run was 15%. A 25% trail was never touched until the blow-off.
- Reasonable exit: the third limit-down close, 19 Dec 2024. A 25% trail from the entry high exits there, at 3.86x of start.
- Rs100,000 from 16 Nov 2023: Rs344,000 on the 25% trail (19 Dec), Rs312,000 on a 30% trail (22 Jan 2025), Rs355,000 held to 16 Oct 2026. From 7 Dec 2023 (1.31x): Rs293,000 and Rs303,000. From 12 Jan 2024 (1.52x): Rs255,000 and Rs263,000. From 9 Aug 2024 (2.05x): Rs188,000 and Rs194,000. From 5 Dec 2024 (3.28x): Rs117,000 on the trail, Rs121,000 held. Exits chosen after the fact; n = 1; costs not deducted.
- Where joining late stopped paying: above about 3x, on 5 Dec 2024, when the last leg was already running at 10% a day.

## The trap

The blow-off. Ten per cent closes on rising volume looked like SSGC in 2024 (case 09), and a buyer on 13 Dec at 4.81x had 3.86x within four sessions. The company's own reply, and the exchange notice, came after the peak. The second trap was the plateau of April to July 2024, long enough to make a 20% trail look safe and a patient reader look wrong.

## Questions that would have caught it

- C7: demand or operator? Here the earnings were real and the last leg still looked like a pump.
- C4 and B11: confirmation at 1.12x, extension at 4.8x; a premium to the sector was the stated reason for the fall.
- B2: the levers (gas price, discoveries, bonus) and what was already in the price.
- C9: the exit written before entry.

## The if-then rule

If an E&P or similar index heavyweight breaks to a new 252-session high with real earnings growth, join and hold with a 25% trail from the entry high; if it then adds 90% in about three weeks on 10% closes, do not join, and sell on the third limit-down close.

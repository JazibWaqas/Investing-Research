# Winner: Attock Refinery (ATRL), November 2022 to October 2026

Written 2026-10-18. Type: results jump, then regime and policy wave. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/ATRL.csv`, `data/panel_close_adj.parquet` (multiples are total return), `data/announcements.parquet`, PSX documents 195767, 199071 and 222415 (scanned, read by eye), `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`, `knowledge/situations/04` and `07`. Simulations (`tests/out/winners_results_jumps_sims.txt`): buy at the next open, sell at the open after a close falls the stated percentage under the highest close since entry, 0.5% round-trip cost, before tax, open positions marked at 16 Oct 2026.

## Dates and regime

Library start 28 Nov 2022 (raw Rs165.62). Policy rate 16% (hiked 25 Nov), rising to 22% by 26 Jun 2023. CPI 23.8% in November 2022. USD/PKR 224, then 286 by July 2023. KMI All Share 20,736, which became 65,474 by 16 Oct 2026 (3.2x); ATRL did 7.6x.

## What was knowable, and when

1. 25 Oct 2022, 13:18: first-quarter results, EPS Rs68.50 against Rs4.36, gross profit Rs9.94bn against Rs1.6m (doc 195767). Four times that quarter was more than the Rs168.67 share price. The day closed -0.7%.
2. 13 Dec 2022: material information, main crude unit shut about eight days for want of furnace-oil storage, refinery at 35% capacity (doc 199071). 20 and 21 Dec: lower locks, close Rs123.04.
3. 28 Feb and 27 Apr 2023: the next two quarters, EPS 78.7 and 86.0 per the EPS file (unverified against filings). Price Rs161 to Rs184.
4. 3 Jul 2023: the first session after the IMF staff-level agreement (case 04). ATRL locked up, +7.5%, to Rs184.50, its first close above its prior 252-day high; it locked again on 4 Jul on 6.0m shares.
5. 12 Dec 2023: material information that two crude units were shut and throughput cut to about 60% (doc 222415). The stock rose 1.4% that day.
6. 2025 and 2026: a Q3 FY25 loss (EPS -9.06, EPS file), crude-unit shutdown notices on 16 Apr and 27 May 2025, and for 2026 the refinery policy approval and Q3 FY26 EPS Rs106.41 (case 07, PSX doc 275863).

## How the move unfolded

The first sign was the filing, and the price did not answer. It drifted to Rs123 (21 Dec, minus 26% from the next open) and spent until July at Rs155 to Rs185 while three quarters of huge profit were filed. The IMF session of 3 Jul 2023 broke it; by 10 Oct 2023 (Q1 FY24 EPS 115.4, lock to Rs284.95) it was 1.7x the start. It paused in a Rs330 to Rs420 range, peaked at Rs418.88 on 19 Apr 2024 (2.5x), fell 29% to Rs298.74 on 9 Oct 2024, then ran 2.5x in 53 sessions to Rs741 on 23 Dec 2024 with two PSX unusual-movement letters (14 Nov 2024, 2 Jan 2025). It then lost 39% to Rs451 on 8 May 2025 (the India shock, a loss quarter, shutdowns) and made new highs in January 2026 (Rs949), 2 Mar 2026 a lower lock, and Rs1,219.10 on 16 Oct 2026. At the first 252-day high about 95% of the log gain was still ahead; at 10 Oct 2023, 73% (4.4x to come); at 19 Apr 2024, 54%.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

- Buying on the 25 Oct 2022 filing and holding through everything: Rs50,000 became about Rs377,000 by 16 Oct 2026 (7.5x net), after a worst close 26% under entry. A 25% trailing stop sold on 21 Dec 2022 at minus 22%: Rs50,000 to Rs38,961.
- Re-entering on 3 Jul 2023 (first 252-day high, buy 4 Jul near Rs178 adjusted) with a 40% stop: 6.8x to 16 Oct 2026. The two legs together: Rs50,000 to about Rs265,000, Rs200,000 to about Rs1.06m, unrealised. The failed first leg cost about a third of the result.
- Same entry with a 30% stop: out on 8 May 2025 at 2.8x (Rs50,000 to Rs138,000); the stock then rose another 2.5x. A 25% stop left on 8 Oct 2024 at 1.7x. The stop width decided most of the outcome; the real reason to stay was earnings arriving at a fraction of the price.
- Joining late: on 10 Oct 2023 with a 40% stop, 4.3x. Chasing the 23 Dec 2024 high: minus 28% with a 25% stop, plus 69% with no stop after a 36% drawdown lasting four months.

## The trap

An EPS jump that the price will not follow is the market saying the profit will not repeat. Here it nearly said right: the December 2022 shutdown took 26% off in two months. "Material Information" titles were mostly shutdown news and the stock rose anyway. Failure twins: the 2026 windfall refiners that lagged for ten weeks (case 07), and the Dec 2024 chase.

## Which questions would have caught it

A1 and A4 (policy and IMF trigger), B1 (earnings power: spread windfall against structural), B11 (price at under one year of earnings), C1 and C4 (a type, and price confirming only from 3 Jul 2023), C9 (stop against pullbacks of 26%, 29%, 39%), C10 (the 15x label was a low base).

## The if-then rule

If a liquid KMI name files a quarter of earnings worth more than its share price and the price does not move, do not buy the filing: put it on a trigger list and buy the first close above its 252-day high, with a stop wider than 35% or an exit tied to earnings turning negative. One case, untested as a rule.

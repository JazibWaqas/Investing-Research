# Winner: Systems Limited, June 2019 to September 2021

Written 2026-10-18. Type: IT-services wave, a quality business re-rated. Sources: `data/prices_raw/SYS.csv` and `data/panel_close_adj.parquet` (a bonus and a 5-for-1 split on 2 Jun 2025 are adjusted; raw Rs90.03 at the start is Rs7.66 on today's shares), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/daily_table.parquet` (KMI flags), Dawn and ProPakistani via search (asserted). Later history is in `knowledge/situations/10_2026_sys_derating_failure.md`.

## Dates and regime

- 24 Jun 2019: rate 12.25% heading to 13.25%, bill 12.7%, KSE 34,472, rupee falling after the IMF deal (case 01).
- 10 Jun 2020: rate 8.0% after 525bp of cuts from 13.25% (case 02), 3-month bill 8.1% (3 Jun), KSE 35,065, 29% above its 25 Mar low.
- 10 Sep 2021: peak; rate 7.0%, CPI 9.0%, USD/PKR 167.6, KSE 47,198.

## What was knowable, and when

1. Dawn (asserted, Jan 2021): SYS was the best performer of 2020, +273.6% to Rs468.63; trailing P/E about 20x against about 38x for Indian and Brazilian IT peers (KASB note). So the multiple, not only earnings, was re-rating.
2. 26 Aug 2020: H1 2020 profit Rs1.06bn (ProPakistani, asserted). 4 Mar 2021: 2020 profit up 38% to Rs2.16bn on revenue up 31% to Rs9.87bn; IT exports above $1bn in seven months of FY21.
3. Filings: a director bought shares 1 Oct 2019 (doc 135577); a director sold 15 Jun 2020 (doc 148927) at 2.04x. Bare "Material Information" on 30 and 31 Jan 2020, 25 Mar and 10 Dec 2020 (unread). Quarterly EPS exists in the repo only from late 2021.

## How the move unfolded

Nothing happened for five months: 0.91x to 1.0x through October 2019 at Rs1m to Rs3m a day (an illiquid start; a reader could not have sized into it). Volume woke in November (monthly median Rs18m, then Rs37m in December); 1.10x on 26 Nov, 1.58x in January, 1.74x on 17 Feb. Covid cut it 41% to 1.04x on 25 Mar, recovered by 29 May. A 7.5% limit close on 8 Jun 2020 took it to 1.98x; 2.03x on 10 Jun.

From there the climb was orderly: 3x on 7 Sep (four limit closes in a week), 4.1x in November, 5.6x in February 2021. Pullbacks stayed at 16% (Oct 2020, Mar 2021) until the end. Peak 10.07x on 10 Sep 2021, median value Rs174m a day that month. A 7.5% limit-down close on 2 Dec took it to 9.0x, and a 26% decline followed to 7.5x on 8 Mar 2022. Then it was a different story: 22x on 7 Jan 2026, 15.5x on 16 Oct 2026 (price only, 14.5x).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Ahead at recognition (log share): 1.16x first 252-high, 27 Nov 2019, 94%; 2.03x, 10 Jun 2020, 69%; 3x, 50%; 5x, 30%; 7x, 15%.

- First reasonable entry: 10 Jun 2020 at 2.03x, a 100% move from the base already made, Rs43m a day by then, member of KMI. Entry on 27 Nov 2019 (1.16x) was stopped by Covid at 0.89x to 1.06x on every trail up to 40%; whoever held through it had 13.3x by October 2026.
- To stay in: pullbacks were 16%, 16%, then the 26% after the peak. A 25% trail from the entry high held until 7 Mar 2022.
- Exit: 25% trail, 7 Mar 2022 at 3.71x of entry; a 100-day average rule, 10 Feb 2022 at 4.28x; or no exit.
- Rs100,000 from 10 Jun 2020: Rs371,000 (25% trail), Rs428,000 (average rule), Rs757,000 (30% trail, hit 2 Mar 2026), Rs763,000 held. Entry at 3.17x (7 Sep 2020): Rs237,000 on the 25% trail and Rs488,000 held. At 4.36x (16 Dec 2020): Rs172,000 and Rs354,000. Late entry paid here because the business went on earning: EPS Rs1.90 to Rs2.10 a quarter in late 2025 (repo series), which is why the exit mattered less. The 2026 fall is a derating, not a failure of the story.
- Not paying: joining at 10x in Sep 2021 would have meant 4 to 6 months of dead money and a 26% loss to 8 Mar 2022.

## The trap

Entering early (the 27 Nov 2019 entry, hit by Covid), and two KMI gaps. The derived lists show SYS out of the All Share index from 31 Dec 2020 to 5 Jul 2021 (unverified), exactly the middle of the run; a KMI-only investor might have had to sell and repurchase. The failure twin is the same name later (case 10): a 31% fall in 2026 with earnings up.

## Questions that would have caught it

- B1, B11: five-year earnings against the multiple; 20x trailing was priced as a growth name, not a cheap one.
- B10: KMI status checked at the time, and again at each add.
- A6: whether the market itself was cheap or rerated (rerated by 2026).
- C9: an exit written before entry; a 25% trail matched this stock's 16% dips.

## The if-then rule

If a high-quality exporter doubles from its base with pullbacks of 16% or less and volume 10x its start, join on the second new high and hold with a 25 to 30% trail; the rule fails when the multiple, not the earnings, is what is rising, so check the trailing P/E and KMI status at every entry.

# Winner case: Fauji Fertilizer Bin Qasim, December 2022 to December 2024

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_delisted/FFBL.csv` (crude adjusted chain; FFBL is delisted), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/delistings.csv`, `data/index_changes.csv`; Profit 5 Aug and 20 Sep 2024 (fetched), ProPakistani Feb 2024 (snippets). Simulations: entry at the signal close, exit at the next close after a closing-price trailing stop, 0.5% round trip, before tax; the last print (20 Dec 2024) is the exit if no stop fired.

## Dates and regime

Start 20 Dec 2022 (Rs14.90, -5.4% that day), a month before the KSE-100 low of 38,342 on 17 Jan 2023. Same regime as UBL and MEBL (agreement 29 or 30 Jun 2023, +5.9% on 3 Jul; first cut 10 Jun 2024). FFC owned 49.88% of FFBL.

## What was knowable and when

"Material Information" notices on 22 Dec 2022 and six dates in 2023 (content not opened), and an "Unusual movement in the price" notice on 22 Dec 2023. The cause of the Nov to Dec 2023 jump is unverified; I found no coverage. In Feb 2024 the ECC approved a gas price increase of up to 35.13% and FFBL raised urea prices by Rs1,350 a bag (ProPakistani). The merger: FFC's board approved evaluating the amalgamation on 19 Jul 2024 (FFBL near Rs41); H1 2024 FFBL profit about Rs10.6bn against a Rs5bn loss a year before, with finance cost down about 60% (Profit; the article's numbers are internally inconsistent, so treat them as indicative); the article's three valuation methods gave 3.26 to 3.59 FFBL per FFC. On 20 Sep 2024 FFBL's board approved 1 FFC for 4.29 FFBL, worse for FFBL holders than that range; the shares fell 7.7% that day. The Lahore High Court sanctioned the scheme on 4 Dec; FFC issued 150,870,449 shares.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

First reasonable entry: 1 Nov 2023 at Rs18.85. Rs100,000 became Rs144,000 on a 20% closing stop (13 Feb 2024), Rs129,000 on 25%, and Rs469,000 on 30% or 40%, held to the merger. Entry at the volume ignition on 15 Dec 2023 (2.06x) drew down 21% and returned 0.8x to 0.9x on 20 to 25% stops, 2.88x on 30% or wider. After the ratio was fixed, entry on 23 Sep 2024 returned 1.93x with no stop needed and no loss beyond FFC's own risk. Rs200,000 was under 0.1% of a day's value at the ignition.

## The trap

The library's 6.5x is measured to a close on 16 Dec; the last trade was 5.97x and the line ends in a swap, so there is no "exit" signal. The ignition of Dec 2023 looked like a merger story, but the merger was not public until Jul 2024. The twins that failed are the 2023 entries stopped at 20 to 25% in the Feb 2024 gas-price shakeout; each stop was followed by a higher high.

## If-then rule

Once a merger swap ratio is fixed, compare the target with the acquirer divided by the ratio: if they are within 5%, you hold the acquirer and should decide on that. Before the ratio exists, buy the first 252-high after a volume ignition only with a 30% stop and a reason you can write down.

# Winner: Unity Foods, May 2019 to June 2021 (6.0x, overstated)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/UNITY.csv` (quoted and adjusted prices are identical: the adjustment does not treat rights issues, see below), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`. News: an Arif Habib sector report, a Profit note on a Rs3bn sukuk (28 Nov 2020) and Wilmar's SGX filing, all from web search on 18 Oct 2026 (snippets only). KMI member at the start and today.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 10.75% at the start, 13.25% in July 2019, 7.0% from June 2020. KMI All Share +52% over the run (15,542 to 23,634). The library sector basket (four food names) rose 39%. The name was formerly Taha Spinning Mills.

## What was knowable, and when

- 19 Feb 2019: declaration of right shares. 22 Mar 2019: an issue of right shares of 221.83% at par Rs10. 18 Jun 2020: a second declaration; 29 Jul 2020: right shares of 82.71% at par; Sep 2020: a "third right issue". Each was priced at Rs10 while the stock traded at Rs13 to Rs25, so each was strongly dilutive. The share count grew many times; the size was not calculated here.
- 52 insider-trade filings in the window (direction not recorded), including director and sponsor subscriptions to the rights.
- 1 Oct 2019 FY2019 results. 30 Oct 2019: results "revoked" and "revised" on the same day.
- Feb 2021: Wilmar's Singapore unit bought about 19% from the founders at Rs10 a share, lifting its holding to about 30% (Wilmar SGX filing, via search). Quarterly EPS: Dec 2020 0.96, Mar 2021 1.24, Jun 2021 0.46.
- A May 2021 broker initiation put a December 2021 target of Rs54.4, 42% above the close (implying about Rs38); revenue growth was about 120% (snippet, unverified).

## How the move unfolded

An early spike to 1.25x on 22 May 2019 (+12% days), then a 35% fall to August. The stock then went 1.5x in November (Rs12.9) on turnover of Rs103m a day and 2x on 1 Jan 2020 (Rs16.8, Rs372m a day in December). The Covid crash took 54% off by 24 Mar; the old high came back on 18 Sep 2020 (Rs18.35). Then a straight line: 2.8x in October, 3x on 26 Nov (Rs24.8), 4x on 20 Jan 2021 (Rs34.2), a 30% break from 1 Feb to 11 Mar, 5x on 24 May (Rs41.3), peak Rs49.36 on 10 Jun 2021. Turnover Rs440m to Rs850m a day.

After the peak: 0.87x at 10 sessions, 0.78x at 40, 0.45x at 120.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax. These returns are on the unadjusted series and ignore rights subscription: someone who did not subscribe was diluted, and the real per-share gain for the shares held through the 2020 issues is lower than shown.

- 26 Nov 2019 (1.5x, Rs12.9): 3.8x still ahead, but a 30% stop sold on 10 Feb 2020 at -9%; a 20% stop at +6% (3 Feb).
- 21 Sep 2020 (first 252-day high, 2.2x, Rs18.35): 2.7x ahead, but a 20% stop sold at -24% two weeks later (5 Oct); a 30% stop sold on 11 Mar 2021 at +38% (Rs100,000 becoming about Rs137,000); a 40% stop on 12 Oct 2021 at +56%.
- 27 Nov 2020 (3x, 1.94x ahead): +13% on a 20% stop, -1% on a 30% stop.
- Where it paid: the 21 Sep 2020 entry with a 40% leash, +56%, for a signal that had 2.7x ahead; nothing else paid well.

## The trap

A rising price that the capital structure contradicts. Two rights issues in 12 months at Rs10 below market, a strategic buyer paying Rs10 to founders while the market paid Rs25 to Rs40, and a result "revised" on the day: the stock was a bet on the story, not on per-share earnings. Twins: the failure of this stock itself (84% down) and food and edible-oil names in 2021 with the same shape.

## Questions that would have caught it

B1 (per-share earnings after dilution; EPS here 0.46 to 1.24 a quarter against a Rs25 to Rs49 price), B6 (how the sponsor treats minorities: rights at par, a founder sale at Rs10), B8 (cash conversion; the company needed a Rs3bn sukuk for working capital), C7 (volume with locks), C10 (corporate-action gaps in the series).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a rising stock has issued rights at par more than once in a year while its price is well above Rs10, and a strategic buyer's price is below the market price, do not let the price multiple stand in for return: size at a quarter of a normal position, use a 30% leash, and exit on the first close 30% below the high.

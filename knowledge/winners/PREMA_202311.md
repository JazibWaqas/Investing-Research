# Winner: At-Tahur, November 2023 to April 2025 (5.5x)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/PREMA.csv` (no corporate action), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`, `data/macro/`. News (web search, snippets): Mettis Global on PREMA's sukuk (April and June 2025) and a trademark licence to Minha Edible Oils (Sep 2025); TradingView technical notes. No cause for the spike was found: unverified. A KMI member.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 22% at the start, 12% at the peak. KMI All Share +82% over the run (27,563 to 50,120); the library's seven-name food basket rose 38%. At-Tahur runs dairy farms and sells milk and dairy products.

## What was knowable, and when

- Basic EPS: Sep 2023 0.14, Dec 2023 0.34, Mar 2024 0.60, Jun 2024 0.54, Sep 2024 0.11, Dec 2024 0.63, Mar 2025 0.90. At a price of Rs60 to Rs87 in April 2025 that is a trailing P/E of roughly 30 to 45.
- Material information on 11 Dec 2023, 20 Sep 2024, 2 Dec 2024, 19 Feb 2025, 7, 8 and 14 Apr 2025 (before the peak) and 6 May, 4 and 13 Jun 2025. Titles bare. 9 Dec 2024: an unusual-movement query.
- April 2025: PREMA withdrew a notice to redeem Rs750m of sukuk early and later repaid it at maturity (Mettis).
- Turnover: Rs2.6m a day at the start, Rs55m in Nov 2024, Rs227m in March 2025, Rs499m in April.

## How the move unfolded

A year of 0.8x to 1.1x, with a 31% dip. A first 252-day high on 30 Aug 2024 (1.12x). 1.7x in November 2024 (+10% on 4 Nov), 2x on 6 Dec (Rs33.2), then three violent legs: a 32% fall to 19 Dec, 1.9x in February, a 38% fall from 18 Feb to 4 Mar, then 3x on 14 Mar (Rs47.1), a 33% fall to 9 Apr, 4x on 18 Apr (Rs63.9), 5x on 23 Apr (Rs85.05), peak close Rs86.74 on 25 Apr 2025. 41 upper locks in all (14 in 2024, 26 in 2025).

After the peak: 0.51x after 10 sessions (the count includes the 8 May 2025 market fall of 6.6%), 0.47x after 40, 0.46x after 60, 0.27x at the 30 Mar 2026 low.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 2 Sep 2024 (first 252-day high, Rs16.4, 1.05x): 5.3x ahead on paper. A 20% leash sold on 11 Oct at -11% (about Rs88,000); a 40% leash held to 8 May 2025 at Rs47.3, +189%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs287,000, after a 45% drawdown from the running high.
- 9 Dec 2024 (1.9x, Rs30.2): -7% on a 30% leash. 17 Mar 2025 (3.3x, Rs51.8): -27% in three weeks.
- The exit even for the 40% leash came at 0.55 of the peak, 13 days after it. A Rs200,000 order at the start was 8% of a day's value.
- Where joining late paid: only before 2x. After that the 20 to 30% leash sold at a loss.

## The trap

An earnings-free spike with locks: the library calls it a policy wave (food basket +38%), but the basket's gains were a fraction of PREMA's and the EPS never moved. Three violent legs, each ending in a -30% to -38% break, are the signature; they stop out any reasonable leash. Failure twin: the same shape in other thin dairy and food names; this is the "operator" shape with a milk label.

## Questions that would have caught it

C7 (volume with locks: demand or an operator?), B9 (float and exit), B11 (what is priced in: 30x to 45x trailing), B1 (EPS 0.9 a quarter), C10 (is the sector story real: +38% for peers against +450%).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a thin food name rises 3x on limit-up days with EPS flat, do not buy after 2x; if you hold from before, take half at 3x, put the rest on a 40% leash, and treat the first -30% day sequence as the exit, since here the last 2x lasted six weeks and the next 50% fall took ten sessions.

## How the move unfolded

A year of ranging: 1.37x in November 2023 on Rs38m a day, down to 0.98x by September 2024. Then nine weeks: 1.45x in October, 3.27x in November, 5.66x in December. 2x on 11 Nov (Rs18.7), 3x on 15 Nov (Rs26.4), 4x on 5 Dec (Rs35.9), 5x on 18 Dec (Rs44.6), peak close Rs48.80 on 30 Dec (the situation file's Rs49.52 is the unadjusted close; the library's series carries dividends). Situation 09 counts 12 closes at +10% from 23 Oct to 31 Jan; the library counts 20 upper locks to the peak; turnover Rs730m a day in November and Rs1.46bn in December.

After the peak: 0.88x at 10 sessions, 0.80x at 20, 0.64x at 40; first close 20% below the high on 7 Jan 2025. A low on 30 Mar 2026 (Rs19.43).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 21 Nov 2023 (first 252-day high, Rs12.3, 1.42x): 4.0x ahead on paper, but the stock then fell 40% by August 2024: a 30% leash sold at -22% on 7 Jun 2024. The library's "first 252-day high" was a year early.
- 12 Nov 2024 (2.4x, Rs20.5, after the 5 Nov results day and the volume): a 20% leash sold on 7 Jan 2025 at Rs39.24, +91%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs190,000. 18 Nov (3.1x, Rs27.1): +45%, about Rs144,000.
- What would have made the first entry rational: the OGRA decision of 1 Oct and the 23 Oct volume ignition, as in the situation file. Three days of volume at 20x the base was the recognisable event.

## The trap

The library's start. A 5.7x headline hides a flat year; the readable signal was one day in October 2024. A second trap: the late accounts (losses in the FY23 accounts on the day of the +10% close). Failure twin: the November 2023 first-high entry itself.

## Questions that would have caught it

C7 (volume with locks), C3 (who decides and when: OGRA), B1 (one-offs: the FY23 profit that was not in the accounts), B9 (can I exit at my size: Rs730m a day), C9 (the exit before the entry).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a regulated utility prints 20x its base volume with a regulator decision in the previous month and the price breaks its 252-day high, buy a starter on the volume day and hold on a 20% leash, because the run lasts nine weeks; a first 252-day high with ordinary volume a year earlier is not the signal.

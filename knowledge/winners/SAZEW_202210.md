# Winner: Sazgar Engineering Works, October 2022 to February 2026 (43.8x)

Written 2026-10-18. The largest run in the library, and the one with the most traceable cause. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/SAZEW.csv` (quoted prices Rs58.67 to Rs2,455.81), `data/announcements.parquet` (monthly production and sales filings, results), `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv`, `data/macro/`. News (web search, 18 Oct 2026, snippets): ProPakistani on Q3 FY23 profit and on FY25 and FY26 annual results, Profit on FY25 sales and Haval volumes. The brief's other mention, a "sales record in January 2024", is unconfirmed (snippet figures: 436 four-wheelers in Jan 2023, 967 in Jan 2024).

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 15% (start), 22% from Jun 2023, then cut to 10.5% by the peak. KMI All Share 20,914 to 70,584 (+237%), so the market contributed 3.4x of the 43.8x. Rupee 224 to 280; imports were restricted for much of 2022 to 2023, which kept car supply tight. Sazgar is a KMI member. Autos sector basket (five peers) +73%.

## What was knowable, and when

1. From at least Jul 2022: a monthly PSX filing of four-wheeler and three-wheeler production and sales ("Production and Sales data", about the 10th of every month).
2. 8 Feb 2023: Dec-2022 quarter EPS 1.13 against 0.51 a year earlier. 18 Apr 2023: Mar-2023 quarter EPS 7.36 against 0.41; profit Rs444.6m, +1,689%, sales Rs7.9bn, +176% (ProPakistani, 18 Apr). Price 0.91x the start (about Rs50).
3. 26 Oct 2023: Sep-2023 quarter EPS 10.89 (vs 0.18 a year earlier); 31 Jan 2024: Dec quarter 12.50.
4. 19 Apr 2024: Mar-2024 quarter EPS 50.2 (a four-fold step); 23 Sep 2024: FY24 results, profit Rs7.9bn per ProPakistani; FY25 (1 Sep 2025) Rs16.3bn, FY26 Rs23.6bn (ProPakistani, Aug 2026), Haval volumes +102% in FY25 and four-wheeler sales 19,179 in FY26.
5. Price-and-volume queries from the exchange: 25 Mar 2024 and 7 Aug 2024 ("unusual movement"). Material information filings on 10, 16 and 26 Jan 2024.

## How the move unfolded

Six months of nothing after the first huge quarter: 0.77x to 0.91x of the start from March to June 2023 while EPS jumped 18x. The 252-day high came on 31 Jul 2023 (1.19x, quoted Rs75). Then steps: 1.5x on 22 Sep, 2x on 12 Oct, 3x on 2 Nov (Rs185), a 28% break in December, 4x on 14 Feb 2024 (Rs250), 7x on 7 Mar, 10x on 22 Apr (Rs597), 15x on 5 Jul 2024 (Rs915), 20x on 14 Apr 2025, 30x on 17 Sep 2025, 40x on 2 Feb 2026 (Rs2,343), peak close Rs2,455.81 on 9 Feb 2026. Turnover grew with it: Rs56m a day in September 2023, Rs1.1bn in June 2024. No pullback exceeded 30%; the largest were -30% (Nov 2022 to Apr 2023), -28% (Dec 2023), -22% (Apr to Jun 2025). 47 upper locks, the biggest day +10%.

The market-wide shocks (26 Dec 2023 index -4.2%, 8 May 2025 -6.6%, 2 Mar 2026 -9.2%) all sat inside those pullbacks.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, dividends included, 0.5% round trip, before tax (`data/costs.md`).

- The first reasonable entry was the 31 Jul 2023 252-day high (about Rs75, 1.28x on the next close): 34x of the peak still ahead. Alternatively, the 18 Apr 2023 results at Rs50 for a reader who valued the earnings, not the chart.
- A 20% leash sold on 20 Dec 2023 at Rs172, +128%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs227,000, then a re-entry on 27 Dec at Rs173 with a 25% leash held to 16 Mar 2026 (Rs1,789, +981%, about Rs1.08m). A single 30% leash from 1 Aug 2023 held to 26 Mar 2026 at Rs1,693: +2,250%, about Rs2.34m. A buyer at 4.3x (14 Feb 2024, Rs250) with a 30% leash made +606%, Rs703,000.
- These stop exits all came at about 0.7 of the peak, after the 9 Feb 2026 top, which no rule would have picked. Rs200,000 was well under 1% of a day's value from November 2023; liquidity was never the limit.
- Where joining late still paid: every date up to about 10x (April 2024). The 15x to 40x stretch gave 2.9x more, still a good deal but a smaller one.

## The trap

Waiting for the chart after a profit report that moved 18-fold. Also the three market-wide shakeouts: a 20% leash sold in December 2023 at 7% of the eventual peak price. The failure twins are single-quarter spikes: GAL's March 2023 quarter was EPS 5.50, followed by -0.55, -1.61 and 0.09 (`quarterly_eps.csv`); the test is whether the next four quarters repeat it.

## Questions that would have caught it

B1 (consolidated, one-offs stripped: here it repeated four quarters in a row), B2 (the sector's levers: import limits, new models), B13 (growing, not stable), C4 (confirmation came in July 2023), C9 (leash and the pullback depth the stock actually showed).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If quarterly EPS rises ten-fold year on year for two quarters running, the monthly sales filing confirms the volume, and the price has not yet made a 252-day high, then buy a starter on the second quarter and add on the high; hold on a 30% leash and re-enter after a stop-out on the reclaim, because here the market took six months to price a 18-fold profit jump and then took three years to finish.

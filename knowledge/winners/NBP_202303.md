# Winner: NBP, 2023 to 2026

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: price and volume panels (adjusted series includes dividends, so multiples are total return), `data/announcements.parquet`, NBP press releases for 2024 and 2025 (via search, figures asserted), Business Recorder, Profit, S&P Global through Arab News (via search). EPS rows for NBP are not in `quarterly_eps.csv`. Not KMI.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 20% to 22% through the start of the run (22% from 26 Jun 2023), cut from 10 Jun 2024 to 11% in May 2025, 10.5% in Dec 2025, hiked to 11.5% on 27 Apr 2026. KSE-100 4.71x and KMI All Share 3.81x from start to peak. Same-window multiples: BOP 10.2x, UBL 9.5x, AKBL 7.6x, MEBL 7.5x, BAHL 5.5x, FABL 5.1x, HBL 4.7x, BAFL 4.6x, MCB 3.6x. NBP was the top of a bank wave that began in 2023.

## What was knowable and when

2023: board kept the profit and paid no dividend; capital adequacy 25.47% (NBP release via search). 2024: pre-tax profit Rs56.7bn after a Rs68.0bn pension-litigation charge, Rs8 a share dividend, the highest payout ever. 2025: highest-ever net profit Rs85.9bn, pre-tax +216% to Rs178.9bn, a Rs35 dividend (350%), cost of funds 8.8%, sovereign upgrade from CCC to B- cited. The release says market value rose about tenfold in two years to about $2.0bn. 2 Feb 2026: material information on a restructuring offer to Hascol, a borrower. Unverified: why the price jumped on 21 Nov 2023 (+10% on 8m shares) and on 10 Jul 2024; the PSX "material information" filings of 21 Mar 2024, 10 and 15 Oct 2024, 27 Dec 2024 and 17 Oct 2025 have no retrievable text here. S&P Global ranked NBP second among Asia-Pacific banks in 2025 at +301% total return.

## How the move unfolded

Rs21.30 on 30 Mar 2023; flat to Rs24 for eight months (15 Mar 2023 volume spike on 3x the base was the first sign, with no follow-through). A new 252-day high on 20 Nov 2023 (Rs27.44): volume 5m to 9m shares, Rs37.16 on 11 Dec, -28% by Feb 2024, then a staircase: Rs51 on 10 Jul 2024, Rs76 on 3 Dec, Rs88 in May 2025, Rs126 in July, Rs177 on 10 Sep, Rs214 on 8 Oct. Seven pullbacks of 15% or more inside the run, the worst 28% (Jan to Mar 2024); limit-ups were rare (16 in 704 sessions). Peak Rs286.42 on 4 Feb 2026. Then the March 2026 shock: -34% (adjusted) by 31 Mar, a recovery to about Rs205 in July, Rs158.20 on 16 Oct.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

First reasonable entry: 20 Nov 2023 (new high, bought Rs27.44 at the next close; 10.7x still ahead at that point). A 25% stop sells on 31 Jan 2024 at 1.01x (the worst result and the one most readers would run). A 30% stop holds to 30 Mar 2026 at Rs157.25: 6.9x, about Rs586,000 net on Rs100,000 (3,644 shares). Entry on 10 Jul 2024 (Rs52.54) 3.6x on the 30% stop; 13 Feb 2025 (Rs76.66) 2.8x on a 25% stop; 17 Jun 2025 (Rs104.26) 2.1x; 8 Oct 2025 (Rs205.77) 1.04x. Liquidity was never a constraint after Dec 2023 (Rs100,000 was under 0.1% of a day's volume). Late entry paid until about mid-2025; the 25% stop hurt only the earliest entry.

For this investor the same shape existed in KMI banks (MEBL 7.5x), so the lesson is transferable even though NBP is not buyable.

## The trap

Taking a flat start as noise (eight months at Rs20 to Rs24) and then a tight stop in a 22% rate regime. The other trap is the one-off: the 2024 profit was after a Rs68bn charge, and 2025's Rs85.9bn was a recovery plus lower funding cost.

## The if-then rule

If a sector rises on an earnings and rate story, buy its biggest laggard-turned-leader only on a new 252-day high with volume, hold with a 30% stop, and check the KMI equivalent first. Stop adding when the stock is more than 5x the entry and the trailing P/E has doubled.

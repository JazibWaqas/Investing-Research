# Winner: NetSol Technologies (NETSOL), May 2023 to July 2024

Written 2026-10-18. Type: results jump (a one-off) then a theme run. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/NETSOL.csv`, `data/panel_close_adj.parquet`, `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv` (the file says 15.28 for the March 2023 quarter; the filing says 15.98), PSX documents 206857, 209683 and 232052 (scanned, read by eye), `data/macro/`. Simulations: `tests/out/winners_results_jumps_sims.txt` (method in the ATRL case).

## Dates and regime

Library start and low 26 May 2023 (Rs70.33). Policy rate 21%, hiked to 22% on 26 Jun. CPI 38%. USD/PKR 285: NetSol sells software in dollars. KMI All Share 19,840, 36,167 at the peak (1.8x against NETSOL 2.15x). Trailing P/E about 5.5 at the March results (EPS file).

## What was knowable, and when

1. 27 Apr 2023, 13:36: Q3 FY23 EPS Rs15.98 against 1.83; nine months 16.31 against 9.76 (doc 206857). Reading the statement: operating profit Rs202m against Rs109m, other income Rs1,468m against Rs164m. Other income was about the whole of the quarter's profit before tax (Rs1,468m of Rs1,460m); the filing does not give its cause (foreign-exchange gains would be a guess, unverified).
2. The stock rose 6.9% that day on 2.0m shares, then lost 11% over 20 sessions to the 26 May low. Worst close 14% under the next open.
3. 1 Jun 2023: the board proposed raising authorized capital from Rs1.5bn to Rs9bn (doc 209683); 3 Jul: IMF day, lock-up to Rs80.44.
4. Quarterly EPS afterwards: minus 2.72 (Jun 2023, filed 22 Sep), 2.42 (27 Oct), 5.65 against minus 2.42 (Dec 2023, filed 12 Feb 2024, 15:01), 0.15 (Mar 2024, 30 Apr), 5.47 (Jun 2024).
5. 22 May 2024: auditors resigned and were replaced (doc 232052). 18 Jul 2024: the peak day, a lock-up on 8.3m shares.

## How the move unfolded

The filing was the first sign and it led nowhere: Rs80 at the filing, Rs70 a month later. The IMF day gave a lock and Rs95.11 by 2 Aug (1.35x), then an 18% fall to Rs77.60 on 3 Oct. Volume came in October and November: 3.2m shares (7x the 20-day average) on 6 Oct, locks on 14 and 23 Nov, Rs121.24 on 11 Dec (the first 252-day high came 5 Dec at 1.62x, with only 37% of the log gain left). A 24% fall to Rs91.89 (26 Dec), a bounce, and a 17% fall to Rs89.04 on 30 Jan. The 12 Feb 2024 quarter (EPS 5.65) arrived at Rs90.77: the stock rose 40% in ten sessions, with five locks from 13 Feb to 5 Mar, to Rs137.26 on 5 Mar (1.95x). A 20% fall to Rs110 on 3 May and a last leg to Rs150.92 on 18 Jul 2024 on a lock. It then fell 18% to 2 Oct 2024 and ended at 81% of the peak two years later.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

- Buying the 27 Apr 2023 filing (Rs81.50): a 14% drawdown in 20 sessions; with a 25% trail out on 31 Jan 2024 at plus 8%; with a 30% trail out on 9 May 2025 at plus 39% (Rs50,000 to about Rs69,600). This is the "failed within 20 sessions" case: the jump had no price support.
- Buying the 3 Jul 2023 IMF day: plus 11% (20% trail), plus 40% (30% trail).
- Buying the 5 Dec 2023 first 252-day high (Rs117.51): minus 25% (25% trail), a 22% drawdown in 20 sessions. The first high came when the run was 63% done.
- Buying the 12 Feb 2024 results, after a pullback (Rs91.50): plus 44% with a 20% trail (out on 3 Mar 2025 at Rs132: Rs50,000 to Rs71,800, Rs200,000 to Rs287,000), plus 41% with 25%, plus 24% with 30%. This was the only entry that never closed below its cost.
- What kept you in: nothing structural. Five pullbacks of 17 to 24% fit inside a 25% stop only barely, and a 20% stop was hit in December 2023 (the 24% fall), so you needed a re-entry rule.

## The trap

Two. The jump was not earnings power: operating profit rose Rs93m, the rest was other income, and the next two quarters were a loss and Rs2.42. A trailing P/E of 5.5 looked cheap on a number that did not repeat. And the stock then rose anyway, on the market wave and a dollar-earner theme, so the reader who judged it by the filing would have bought the wrong thing for a wrong reason and sold it too soon. Failure twins inside this run: the 27 Apr 2023 filing and the 5 Dec 2023 breakout.

## Which questions would have caught it

B1 (one-off stripped: operating profit against other income), B8 (is the cash real), B11 (a P/E on a lumpy number), C2 (the base rate for results jumps is no better than other days: `tests/results_surprise_drift.md`), C4 (price confirming from 5 Dec), C9 and C10.

## The if-then rule

If a quarterly EPS jump is mostly other income while operating profit barely moves, treat it as no signal and ignore the filing. Look for the next quarter in which operating profit rises and the price pulls back to its range, buy that, and use a 20 to 25% trail with a written re-entry rule. One case; the one-off test is a reading rule, not a measured edge.

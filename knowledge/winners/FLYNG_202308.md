# Winner: Flying Cement, August 2023 to June 2025 (10.1x)

## How the move unfolded

Three months asleep, then 1.37x in November 2023 (first 252-day high on 27 Nov, 1.34x), a 24% fall into February 2024 and quiet through April. 1.5x on 10 May 2024, then the step: +12% and +11% on 6 and 7 Aug 2024, 2x on 21 Aug (Rs12.7), 3x on 24 Sep (Rs17.9), 4x on 1 Nov, 5x on 3 Dec (Rs31.1), a 30% fall to 8 Jan 2025 and a recovery to 5x by April. May 2025: 4.8x to 10.1x in a month on Rs102m a day, 7x on 14 May (Rs45), 10x on 30 May (Rs59.9), peak close Rs59.99 on 11 Jun 2025. 28 upper locks (13 in 2024).

After the peak: 0.91x, 0.84x, 0.88x, 0.92x at 10, 40, 60, 120 sessions, then 0.69x a year later, with turnover under Rs1.5m a day from April 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 28 Nov 2023 (first 252-day high, Rs8.28, 1.40x): 7.25x ahead. A 20% leash sold on 16 Jan 2024 at -18% (Rs100,000 became about Rs82,000); a 30% leash held to 17 Mar 2026, Rs41.44, +400%, about Rs498,000, after a 21% drawdown against entry; a 40% leash sold on 16 Sep 2026 at +330%.
- 22 Aug 2024 (2.4x, Rs14.0): +196% on a 30% leash, about Rs294,000, with 4.3x of the peak still ahead at entry.
- The 30% leash exit was at 0.69 of the peak. In liquid terms, a Rs200,000 order was 7% of a day's value at the start but 0.2% in 2024. The exit in March 2026 met Rs3.7m a day, and April's was Rs1.4m.

## The trap

The thin-float opening: 3.0m a day, so a Rs100,000 order was 3% of the day. The 24% dip of December to February 2024 looked like failure and killed the 20% leash. And the stock's final year: turnover fell by 99% from the Aug 2024 level, so the last exit on a stop could not fill near the stop price. Failure twins: thin cement and small-cap names with limit-up days and no cause (the library's thin list).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a small cement name closes at a 252-day high with turnover five times normal and a stake-change filing exists, buy a starter and hold on a 30% leash, since 20% was too tight here and the first dip is usually 20 to 25%; keep size at 1% of a day's turnover and expect the exit to be illiquid.

# Winner TPLT, 2025 to 2026: two legs, a 51% fall between them, and a KMI deletion

Written 2026-10-18. ## Dates and regime

Library start 10 Feb 2025 (Rs7.07 adjusted), peak 7 Sep 2026 (Rs33.98, 4.8x, 389 sessions), 16 Oct 2026 Rs30.50. ## How the move unfolded

Rs6.97 on 28 Nov. 8 Dec Rs7.20 (+4% on 486k shares), 9 Dec Rs7.56, 10 Dec Rs8.56 (+13%, Rs34m), 11 Dec Rs9.55 (+12%, 16.9m shares, Rs161m), 17 Dec Rs10.71 (first 252-high, 73% of the log gain ahead), then four consecutive 10% closes from 1 Jan to Rs15.74 on 6 Jan, the day of the exchange query. First leg high: Rs15.74. Then 51% down: Rs12.57 on 2 Feb, Rs9.58 on 24 Feb, Rs7.75 on 30 Mar. Second leg: 9 Apr Rs9.89 (+8%, 8.9 times volume), Rs12.78 on 30 Apr, Rs17.05 on 22 May, Rs19.23 on 4 Jun, Rs25.04 on 22 Jun (two sessions before the filing), Rs25.33 on 24 Jun, a dip to Rs21.50 on 30 Jun, Rs28.64 on 31 Jul, Rs33.98 on 7 Sep.

Recognition points: 10 to 11 Dec 2025 (volume and a first lock, 3.6x ahead from Rs9.55), 17 Dec (3.2x), 9 Apr 2026 (3.4x from Rs9.89), 24 Jun (1.34x).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

First reasonable entry: 11 Dec 2025 at Rs9.55 (Rs34m to 161m a day, enough for Rs100,000 to Rs200,000). A 20% trailing stop sold on 2 Feb at Rs12.57 (1.32x); a 30% stop on 16 Feb at 1.13x. Holding through gave 3.19x at 16 Oct but a 0.81x trough on 30 Mar. Re-entry on the 9 Apr volume day at Rs9.89 was the second chance, and for a KMI-only investor it ended on 4 Jun (Rs19.23, 1.94x) because of the index deletion. Stop plus re-entry plus forced exit: 1.32 x 1.94 = 2.6x.

## The trap

A thin-float spike looks the same from outside whether it is the start of a 5x or the end: February 2025 (a spike that died) and the 6 Jan top (four locks, then minus 51%) both looked like this. The takeover filing was 82% of the gain late and named nobody. Twins from the 2026 pilot: JVDC (15% of the gain left by day 20) and CRTM (fell back to Rs0.2m a day).

## Questions that would have caught it

B9 (float and exit, tier thin: size to Rs2m or less of a day's value and expect gaps), B10 (the KMI deletion decided the exit date), C7 (volume with locks: the exchange itself asked), C3 and C6 (who decides: an unnamed group), C9 (stop width), D2 (TPLT is missing from the daily table, so the engine could not say anything).

## The if-then rule

If a thin KMI name prints three limit closes in ten sessions on ten times volume plus a management change, take a small position the next session, size it to a tenth of a day's value, use a 30% stop and re-enter on the next volume day; treat an unnamed "interest in a majority stake" as a reason to sell half, and treat a KMI deletion date as a hard exit.

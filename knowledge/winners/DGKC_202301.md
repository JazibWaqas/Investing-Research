## Dates and regime

Policy rate 17% at the start, 22% in mid-2023, 11% at the peak. KMI All Share +248%. The library's eight-peer cement basket rose 425% from this start; DGKC is a member of a wave, not a leader.

## How the move unfolded

Nine months of ranges around 1.0x to 1.37x, with a 28% fall from July to September 2023. The 252-day high came on 2 Nov 2023 (1.41x, Rs57), 2x on 12 Dec (Rs82.4), then four pullbacks of 18% to 23% (Dec 2023, Jan to Mar 2024, Jun to Sep 2024, 16 Dec 2024). 3x on 26 Feb 2025 (Rs123), 4x on 11 Jun (Rs163), 5x on 29 Aug (Rs207), peak Rs271.40 on 26 Sep 2025 after a +10% day. The quarter that set the peak was the best in the table (EPS 7.88 in June 2025). 13 upper locks, none of them the story.

After the peak: 0.91x at 10 sessions, 0.82x at 40, 0.56x at 120, 0.71x at 250.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 3 Nov 2023 (first 252-day high, Rs57.3): 4.7x ahead. A 20% leash sold on 14 Feb 2024 at +15%; a 30% leash on 2 Mar 2026 at Rs184.7, +225%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs323,000; 40% on 16 Mar 2026 at +173%.
- 13 Dec 2023 (2x, Rs80): +133% (30% leash). 27 Feb 2025 (3x, Rs123): +54% (30%) or +79% (20%, 5 Nov 2025).
- Sector context lowered the risk of the idea: the same entry in LUCK, FCCL and PIOC (2022) worked on 20 to 30% leashes. Rs200,000 was under 0.1% of a day's turnover.

## The trap

Buying the stock for the sector rather than the earnings: a quarter of -13.33 and another of -3.95 sat inside a 6.7x run. And the 20% leash: three pullbacks of 18% to 23% would have stopped it out repeatedly. Failure twins: cement names that lagged the wave (the library's peers in the 1.1x to 3x range).

## Questions that would have caught it

A1 (rate direction), B2 (sector levers: coal, retail price per bag), B1 (one-offs: the June quarter), C4 (confirmation), C10 (counting one episode as many: nine cement runs in this library are one cycle).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If the cement basket makes a joint 252-day high after the first rate cut of a cycle, buy the liquid names with the steadiest EPS, and hold on a 30% leash through the sector's 20% shakeouts; the choice inside the sector (LUCK, FCCL, DGKC) mattered less than the leash.

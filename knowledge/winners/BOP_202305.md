# Winner: BOP, 2023 to 2026

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: price and volume panels, `data/announcements.parquet`, PSX document 244309 (10 Dec 2024 reply, read), Business Recorder and ProPakistani on the 2024 results, S&P Global through Arab News and Business Recorder on 2025 returns (via search). No BOP rows in `quarterly_eps.csv`. Not KMI.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 21% (Apr 2023) to 22%, cuts from 10 Jun 2024 to 11% (May 2025). KMI All Share 3.56x, KSE-100 4.41x over the run; sector, from the same dates: NBP 11.9x, UBL 7.5x, AKBL 7.0x, MEBL 6.5x, BAHL 4.8x, BIPL 2.5x.

## What was knowable and when

- 17 Jul 2023: +18% on 49m shares (the pack's biggest early day); the text of the Jun 2023 "miscellaneous information" filings (8, 9, 19, 21 Jun) is unverified.
- 26 Dec 2023: PSX unusual-movement clarification, the same day the price fell 13.7%. 22 Mar 2024: material information, content unverified; 20 Mar 2024 -14.0%.
- 17 Feb 2025: 2024 results: pre-tax profit Rs24.56bn, a record, and a Rs1.8 dividend (ProPakistani via search, asserted).
- 27 and 28 Nov 2024: +14.6% and +12.7% on 115m and 179m shares, then +11.0% on 5 Dec; PSX query 6 Dec and the bank's reply on 10 Dec: "not aware of any material information or any development". Cause unverified.
- 2025: S&P Global ranked BOP first among Asia-Pacific banks over $100m at +333.8% total return, with a +176.4% third quarter (asserted). 12 Jan 2026: BOP rebutted news of a cyberattack; 9 Jan, material information of unknown content.

## How the move unfolded

From Rs3.77 the price stayed between Rs3.4 and Rs4.4 until Nov 2023, then a new 252-day high on 28 Nov (1.45x the start; bought next close at Rs5.61), Rs5.42 on 30 Nov and Rs6.97 on 14 Dec. A 39% fall (total-return series) to Rs4.64 on 12 Jun 2024, and eleven months under the Dec 2023 high until 27 Nov 2024. Then the news-less spike from Rs6.75 on 22 Nov to Rs10.84 on 6 Dec 2024, a 35% pullback from Mar 2025 recovered by 24 Jul 2025 at Rs13.60 and a vertical stretch to Rs34.77 on 30 Oct 2025; the final peak was Rs43.09 on 5 Jan 2026. After it: -10% in 20 sessions, -44% by the March 2026 low, Rs28.76 on 16 Oct 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

The 28 Nov 2023 signal (bought Rs5.61) with a 25% or 30% stop is stopped on 20 Mar 2024 at Rs5.28: -6%. Re-entry on the 27 Nov 2024 spike (Rs8.85) with a 25% stop exits on 23 Apr 2025 at Rs10.04: +13%. The profitable entry was the Jul 2025 recovery: 24 Jul 2025 at Rs13.60, exit 23 Feb 2026 at Rs30.19: 2.22x, about Rs121,000 net on Rs100,000 (7,353 shares). The 30 Oct 2025 entry (Rs34.77) lost 14% by the same stop. Liquidity: Rs100,000 against 10m to 31m shares a day, no problem.

Late joining paid well only after the stock had already tripled from the start, which shows how little the library's "start" matters to a reader: the money in BOP was made in the last 15 months, mostly in Jul to Dec 2025.

## The trap

An eleven-month fall that looks like the end of the story, and news-less spikes that look like operator moves. This is the bank-sector wave seen from a weaker, smaller bank with a state owner.

## Which questions in QUESTIONS.md would have caught it

A1 (rates), B1, B5 (who runs it; the CEO's tenure was extended 10 Apr 2025), C4, C9, D1.

## The if-then rule

If a bank is on a new 252-day high after a 30%-plus drawdown in a falling-rate regime and the sector is up, enter at the next close with a 25% stop and add nothing on the news-less spike days; a reply of "no information" to a PSX query is neutral.

# Policy and sector waves: what eight cases share

Written 2026-10-18 from the eight files in this folder. n = 8, hand-picked winners; the denominator (about three in four first 252-highs do not run, `tests/winners_population.md`) is not in them. Exits were chosen after the paths and are untested.

**Shared signature.** All eight started inside a market regime: the 2019 IMF low (TRG, SYS), the 2023 Stand-By and cut cycle (PIOC, MEBL, MARI, PSO, FFC), a 5.75% policy rate (ISL). The winner beat the KMI All Share by about 2x to its peak (MEBL 7.65x against 3.4x, PIOC 7.7x against 3.3x, FFC 9.5x against 3.6x, ISL 2.5x against 1.2x), and the wave carried peers: all 16 cement names made 2.7x or more. The tide is the first answer; the case is which name carried it furthest. A dated name-specific cause existed in four (ISL's tariff ruling, FFC's gas lever and doubled EPS, MARI's discovery and bonus, PIOC's takeover) and was weak or absent in TRG, SYS and PSO.

**Recognition.** In the five 2023 cases the first new 252-session high came 76 to 138 sessions after the start, at 1.06x to 1.45x, with 75% to 97% of the log gain still ahead. In the 2019 cases the first high (TRG 1.97x) was followed by the Covid crash; the re-breakout at about 2x in June 2020 was the better signal.

**Late entry.** At about 3x of the start, six of seven cases still paid (1.2x to 4.9x on a sensible exit; PSO about 1.0x). At 4x to 5x, 0.8x to 3.5x and mostly under 2x. At 7x to 10x, under 1.2x everywhere (TRG at 10x: 0.4x held). It stops paying when the last leg is on, not at a fixed multiple.

**Exits that kept most of the move.** A trail sized to the name's own worst dip: SYS 16%, MARI and FFC 15%, MEBL 20%, PIOC 23% (a 25% trail held through all five), PSO and TRG 33% to 35% (a 40% trail needed). An event exit: the signed deal (PIOC, 18 Dec 2025, 6% under the peak), directors' sales (ISL), the third limit-down close (MARI). A 100-day-average rule worked for TRG (5.1x) and left MEBL, FFC and PIOC at 2.2x to 4.3x, far below their peaks.

**Failures.** ISL (an 82-session policy leg, a double top, a 31% fall); MARI (a 95% blow-off in 16 sessions); TRG (an unlisted asset, then governance); PSO (a 35% shake-out at its first 2x). And eligibility: TRG never KMI, FFC not until December 2025, SYS out for six months in the middle of its run.

**One rule?** No. The cases share a tide, a confirmed breakout and a wide exit; the ending differs each time. One rule per case is in each file.

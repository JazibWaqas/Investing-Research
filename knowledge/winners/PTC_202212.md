Ten months flat below 1.1x. Leg one: 1.85x in Dec 2023 (2x on 15 Dec at Rs14, 10% days on 4 and 8 Dec), a 39% pullback into February, a spike to 2.8x in March 2024 and a 40% fall to June. Leg two, from 2.2x in October 2024 to 4.1x in December: 3x on 29 Nov (Rs20.3), 4x on 11 Dec (Rs27.4), Rs354m a day. A 38% fall to the market low of 8 May 2025, recovery by 29 Sep. Leg three: Rs1.4bn of turnover in October 2025, 5x on 8 Oct (Rs33.4), 7x on 8 Dec (Rs49.6), 10x on 13 Jan 2026 (Rs69.7). A 33% fall to 30 Mar 2026, then the high on 3 Jul 2026 (Rs74.01). 42 upper locks.

After the peak: 0.96x, 0.97x, 0.93x at 10, 20 and 40 sessions; 0.72x on 16 Oct 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- 24 Nov 2023 (first 252-day high, Rs7.27, 1.10x): 10.2x ahead on paper, but a 20% leash sold on 28 Dec at +64%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs163,000, and a 40% leash sold on 27 Jun 2024 at +51%. The 40% pullbacks of Feb and June 2024 made the long hold worth only +51%.
- 2 Dec 2024 (3x, Rs20.7, after the Telenor gate): a 20% leash +9% (exit 4 Feb 2025), a 30% leash -6%, a 40% leash still holding at Rs53.6, +159%, about Rs258,000, after a 38% drawdown.
- The gates were public: each approval step lifted the stock, and each gap after one was fast. Rs200,000 was under 0.1% of a day's turnover after 2023.

## The trap

Chasing a gate. The stock rose 40% in two weeks in October 2024 on a hearing, before any approval; entering after that at 3x on 2 Dec 2024 meant a 38% drawdown to May 2025 on any leash that would hold. Even the approval of 1 Oct 2025 was followed by a +31% month, and the high came two days after a 1 Jul 2026 material information filing whose text I did not read. Also the first leg: a 252-day high at Rs7 in a 22% rate environment, where anything holding cash flow for 10 months looks like a trade. Failure twins: other telecom and deal-anticipation names whose deal stalled; PTC's own Dec 2023 run was not explained.

## Questions that would have caught it

C1 (type: deal), C3 (who decides and when: the Competition Commission, a dated Phase II), C2 (completed deals: `tests/corporate_events.md`), B6 (funding: a $400m financing plan), C6 (what the deal buys), C9.

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a regulator-gated acquisition has a dated hearing and the price is below the level reached on the last gate, buy after the next gate passes and hold with a 40% leash; here every gate (the hearing, the approval of 1 Oct 2025, the shareholder vote) was followed by a further leg, so selling on the news would have cut the gain, and the stop did the exit.

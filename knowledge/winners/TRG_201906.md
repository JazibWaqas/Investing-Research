# Winner: TRG Pakistan, June 2019 to May 2021

Written 2026-10-18. Type: technology wave with the August 2019 market low and the March 2020 crash inside it. Sources: `seed/data/prices/TRG.csv` (no corporate action; no dividends of note, so price and total return agree), `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/macro/`. News is from search snippets (Profit, ProPakistani, Aaj), marked asserted. TRG is in no KMI list, so it was never open to a KMI-only investor; it is here as the largest liquid winner of the wave.

## Dates and regime

- 10 Jun 2019: policy rate 12.25% (13.25% from 16 Jul), CPI 8.9%, 3-month bill 12.7%, KSE 34,568. IMF Board 3 Jul; KSE low 28,765 on 16 Aug (case 01).
- 25 Mar 2020: Covid low, KSE down 37% from 13 Jan. Rate 7.0% by 25 Jun 2020 (case 02).
- 18 May 2021: peak. Rate 7.0%, CPI 10.8%, bill 7.3%, KSE 45,982.

## What was knowable, and when

1. The tape said almost nothing: no filing in the 20 sessions before the start. Bare "Material Information" filings on 21 May, 30 Jul and 7 Aug 2020 (docs 148030, 150459, 150703), unread. 7 Aug matches the IBEX Nasdaq listing, a TRG International holding (my recollection; unverified).
2. Profit (asserted): about Rs12 in April 2020, Rs51 by August; insider-trading complaints went to the PM's portal and the SECP asked PSX for a report.
3. 1 Mar 2021 (ProPakistani, asserted): H1 FY21 profit Rs4.4bn, EPS 8.1 against 0.34. The price had already made 9.9x of the start.
4. Nov 2021 (Aaj, asserted): allegations against Zia Chishti of Afiniti, reported as 57% of TRG's value; five limit-down closes, 17 to 23 Nov.

## How the move unfolded

The first sign, a four-session pop from 14.32 to 17.7 on 10 to 14 Jun 2019, failed: 35% down by 29 Jul, then a flat base through October. The second sign was volume: monthly median traded value rose from Rs58m (Jul 2019) to Rs215m (Nov) and Rs307m (Jan 2020). The first 252-session high came on 3 Jan 2020 at 1.97x, with 73% of the log gain ahead. Then Covid: 2.0x on 17 Jan to 0.83x on 26 Mar, 59% down against 37% for the market.

The third sign was the recovery to a new 252-high on 1 Jul 2020 at 2.09x. July added 68%, August reached 4.1x, then a 33% pullback to 2.96x on 6 Oct, recovered by 30 Nov. From December to February it went 4.7x to 9.9x on 7.5% limit closes; median value hit Rs4.9bn a day in March 2021, 75 times the start. March to May brought pullbacks of 19%, 24% and 14%. Peak 12.72x (Rs182.17) on 18 May 2021.

It ended in two steps: below its 100-day average on 9 Aug 2021 at 5.1x, then the November allegations, 5.44x on 3 Dec. It was 3.94x on 16 Oct 2026.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Recognition points, with the log share of the gain still ahead: first 252-high 3 Jan 2020 (1.97x), 73%; re-breakout 1 Jul 2020 (2.09x), 71%; 3x on 28 Jul, 56%; 5x on 4 Dec, 36%; 10x on 22 Feb 2021, 9%.

- First reasonable entry: 1 Jul 2020, a new high three months after a crash, June median value Rs241m a day.
- To stay through pullbacks: the 33% fall of Aug to Oct 2020 breaks any stop under 35%.
- Reasonable exit: a close more than 5% under the 100-day average (9 Aug 2021), or a 35% trail (18 Nov 2021).
- Rs100,000 from 1 Jul 2020: Rs507,000 on the average rule, Rs372,000 on the 35% trail, Rs156,000 on a 25% trail (stopped 2 Oct 2020), Rs188,000 held to 16 Oct 2026. Rs50,000 or Rs200,000 scale directly; costs of 0.5 to 1% a side are not deducted. I picked the exit rule after seeing the path: one case, not a test.
- Joining at 3.09x (28 Jul) made Rs344,000 or Rs252,000 on those exits; at 5.07x (4 Dec), Rs210,000 at best; at 10.2x (22 Feb 2021), under Rs104,000 on every rule. Joining on the June 2019 pop or 3 Jan 2020 with any stop under 40% lost 21 to 42%.

## The trap

The early pops and breakouts that a normal stop sells before the real leg; and the end, value in an unlisted asset run by one man, with insider-trading complaints public by August 2020. Twins in the same sector list: STL stood at 0.33x of its 1 Jul 2020 price on 16 Oct 2026; NETSOL made 2.75x by May 2021 and then fell 44%.

## Questions that would have caught it

- C4: confirmation (1 Jul 2020, new high on volume) against extension (above 5x).
- C9: an exit written before entry and wide enough for a 33% pullback.
- B5, B6: who runs it, and what the holding is worth.
- B10: not in KMI.

## The if-then rule

If a liquid name makes a new 252-session high within three months of a 50% crash, on volume several times its base, join it with a close-5%-under-the-100-day-average exit rather than a 20 to 25% stop, and stop adding above 5x. If one holder or one unlisted asset carries the value, size it as a trade and sell on any governance headline.

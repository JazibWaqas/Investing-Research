# Winner: Pioneer Cement, April 2019 to February 2021 (5.4x)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/PIOC.csv` (quoted prices ran Rs26.2 to Rs142.2; the library uses adjusted prices), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/macro/`. News: Profit (Pakistan Today) market reports of 13 Jul and 5 Aug 2020 and a Business Recorder weekly review of the construction package, found by web search 18 Oct 2026 (snippets only; the exact date of the package announcement was not in the snippet). KMI member at the start and today.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 10.75% at the start, 13.25% from July 2019, then 7.0% by 25 Jun 2020. KMI All Share 17,774 (18 Apr 2019), 13,628 (30 Aug 2019, the low), 12,972 (25 Mar 2020), 23,245 at the peak (+31% on the start, +79% on the Aug 2019 low). The sector flag in the library: nine cement peers up 90% over the same span, so PIOC was not special. The library start sits above a lower low: the stock was 0.69x the start in July 2019.

## What was knowable, and when

- 13 Jun 2019: a filing on the acquisition of Galadari Cement (Gulf) Limited. 3 Oct 2019: FY2019 results.
- 15 and 21 Jan 2020: two material information filings (bare titles). 27 Feb 2020: Q2 results.
- Mar to Apr 2020: the Covid crash and recovery. Per Profit and Business Recorder, analysts tied the cement rally to a construction package and a housing scheme (April 2020 onwards, date not confirmed), and in July to rising retail cement prices and falling coal prices; on 5 Aug 2020 cement price-increase reports sent several cement stocks to the upper circuit (KSE-100 +305 points).
- 30 Sep 2020 FY2020 results, 7 Oct and 26 Nov 2020 material information. 16 Mar 2021 material information, one month after the peak.

## How the move unfolded

Nothing for seven months, in fact a 37% fall (29 Apr to 8 Aug 2019). A recovery in November 2019 (1.25x), a stall to 1.1x in February 2020, and the first 252-day high on 10 Mar 2020 at Rs41.4 (1.58x), one week before the crash: -48% from 11 to 26 Mar. Then the real leg: 2x on 22 Apr (Rs52.6), 2.4x in June, 3x on 4 Aug (Rs79.7) on turnover of Rs300m a day, a 20% pause from 4 Sep to 23 Nov, 4x on 13 Jan 2021 (Rs111), 5x on 10 Feb (Rs136), peak Rs142.21 on 16 Feb 2021 on Rs236m a day. 55 upper-lock closes in all. Several cement peers made the same path at the same time.

After the peak the price drifted: 0.94x at 10 sessions, 0.88x at 60, 0.82x at 120, 0.59x at 250.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, dividends included, 0.5% round trip, before tax. A Rs200,000 order was well under 1% of a day's turnover from April 2020.

- 10 Mar 2020 (first 252-day high, Rs41.4): 3.2x still ahead, but the crash took -24% by the 20% stop on 18 Mar (Rs100,000 became about Rs81,000). The sensible entry lost again.
- 23 Apr 2020 (2x, Rs53.6): 2.65x ahead. A 20% stop sold on 23 Nov 2020 at Rs83.8, +56%; a 30% stop sold on 16 Sep 2021 at Rs99.2, +85%, about Rs184,000. Worst close against entry -3%.
- 5 Aug 2020 (3.3x): +16% with a 30% stop; a 20% stop broke even. By then 1.66x was ahead.
- The exits worked on a 30% leash because the fall from the peak was slow; the market (KMI) still rose 8% in the six months after the peak.

## The trap

Entering at the first 1-year high in early March 2020; and believing the sector theme was PIOC's own: when nine peers rise 90% the stock is a sector bet, and in a sector bet the stock-specific stop is triggered by the sector's wobble (the 20% dip of Sep to Nov 2020). Twins: the other cement names of 2019 to 2021 that rose less (the library's 9 peers), and the same sector in 2023 to 2025 (PIOC_202212, FCCL_202304, DGKC_202301).

## Questions that would have caught it

A1 (rate regime: first cut and the lowest rate in years), B2 (sector levers: cement price per bag, coal), C3 (who decides: the government package), C4 (confirmation after the crash, not before), C9 (loss in rupees, with a leash).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a whole sector (here nine cement names) breaks higher after a policy backstop and the stock is 2x off its low after a crash, join with a 30% leash from entry; if the only signal is a 1-year high immediately before a macro risk, wait.

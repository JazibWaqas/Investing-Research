# Winner: PIAHCLA, 2022 to 2025

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/panel_close.parquet` (the series runs across the May 2024 conversion; I take it as continuous on the Profit account that holders received PIAHCL shares in proportion, not verified at source), `data/announcements.parquet`, SECP order on PSX document 267378 (17 Dec 2025, text read), PSX documents 267176 and 267741 (read as images), Profit 3 Jun 2024 (opened), The News, Dawn, ProPakistani (search). Non-KMI, so not buyable under the mandate.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 22% until Jun 2024, then 13% in Dec 2024, 11% in May 2025, 10.5% from 15 Dec 2025. KMI All Share +0.2% on 22 to 25 Sep 2023, +0.5% on 13 to 18 Dec 2023, +4.9% from 17 Nov to 9 Dec 2025. The company: a loss-making state airline (negative equity is asserted, not checked).

## What was knowable and when

- 22 Sep 2023: the Federal Government's S.R.O. 1351(1)/2023 allowed schemes of arrangement for state companies (cited in the SECP order). The next session, 25 Sep, the price closed +22.8% on 29.7m shares, a new 252-day high (the link to the S.R.O. is my inference; no filing says so).
- 14 Dec 2023: privatisation announced; +50% over days, upper limits daily (Profit).
- 19 Feb 2024: cabinet approved the scheme; 3 May: SECP sanction; 25 May: PIA delisted, PIAHCL listed (Profit, ch-aviation via search).
- Dec 2025: PSX unusual-movement query 11 Dec, reply 12 Dec "no details"; 17 Dec SECP order sanctioning the demerger of the Precision Engineering Complex; Fauji Fertiliser out, three bidders 22 Dec; 23 Dec bids opened: Arif Habib consortium Rs135bn for 75% against Lucky Cement's Rs134bn and a Rs100bn reserve. PIAHCL's filing of 24 Dec: Rs10.125bn (7.5%) to the government through PIAHCL, Rs124.875bn into PIACL by a rights issue.

## How the move unfolded

2023: Rs3.2 to Rs4.1 until Sep, then Rs5.54 on 26 Sep, Rs8.12 on 21 Dec, a 12.9% fall on 26 Dec. Jan 2024 Rs12.07; Mar 2024 Rs27.61 on 21 Mar, Rs31.54 on 29 Mar (+156% from Rs10.8). From 16 to 23 May a 30% fall as the book closure and a compressed futures expiry forced selling (Profit). The 12 Jun low Rs12.05, 62% under the March high, then Rs25.08 on 10 Jul. Sixteen months at Rs15 to Rs25 followed, with a spike to Rs22.38 on 13 Jun 2025. From Rs24.8 in early Nov 2025 the price climbed to Rs47.63 on 9 Dec on 20m to 60m shares a day. After the peak: -9.8% on 22 Dec, -5.2% on 23 Dec (bid day), -9.2% and -10.0% on 24 and 26 Dec, Rs28.90 on 29 Dec. On 16 Oct 2026, Rs22.33.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Entry 26 Sep 2023 (Rs5.54): a 25% stop sells at the 26 Dec flush at Rs6.77 (1.22x); a 30% stop holds to 21 May 2024 at Rs20.80 (3.75x, about Rs274,000 net on Rs100,000), through a 34% drawdown. Entry 19 Jan 2024 (Rs12.07) with a 30% stop made 1.72x. Entry 15 Dec 2023, on the announcement, lost 26% at the 26 Dec fall. Entry 19 Mar 2024 (Rs25.68) lost 19%. Entry 17 Nov 2025 made 1.16x on a 25% stop that sold on 24 Dec at Rs34.16. Rs100,000 was about 3,000 to 18,000 shares against millions a day: liquidity never mattered.

The wide stop decided everything: 1.2x or 3.7x from the same entry. Late joining paid up to about Jan 2024, then it was a coin flip.

## The trap

The announcement is the top of each leg: the 14 Dec 2023 buyer lost 26% in two weeks; the 23 Dec 2025 bid-day buyer (Rs37.62) lost 23% in three sessions. The futures rollover in May 2024 shows a mechanical risk (book closure, expiry). The library mislabels this as an operator run because the filings were titled "other".

## Which questions in QUESTIONS.md would have caught it

## The if-then rule

If a state asset sale has a published procedural sequence, buy the first confirmed step that is not yet in the price and hold it with a stop of 30%. Sell into the decisive step (bid or approval date) rather than after it: the price fell a further 23% in the three sessions after the Rs135bn bid, 39% from the peak, and PIAHCL's own share of the proceeds was only Rs10.125bn.

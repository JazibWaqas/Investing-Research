# Winner PIOC, from December 2022: the wave, the takeover, and a 49% fall after the top

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, price panels (raw closes, no split in the window), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/deals_history.csv`, `knowledge/playbooks/deals.md` (the pro-rata worked example, PSX docs 267426, 270524, as cited there), PSX document 267429 (read in part), ProPakistani 18 Dec 2025 (via web search; asserted). It is the target twin of MLCF_202404.

## Dates and regime

Library start 5 Dec 2022 (Rs57.5), peak 15 Dec 2025 (Rs421.37, 7.7x, 750 sessions), Rs231.9 on 16 Oct 2026. Tier H (Rs76m a day), KMI member. Policy rate 16% to 22% and back to 10.5% on 15 Dec 2025, the peak day. Maximum drawdown inside the run 23%, then 49% after the peak. The first 700 sessions are the cement wave (thirteen cement names made a median 4.6x from Jan 2023 to Dec 2025); the last five weeks are a deal.

## What was knowable, and when

- 13 Nov 2025: Maple Leaf (MLCF) announces its intention to take control (`deals_history.csv`; MLCF material information the same day). Pioneer closed Rs221.98 the day before.
- 18 Dec 2025, 12:52 and 13:09: MLCF's public offer for 26,623,096 shares (11.72%) and control (doc 267429), and a Pioneer material-information filing. Price Rs478.43, alongside an agreement for 131.82m shares (58.03%) (ProPakistani, asserted). Whether the price was public on 13 Nov: not checked.
- 8 to 14 Feb 2026: acceptance window. 17 Feb: payment; 61.64% of tendered shares were accepted (doc 270524, via deals.md).

## How the move unfolded

Phase one: Rs86.6 on 30 Jun 2023, Rs114.9 at year-end, Rs168.7 mid-2024, Rs201 at year-end, Rs228 in June 2025, Rs210 to 220 in the weeks before the news. Phase two: 13 Nov 2025 +10% to Rs244.18 on 6.4m shares (Rs1.6bn), +8.7% on 17 Nov, Rs340.5 on 25 Nov, Rs372 on 1 Dec, Rs398.5 on 9 Dec, Rs421.37 on 15 Dec. The price then did something the deal arithmetic should have predicted: 18 Dec, the offer day, closed Rs396.45 (minus 4.7% on 16.4m shares, Rs6.5bn). Rs384 on 22 Dec, Rs354.5 on 11 Feb, Rs276 on 19 Feb after the payment, Rs216 on 9 Mar. 1 month after completion Rs229 (deals_history).

Recognition points: 13 Nov (a takeover with a named buyer; price Rs244, 1.73x ahead to the peak), 17 Nov (Rs292, 1.44x), 1 Dec (Rs372, 1.13x), the offer day (nothing ahead).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Phase one: first entry at the first 252-high, 3 May 2023, Rs79.47: a 20% trailing stop sold on 26 Nov 2024 at 2.17x; a 30% stop on 19 Feb 2026 at 3.47x (Rs347,000). That paid at every date to the end of 2024.

The reasonable exit was to sell the whole position in the market in the first days of the offer (Rs384 to 397) or to tender and sell the unfilled 38% immediately. Joining after 1 Dec had nothing ahead after costs.

## The trap

Convergence arithmetic. The offer was 13.5% above the peak but only 61.64% of tendered shares were bought; the unbought 38% was a minority stub in a company with a 76% to 88% owner, and it fell 49% from the top in three months, helped by the March 2026 shock. The headline spread (Rs478 against Rs416, 15%) was the trap that deals.md already records for the 17 Dec buyer: +1.4%. Failure twins: EPCL 2026 (situation 11, down 17% from the pre-news peak), KEL 2022 (see KEL_202206), and the acquirer MLCF, which topped three days before the offer document.

## Questions that would have caught it

C6 (what fraction of my holding does the offer buy: 61.64%, or about 50% to 53% in the EPCL case), C2 (n = 11 takeovers: median price one month after completion 13% below the offer), C3 (who gains: Maple, at 88% ownership), C4 (extension: 90% in five weeks), C9 (exit date: the offer window), A1 (cut on the peak day).

## The if-then rule

If a KMI name has been in a sector wave for years and a named buyer announces control, buy only on the first day or two, compute the blended value (offer price times the likely fill plus a stub price 13% to 31% below the offer for the rest), and sell the whole position, tendered or not, before the payment date; if the blended value is not above the current price by 15%, do not buy.

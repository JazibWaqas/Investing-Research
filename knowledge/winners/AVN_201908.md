# Winner: Avanceon, August 2019 to September 2021 (5.5x)

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, `data/prices_raw/AVN.csv` (quoted prices Rs35.04 to Rs139.79; the library series is adjusted for the 2020 and 2021 bonus issues), `data/announcements.parquet`, `data/macro/`. News (web search, snippets): Profit on Avanceon's 2019 slide and a 6 Aug 2020 market report; Business Recorder on a planned Octopus Digital IPO, date not retrieved. Not a KMI name at the start; it is today.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 13.25% at the start, 7.0% from Jun 2020. KMI All Share +61% over the run (14,276 to 23,037). The tech basket had two peers in the library. Per Profit the stock fell from Rs80.61 to Rs49.04 in the first half of 2019 and to Rs31.66 by September: the start is the bottom of a 60% slide.

## What was knowable, and when

- A huge volume of "material information" filings: 62 in the window, 56 inside the run, often several a week (for example 24 Sep, 30 Sep, 31 Oct, 21 Nov, 27 Nov, 13 Dec, 18 Dec 2019). Their titles are bare; the text was not read.
- 28 Jun 2019 and 30 Jul 2019: shares issued under employee stock option schemes. 27 Apr 2020: board resolution for bonus shares; credited 23 Jun. 21 Apr 2021: bonus again, credited 18 Jun.
- 2 Sep 2020 and 26 Aug 2021: investor briefings; 6 Aug 2020 report of a DHA Lahore contract of Rs30m.
- A plan to list a subsidiary, Octopus Digital: the PSX and SECP approved the listing application (Business Recorder, date not retrieved).
- 25 Jan 2021: ESOP capital enhancement.

## How the move unfolded

The first signal was turnover, not price. In Oct 2019 turnover went from Rs3m to Rs19m a day, to Rs70m in November and Rs134m in December; price 1.15x. 1.25x on 16 Jan 2020 (Rs45), then -60% to 25 Mar. The old high came back on 7 Jul 2020 (1.5x, Rs47). 2x on 26 Aug 2020, 3x on 21 Dec (Rs96, Rs776m a day), a 32% pullback to 11 Mar 2021, then the second leg: 4x on 16 Jul (Rs103), 5x on 24 Aug (Rs128), peak Rs139.79 on 9 Sep 2021. 47 upper-lock closes.

After the peak: 0.89x at 10 sessions, 0.80x at 40, 0.60x at 60, then a long range at 0.58x to 0.68x.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Closing prices, 0.5% round trip, before tax.

- Recognition: 9 to 10 Jul 2020, a first 252-day high at 1.54x (Rs47.0) with turnover Rs162m a day (40x the 2019 base): 3.55x of the peak ahead.
- A 20% leash sold on 2 Nov 2020 at +25%; a 30% leash on 11 Mar 2021 at +57%, Rs100,000 becoming about Rs156,000; a 40% leash on 13 Dec 2021 at Rs80.45, +105%, about Rs204,000, after a 42% drawdown from the running high.
- 26 Aug 2020 (2x): -6% (20% leash) to +54% (40% leash). 21 Dec 2020 (3x, Rs96): -23% on a 30% leash; this late entry bought into the March 2021 break.
- Waiting for the Covid dip to end was the entry; the pre-Covid 1.25x of January 2020 was not.

## The trap

Disclosure volume read as news. Fifty-six "material information" filings in 24 months told a reader nothing; a model that scores filings by count would have been fooled. Entering at 3x in December 2020 with Rs776m a day of turnover was entering the top of the first leg. Failure twins: other small caps with ESOP and bonus noise and no earnings trend (the library's thin list).

## Questions that would have caught it

B3 (what the business earns on new capital), B6 (ESOP issuance and bonus pattern), C4 (price and turnover confirming), C10 (filing counts as signal), C9 (exit, with a leash of 30 to 40% for a stock that fell 60% in a month).

## The if-then rule this case suggests

If a small tech name falling for a year starts to trade 10x normal turnover and then closes above its 252-day high after a market-wide crash, buy a starter and hold with a 40% leash; do not add after 3x, and do not read filing counts as a catalyst.

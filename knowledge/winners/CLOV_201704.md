# Winner CLOV, from April 2017: the control sale that was sold, and the run that came after

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, price panels (raw closes used; prices after the Oct 2018 scheme are unreliable and ignored), `data/announcements.parquet`, Profit (14 Nov 2020, "After a lucky streak Clover stumbles in 2020", via web search) and a web search on the 2018 scheme. Prices are raw PSX closes. CLOV is not a KMI member, so it fails the investor's rule; it is kept because it is the cleanest case of a control sale.

## Dates and regime

Library start 11 Apr 2017 (Rs50.29), peak 10 Aug 2018 (Rs258.23), 5.1x in 331 sessions, 73 limit closes. Daily limit 5%. Policy rate 5.75% until the first hike on 29 Jan 2018, then 10% by Nov 2018. KMI All Share fell 10.3% from 13 Jun 2017 to 10 Aug 2018 (situation 12): CLOV rose 4x in a falling index. Median traded value Rs2.2m before the start (tier L), Rs5 to 40m a day during the run.

## What was knowable, and when

- 30 May 2017: PSX notice "Price fluctuation in Clover Pakistan Limited".
- 13 Jun 2017, 12:50: "Execution of Share Purchase Agreement for the Acquisition of 55% Voting Shares" (title only; acquirer, price and any open-offer terms unread). Profit (2020) says Fossil Energy took control in 2017 (55%, asserted).
- 15 Dec 2017: change of management. 8 Jan 2018: EOGM notice. 12 Feb 2018: material information plus new chairman, CEO and CFO.
- 2 May 2018: clarification under Rule 5.6.1(b) on price movement. 5 Jun 2018: notice on advertising on false pretence.
- 18 Sep and 8 Oct 2018: material information, EOGM, "Scheme of Arrangement and Swap Computation Certificate". Profit (2020) says the board merged Hascombe Business Solutions into the company, court-approved Feb 2019 (asserted; swap ratio not found).

## How the move unfolded

Pre-news run: Rs55.79 on 17 May to Rs69.58 on 7 Jun, 13 times normal volume on the last day. Then the disclosure, then five lower-limit closes in three weeks: Rs60.54 on 13 Jun, Rs47.88 on 3 Jul, down 31% from the high. It sat at Rs43 to 52 for seven months. The real run started with the management change: Rs75.07 on 7 Feb 2018 (first 252-high, 205 sessions after the library start, 75% of the log gain still ahead), Rs80.49 on 12 Feb, Rs87 by late February, a pause at Rs78 to 85 through March and April, then limit-up days from 2 May (Rs107, Rs148.8 on 25 May, Rs197.85 on 20 Jun). It peaked at Rs258.23 on 10 Aug, held Rs256 on 30 Aug, then fell: Rs213.7 on 17 Sep, Rs131 on 15 Oct (minus 49%). Volume dried up as it fell (0.5m rupees a day on 15 Oct), so the exit door was narrow.

Recognition points: 7 Feb 2018 (first 252-high, 3.4x ahead), the 12 Feb filing (3.2x), and the 4 May limit run (2.2x).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

Not the news: a buyer at the 13 Jun 2017 close (Rs60.54) was down 21% by 3 Jul and, on a 20% stop, out at 0.79x; a 30% stop exited at 0.70x in December. The first reasonable entry was the management-change phase: 12 Feb 2018 at Rs80.49. Staying in: a 20% trailing stop on closes was not hit until 24 Sep 2018. Exit: that stop, at 2.49x of entry (about 78% of the peak multiple); a 30% stop gave 2.19x on 5 Oct.

Rs100,000: 12 Feb 2018 entry to the 20% stop, about Rs249,000; the 4 May entry (Rs118.51) about Rs169,000; the 13 Jun 2017 entry, Rs79,000. Joining after the 20 Jun close (Rs197.85) left at most 1.3x. Limit-up days cannot be bought, so the fill is worse than these closes.

## The trap

The filing. A control-sale disclosure after a 24% run on 13 times volume was an exit for the people who ran it up. Twins: PIOC (a target topping at its own offer) and KEL (a deal chain with no price). The second trap is a thin, limit-bound tape: the price series looks tradable and is not on up-days.

## Questions that would have caught it

C3 (who gains, who decides: new owner, new board), B6 (how the sponsor treats minorities; swap ratio unknown), C7 (volume with locks: here partly demand), C6 (what the offer buys: unread), B9 (float and exit, tier L), B10 (not KMI: out of scope), C9 (a stop).

## The if-then rule

If a control sale is disclosed after the price has already run 20% or more on volume, do not buy the filing: wait for the sell-off to finish and for new management to start (a first 252-high), then buy with a 20% trailing stop. For this investor, only if the name is in the KMI list.

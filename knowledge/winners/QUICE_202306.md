# Winner: QUICE, 2023 to 2026

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/panel_close.parquet`, `panel_volume.parquet`, `data/announcements.parquet`; PSX documents 267580 (22 Dec 2025), 268336 (6 Jan 2026) and 268832 (19 Jan 2026), read as images; `tests/winners_2026_postmortem.md` for the share count (about 109m) and the 2026 merger terms. Prices are Rs, raw equals adjusted (no corporate action). Costs 0.5% a round trip, no capital gains tax deducted.

## Dates and regime

Policy rate 22% through Jun 2024, then cuts to 11% by May 2025 and 10.5% from 15 Dec 2025 (`policy_rate_decisions.csv`). KMI All Share +8.4% from 4 Dec 2025 to 8 Jan 2026, while food peers (UPFL, RMPL, FFL, TREET, SHEZ, NESTLE, UNITY) moved between -2% and +15%. QUICE FY25 revenue was about Rs1.1bn with a loss of Rs11.7m (Simply Wall St via search, unverified at source), so at Rs9 the company was worth roughly Rs1bn and at the peak about Rs4.6bn.

## What was knowable and when

- 5 Dec 2025: volume 11.9m shares against 0.3m to 1.0m on every prior day; price +4%. No filing.
- 18 Dec: PSX wrote to the company about the price (letter Gen-2204). 22 Dec reply: "unaware of any matter"; the rise reflected "a perceived difference in valuation".
- 6 Jan 2026, 10:01: Khanani Securities (a brokerage) had bought 14,570,400 shares on 5 Jan and become a substantial shareholder, about 13% of the shares (ratio from the 109m count, approximate). The close that day was Rs34.60, 3.8x the 4 Dec price.
- 15 Jan: PSX wrote again; the 19 Jan reply credits "market forces" and "the state of National economy".
- 15 May 2026: merger with Indus Fruit Products at 13.66 new shares per IFPL share; EGM approval 23 Jun; Sindh High Court restraint reported 7 Jul (Mettis and Profit, via search, not opened).

## How the move unfolded

The 30 months before the last leg were a grind with two false starts. 17 Nov 2023: +22% on 6m shares, then back to Rs4. 24 and 25 Jul 2024: +22% and +18% on 4m and 13m shares, to Rs8.25 by 5 Aug; PSX query 23 Aug; then -39% to Rs5.05 on 2 Oct. After that the price crawled from Rs5 to Rs9.4 by Oct 2025 on ordinary volume.

The ignition on 5 Dec 2025 was different in size. From Rs9.55 the price closed at the +10% limit on 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 17 and 18 Dec (Rs19.22, volume 6m to 10m a day). It paused for five sessions at Rs17.75 to Rs19.50. From 29 Dec it locked eight days running to Rs41.87 on 8 Jan; volume was only 0.4m to 0.5m on the two locked days (31 Dec, 1 Jan) and then 16m on 2 Jan. Then -9.4%, -9.8%, -4.1%, a rebound to Rs40.87 on 19 Jan, and a slide to Rs21.11 on 9 Mar, a 50% fall. A second rise to Rs38.19 by 9 Jun came with the merger terms.

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

First reasonable entry: the 5 Dec volume day, bought on 8 Dec at Rs10.55 (a fill on a limit day is partial). With a written 20% stop from the running high the exit is 13 Jan at Rs32.80: 3.1x, about Rs209,000 net on Rs100,000, held 25 sessions; 9,479 shares against 7m a day of volume, so the exit was easy. A buyer on 18 Dec (Rs19.22, the day PSX asked) still made 1.8x. A buyer at the 2 Jan volume spike made +4%. A buyer on 15 Jan (Rs38.93) lost 21% at the stop on 29 Jan. A 25% stop from 25 Jul 2024 lost 5%, and from the Nov 2023 spike made 1.18x in 208 sessions.

Late joining paid for about two weeks and not after the disclosure. The 20% stop kept the position through the 19 Dec -5% day and the Christmas pause (worst drawdown 22%).

## The trap

Two earlier ignitions in the same stock had faded. The company's replies were uninformative, the disclosure came after the price had tripled, and the volume on locked days was tiny, so a late order may not fill at the price you see. The twins are QUICE's own Aug 2024 run and CRTM's Jun 2025 plateau.

## The if-then rule

If a KMI name under Rs1m a day of value prints 10x its volume with a +10% close, and the next five closes hold, then a Rs50,000 to Rs100,000 position at the next close is reasonable, with a 20% trailing stop written first and size under one day of volume. Do not add after a substantial-holder disclosure or after the price has doubled again; the engine's analogues for this situation had a median near zero, so this is one position among others, not the core.

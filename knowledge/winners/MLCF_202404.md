# Winner MLCF, from April 2024: the buy-back, the bids, and a top on the deal day

Written 2026-10-18. Sources: `data/winners_library.csv`, price panels, `data/announcements.parquet` (titles), PSX document 267429 (the Pioneer public announcement, text read), `data/deals_history.csv`, `data/macro/`, a ProPakistani report of 18 Dec 2025 on the Pioneer deal terms (via web search; asserted). Prices are raw closes (no split in the window).

## Dates and regime

Library start 2 Apr 2024 (Rs35.01), peak 15 Dec 2025 (Rs130.38, 3.7x, 420 sessions), 16 Oct 2026 Rs89.95 (2.6x). Policy rate 22% on the start date, first cut 10 Jun 2024, 11% by May 2025, 10.5% on 15 Dec 2025 (the peak day), hike to 11.5% on 27 Apr 2026. KMI All Share +115% over the run. Cement peers: median 3.0x over the same dates (range 2.0x to 3.8x); MLCF's 3.7x was near the top. Largest drawdown inside the run 21%; 42% after the peak.

## What was knowable, and when

- 28 Mar 2024: disclosure of acquisition of Agritech shares. 16 to 18 Apr 2024: completion of the purchase period for a buy-back of up to 100m shares and the final report; 25 Apr: CDC letter on cancellation of 25,783,624 shares actually bought.
- 5 Nov 2024: purchase of 2.68m Agritech shares; 11 Nov: public announcement of a competitive bid for Agritech; 24 and 27 Dec: offer letters; 8 Jan 2025: obligations fulfilled. Outcome and price: unverified.
- 13 Nov 2025: public announcement of intention to take control of Pioneer Cement (`deals_history.csv`; Pioneer rose 10% that day). 18 Dec 2025, 12:52: the public offer for 26,623,096 Pioneer shares (11.72%) and control (doc 267429); the price was Rs478.43, alongside an agreement for 58.03% of Pioneer (ProPakistani, asserted).

## How the move unfolded

Apr 2024 to Sep 2024: flat, Rs34 to 38.5, with one 4 times volume day (29 Apr) and a dip to Rs34 on 22 Aug. The first 252-high came on 8 Nov 2024 (145 sessions in, 84% of the log gain ahead), three days before the Agritech bid. Then a staircase: Rs46 in December, Rs53 in March 2025, Rs69 on 21 Apr, Rs75.9 on 13 May (+9% on 41m shares), Rs85 in late June, Rs105 on 1 Sep. The last leg came from 9 Dec 2025: Rs110.93 (+4.7% on 26m shares), Rs122.02 on 10 Dec (+10%), Rs130.38 on 15 Dec, a volume peak of 22m shares. That is three days before the formal offer was posted and five weeks after the intention was already public. Afterwards: Rs118.98 on 19 Dec, Rs101.8 on 23 Feb, Rs84.5 on 3 Mar (the March 2026 shock), Rs73.5 on 31 Mar.

Recognition points: 8 Nov 2024 (252-high; 3.0x ahead), 11 Nov (competitive bid), 23 Apr 2025 (2.0x ahead), 1 Sep 2025 (1.24x ahead).

## What a late but attentive reader could have done

First reasonable entry: 8 to 11 Nov 2024, around Rs43. Staying in: the run's worst pullback was 21%, so a 20% stop was on a knife edge; a 30% stop tracked the staircase. Reasonable exit: the offer day or the first 20% stop, both in December 2025 to February 2026.

## The trap

The deal. The acquirer's price ran 20% into the target's formal offer and then fell 44% in three and a half months, helped by the March 2026 shock, while the target Pioneer fell 49% from its own peak (see PIOC_202212). A reader who treats "acquires a company" as bullish buys the day the news is already priced. The buy-back was irrelevant: 25.8m shares is a small fraction of the share count (count not checked), and a 20% stop from the buy-back date sold the whole run in August 2024.

## Questions that would have caught it

C1 and C3 (type: acquirer; who gains: Pioneer holders at Rs478), C4 (the December leg was extension: +23% in the five sessions from 8 Dec), C6 (what the offer buys: the SPA already buys 58%), C9 (stop width), B7 (an acquirer's funding: unverified), A1 (the cut on the peak day).

## The if-then rule

If a cement-wave acquirer runs 20% in the two weeks before its offer document, sell into the document, not after: the acquirer tops on announcement. Before that, hold with a 30% trailing stop.

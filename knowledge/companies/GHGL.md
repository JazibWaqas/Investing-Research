# GHGL (Ghani Glass): underwriting card

Written 17 Oct 2026, before the 1QFY27 result (expected about 27 to 28 Oct). Price Rs39.22 (close 16 Oct 2026, `data/prices_raw/GHGL.csv`). KMI All Share member (`data/kmi_allshare_2026-06-05.csv`) and KSE-100 member (16 Oct screener). **Verdict: WATCH. The stock is cheap and cash-rich, but the expected return (about 15% including dividends) is only a few points above the 12-month T-bill (about 12.5%), and one line in the tax note could take Rs1.7 a share off earnings. Buy after the 1Q print if it confirms the tax rate and margin, or at Rs35 or below.**

## The story

Ghani Glass is Pakistan's largest glass maker: 390,418 tonnes a year of capacity, run at 85% in FY26. Revenue was Rs48.4bn in FY26: float glass Rs27.1bn (56%, the TGL-competing business tied to construction and autos) and container glass Rs20.7bn (bottles and jars for pharma, beverages and food). Exports collapsed from Rs4.3bn to Rs0.5bn, so this is now a domestic story. The company also owns 49.9% of RAK Ghani Glass in the UAE, which makes pharma glass bottles. That stake is equity-accounted and contributed Rs501m (Rs0.50 a share) of profit plus Rs328m of cash dividends. There are no subsidiaries, so reported EPS is the group figure (lesson 1 does not bite here).

Why look now: FY26 EPS rose 22% to Rs7.23 and the quarters got better through the year. Quarterly EPS was 0.71, 1.69, 2.29 and 2.54, and gross margin went from 21.1% in 1Q to 32.2% in 4Q. Net cash is about Rs5.9bn, debt is zero apart from leases, and the payout jumped to Rs3.50 for FY26 (48% of EPS) from Rs1.00 (17%) for FY25. The comparison for the coming 1QFY27 result is the weak 0.71.

**Verdict on price:** mispriced by about 15 to 20% on the base case, not 50%. The discount has reasons. Glass lagged the market by 78 points in 2022 to 2024. Float glass, more than half of revenue, has excess capacity: TGL has a rebuilt 550 t/day furnace sitting idle, and the 1,000 t/day Lucky-TGL JV is on hold. The FY24 commentary said GHGL would enter the full tax regime at about 39% from FY26 (Profit, unverified). The FY26 accounts show Rs1.75bn of credits holding the rate at 21%. If those credits are a temporary investment allowance, the real P/E is 7x, not 5.4x.

## What kills it

1. **Tax credits end.** Trigger: an effective tax rate above 30% in 1Q or 2QFY27. That cuts EPS by about Rs1.7 and makes the stock fairly priced.
2. **Float glass price war.** Trigger: TGL announces it is restarting its idle 550 t/day furnace or commissioning the Lucky JV, or GHGL's gross margin falls below 25% in any quarter.
3. **Energy shock feeds through.** Trigger: an OGRA or RLNG notice raising the industrial gas price, or Brent staying above US$120 into the December quarter (it is at 126.6 now). Fuel, gas and power was Rs12.2bn, 35% of cost of sales.
4. **The payout reverts.** Trigger: no 1Q interim in November, or an interim below 5%. The FY26 payout jump is a large part of the case.
5. **Related-party leakage.** Trigger: amounts due from group companies (Ghani Value Glass, Ghani Ceramics) above Rs1.5bn. They were Rs935m at June, against Rs716m a year earlier. Trade debts also rose 35% to Rs7.4bn.

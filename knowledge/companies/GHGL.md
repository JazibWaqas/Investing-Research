# GHGL (Ghani Glass): underwriting card

Written 17 Oct 2026, before the 1QFY27 result (expected about 27 to 28 Oct). Price Rs39.22 (close 16 Oct 2026, `data/prices_raw/GHGL.csv`). KMI All Share member (`data/kmi_allshare_2026-06-05.csv`) and KSE-100 member (16 Oct screener). **Verdict: WATCH. The stock is cheap and cash-rich, but the expected return (about 15% including dividends) is only a few points above the 12-month T-bill (about 12.5%), and one line in the tax note could take Rs1.7 a share off earnings. Buy after the 1Q print if it confirms the tax rate and margin, or at Rs35 or below.**

## The story

Ghani Glass is Pakistan's largest glass maker: 390,418 tonnes a year of capacity, run at 85% in FY26. Revenue was Rs48.4bn in FY26: float glass Rs27.1bn (56%, the TGL-competing business tied to construction and autos) and container glass Rs20.7bn (bottles and jars for pharma, beverages and food). Exports collapsed from Rs4.3bn to Rs0.5bn, so this is now a domestic story. The company also owns 49.9% of RAK Ghani Glass in the UAE, which makes pharma glass bottles. That stake is equity-accounted and contributed Rs501m (Rs0.50 a share) of profit plus Rs328m of cash dividends. There are no subsidiaries, so reported EPS is the group figure (lesson 1 does not bite here).

Why look now: FY26 EPS rose 22% to Rs7.23 and the quarters got better through the year. Quarterly EPS was 0.71, 1.69, 2.29 and 2.54, and gross margin went from 21.1% in 1Q to 32.2% in 4Q. Net cash is about Rs5.9bn, debt is zero apart from leases, and the payout jumped to Rs3.50 for FY26 (48% of EPS) from Rs1.00 (17%) for FY25. The comparison for the coming 1QFY27 result is the weak 0.71.

**Verdict on price:** mispriced by about 15 to 20% on the base case, not 50%. The discount has reasons. Glass lagged the market by 78 points in 2022 to 2024. Float glass, more than half of revenue, has excess capacity: TGL has a rebuilt 550 t/day furnace sitting idle, and the 1,000 t/day Lucky-TGL JV is on hold. ## What kills it

1. **Tax credits end.** Trigger: an effective tax rate above 30% in 1Q or 2QFY27. That cuts EPS by about Rs1.7 and makes the stock fairly priced.
2. **Float glass price war.** Trigger: TGL announces it is restarting its idle 550 t/day furnace or commissioning the Lucky JV, or GHGL's gross margin falls below 25% in any quarter.
3. **Energy shock feeds through.** Trigger: an OGRA or RLNG notice raising the industrial gas price, or Brent staying above US$120 into the December quarter (it is at 126.6 now). Fuel, gas and power was Rs12.2bn, 35% of cost of sales.
4. **The payout reverts.** Trigger: no 1Q interim in November, or an interim below 5%. The FY26 payout jump is a large part of the case.
5. **Related-party leakage.** Trigger: amounts due from group companies (Ghani Value Glass, Ghani Ceramics) above Rs1.5bn. They were Rs935m at June, against Rs716m a year earlier. Trade debts also rose 35% to Rs7.4bn.

## Red team (17 Oct 2026)

**Final: WEAKENED. Corrected expected return about +9.5% at Rs39.22 (below the 12.5% T-bill), not +15.3%. The plan's own entry rule (buy up to Rs42 after the print) gives about +5.7%.**

Three strongest objections:

1. **The base case is a re-rating, not earnings.** Rs44 is FY27E EPS 7.3 at 6.0x, against 5.4x today. At an unchanged 5.4x the base is Rs39.4 and the probability-weighted value is 0.25 x 29 + 0.5 x 39.4 + 0.25 x 52 = Rs39.96. Add Rs2.975 of net dividends and the return is +9.5%. Nothing in the evidence supports the extra 0.6x: the glass peers fell over six months (TGL -7.7%, GGGL -12.3%, GVGL -8.4%) and over 12 months (TGL -30%, GGGL -35%), TGL at 6.8x has falling EPS, and `tests/sector_momentum_regime.md` and lesson 17 say value is a tiebreaker inside a sector view, not an edge. Glass is a bottom-momentum sector. Also unaccounted: capital gains tax and about 0.5% round-trip cost (`tests/` cost assumption), roughly 1.5 points together (CGT rate unverified), which takes the return to about 8%.

2. **The plan contradicts the expected return.** The card says buy after the print at up to Rs42. The 12-month return from Rs42 is (42.25 + 2.125) / 42 - 1 = +5.7%, because a post-print buyer misses the Rs1.00 final (2.125 is the net of the three remaining dividends: 0.50, 1.00, 1.00). To beat 12.5% on the card's own values the entry must be about Rs39.4 or lower. And the print is a coin flip with a bad history: on 28 Oct 2025 the stock fell from 39.51 to 33.37 (-15.5%) in two days. The repo has no post-results drift test, so "wait for confirmation" has no measured edge. The 6-month move (+5.4%, price 6 months ago Rs37.21) is already above peers, and the stock is +40% off the 9 Mar low, so part of the 4Q margin recovery is priced. Lesson 13 (waiting has a cost) cuts the other way, but the numbers still say the entry has to be lower.

3. **Dividend and cash quality are weaker than the headline.** (a) `data/payouts_raw.csv` shows the FY25 final was 15% (Rs1.50), not the Rs1.00 the card uses as the FY25 payout. The FY26 final is Rs1.00, a 33% cut in the final despite EPS +22%, and the trailing 12 months paid Rs4.0 (sector note). The "payout rose" story is mostly the three new interims, so the Rs3.5 run-rate is a board choice, not a trend. (b) Net cash of Rs5.93bn is before the declared Rs1.0bn July interim and the Rs1.0bn final (declared 27 Aug), so cash after declared dividends is about Rs3.9bn (Rs3.9 a share, 10% of market cap, not 15%). (c) Trade debts rose 35% to Rs7.4bn, related-party receivables rose to Rs935m, and Rs3.2bn of tax refunds is a receivable: OCF of Rs7.26bn on net profit of Rs7.23bn is not strong cash conversion. FCF of Rs4.69bn still covers Rs3.5bn of dividends 1.3x, so the payout is affordable but not safe if tax credits stop (cash profit falls by Rs1.75bn).

Checks that held up:
- **Share count and EPS basis:** 999.7m shares, no subsidiaries, RAK stake equity-accounted, so EPS 7.23 is the group figure. One-offs net about +Rs0.05, immaterial.
- **Liquidity:** 60-day median traded value Rs8.4m, median volume 209k shares. Rs12k is 0.14% of a median day; the thinnest day in 60 was Rs0.53m, still 44 times the order.
- **Announcements in 2026:** no suspension, unusual-movement letter, non-compliance notice, auditor change or pledge found in the titles (`data/announcements.parquet`, 434 GHGL rows, titles only, PDFs unread, so absence is weak). One governance item: the CFO died (15 Jul 2026) and a new CFO was appointed on 27 Aug, the day of the annual results. A first set of numbers under a new CFO adds risk to the tax-credit disclosure.
- **Tax credits** are the real swing: credits recurred two years running (Rs1.59bn FY25, Rs1.75bn FY26), which supports the card's 75% survival probability, but the source is still unverified, so the 25% bear weight is a guess. The bear case (EPS 5.5 at 5.2x) also leaves out the energy hit with Brent at 126.6 while 4Q margin of 32.2% was earned when Brent was near 70.

Base rates: nothing in `tests/` supports a 12-month edge from value, momentum or results timing for this setup (placebo 20-day median -1.5%, win 43%; sector momentum long-only no better than the market). Probabilities of 25/50/25 are symmetric around a base that already assumes re-rating, so they are generous to the upside.

# FFC: management and business quality (18 Oct 2026)

Sources are PSX documents saved in data/raw/filings/quality/ (doc ids in brackets). AR = annual report, CB = corporate briefing slides. No briefing transcripts exist on PSX, only slides and video.

## People

- **Sponsor:** Fauji Foundation, 44.32% (H1 2026 report [281738]). No other holder is above 10% (AR2025 [270829]).
- **Chairman:** Lt Gen Anwar Ali Hyder (Retd) since 5-6 Apr 2024 [228768], replacing Waqar Ahmed Malik. He is also MD and CEO of Fauji Foundation and a former defence minister (AR2025). Sponsor and board chair are the same person.
- **MD and CEO:** Jahangir Piracha since 1 Apr 2024 [227290]. An outsider to the Fauji line: earlier CEO of Engro Polymer, Engro Powergen Qadirpur and Engro Vopak (AR2025 profile). His predecessor Sarfaraz Ahmed Rehman served Oct 2021 to Mar 2024 [172131]; the one before him was a retired general (OCR garbled the name, unverified).
- Record under them: standalone EPS Rs15.76 (2022), 23.32, 45.49 (merger year), 51.69 (2025); consolidated 26.6, 36.6, 62.4, 58.4 (quarterly_eps.csv), so 2025 fell 6%. Stock Rs113 (Dec 2023) to Rs538 (16 Oct 2026), mostly re-rating plus merger. Board: 13, four independent; auditor A. F. Ferguson (AR2025), history unverified.

## Big capital decisions

- **FFBL merger** (swap 1 FFC per 4.29 FFBL, KPMG letter, effective 1 Jul 2024; 150.9m shares issued, about +12%): strong. Combined profit Rs73.6bn in 2025 vs FFC alone Rs29.7bn in 2023. Whether the ratio was fair to FFC holders is unverified.
- **Thar 30%** (cost about Rs7.3bn): share of profit about Rs3.7bn on Rs12.4bn TEL profit, but cash arrived three years late and depends on circular-debt payments.
- **Food:** Fauji Foods carried at Rs12.1bn after a Rs2bn impairment; FFF cost Rs6.3bn plus. Roughly Rs18bn for about Rs1.5bn of profit in the best year: below cost of capital.
- **PIA:** Rs30.6bn paid plus Rs30.59bn committed for 33.99% of PIA Equity [281738], consistent with a Rs135bn bid for 75% (Arab News, Business Recorder, 2026). About 8% of market cap into an airline, outside the fertilizer skill set. Return unknown; losses start from Q3 2026.

## Minorities

- No rights issue and no bonus shares in corporate_actions.csv since 2021; share count rose only by merger and swap issues (1,272m to 1,439m).
- Dividends: DPS Rs15.49 (2023), 34.86 (2024), 37.00 (2025), about 66-77% of standalone EPS, never cut in the period.
- Against that, every sponsor-side deal is with Fauji Foundation: the wind purchase, the FFBL merger, and the FPCL 25% swap (15.9m FFC shares, 13.49 FPCL per FFC share, "independent valuer", Jan 2026 [281738]). Foundation's stake rose from 43.70% to 44.32% as a result. Price fairness unverified.
- Insider dealings: unverified. insider_trades.csv has zero FFC rows; about 60 director/executive interest filings exist since 2019 but were not parsed.

## Disclosure

Four briefings a year with slides posted since at least 2020, plus video from 2026; integrated annual report, SAFA overall winner in 2024 and 2025 (AR2025); PSX Top 25 for 13 years (AR2023). One briefing notice was revoked in March 2019 [127512]. Weak spots: no transcripts; the Q4 2025 sales-tax charge size is a broker estimate (Topline, 29 Jan 2026), not in the filing summary; board and CEO notices are scanned letters.

## Growth engine, next five years

Nothing clearly doubles EPS. Urea runs at 112% of capacity, shares are already 56-58%, so volume is capped by gas. Plausible adds: Port Qasim gas from Ghazij/Shawal, PEF holding output, Thar dividends, Agritech, Askari Bank. Realistic 1.3 to 1.6x nominal (my estimate, unverified). PIA and coal-to-urea (evaluation only, CB [264015]) are options, not drivers. Breakers: Mari feed-gas repricing, GIDC (Rs85bn under a stay), super tax, payout cut to fund PIA, a PIA loss spiral.

## Red flags

1. Rs61bn into an airline while GIDC (Rs85bn) is unpaid.
2. Rs39.6bn provisioned against sales-tax receivables; growing.
3. Repeated purchases from the sponsor, and sponsor chief is chairman.
4. Food arm reached a first profit only in 2025, after a decade or more (start date unverified).
5. Dividend and investment income were about 38% of 2024 profit (CB [246812]); part is Askari Bank equity income, not cash.

## Scores (0 to 5)

- Promises vs delivery: **4**. Market and operating promises met, projects late.
- Capital allocation: **3**. Merger and wind strong, food weak, PIA unproven.
- Minority treatment: **3**. Steady payout and no dilution, but sponsor-side deals.
- Disclosure quality: **4**. Frequent, award-winning, but no transcripts and key charges unsized.

Overall **14/20**.

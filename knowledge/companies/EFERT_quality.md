## Capital decisions
- EnVen (built 2007 to 2011 per AR 2025 history, about US$1.1bn per Dawn 30 Mar 2015, secondary): world-scale urea plant that carries the volume (2.3Mt urea 2023 to 2025). Returns have been strong: ROE 35 to 59% 2021 to 2025 [sa_ratios], but earned on subsidised gas and cheap equity after near-full payouts.
- Since then: maintenance capex (about Rs10 to 12bn a year, 2025 note), the PEF (about US$300m industry-wide, EFERT share unverified), phosphates trading, digital. Nothing that adds capacity. Rs14.8bn went into mutual funds at Dec 2025 (from Rs2.4bn) [272025 note 13].

## Minorities
- Dividends 2022 to 2025: Rs13.5, 20.5, 21.5, 15.0 a share = Rs94bn against Rs93bn profit, about 80% of operating cash flow (OCF 2022 to 2025 about Rs116bn, 2024 negative at about -Rs4bn). Payout cut 42% in H1 2026 (Rs3.75) as profit fell [280740].
- Parent takes Rs14.3bn of dividends (2025), royalty and reimbursements of roughly Rs3bn a year, and lent/borrowed Rs33bn short-term in 2025 (subordinated loan, mark-up Rs227m) [272025 note 45]. All disclosed, arm's length per the board. No rights issue or bonus in the record (corporate_actions.csv holds only 2025-26 cash; earlier years unverified). Insider file shows one low-confidence 2020 line; no sponsor dealings known.

## Disclosure
Quarterly briefings with slides since 2014, video recordings posted, plant outages disclosed within days, CEO exit same day. Negatives: annual reports 2021 to 2024 are scans, no explicit payout policy found, resignation reasons not given.

## Growth engine
Nothing doubles EPS in five years. Volume is capped at about 2.3Mt urea; earnings are gas price minus urea price. Best case is returning to 2023/24 EPS (about Rs20 to 21 from Rs15.9 TTM) if the Rs150/bag premium holds, inventory clears and the gas gap closes. Specialty, Markaz and UgAi are immaterial. What breaks it: Mari pressure decline if PEF slips again; the SNGPL contract for EnVen ends 2027 (sector card, unverified); GIDC/NGDS cess up to Rs700/bag; super tax; 519kt inventory.

## Red flags
CEO/CFO churn; PEF slippage; structural gas cost disadvantage and share loss in 2026; payout above cash flow in 2024; Rs6.5bn government receivable aged 7 years; tax and cess litigation; holding-company layers (Engro Holdings, Engro Corp).

- Promises vs delivery 3: plants and share delivered, timelines and policy asks missed.

- Minority treatment 3: near-full payouts, disclosed related parties, but parent charges and cash-flow-short payout in 2024.

# SAZEW (Sazgar Engineering Works): management and business quality

Written 18 Oct 2026. AR = annual report, CBS = corporate briefing slides (no Q&A text is published). Numbers are PSX document ids saved in data/raw/filings/quality/. Many material-information filings are one-page scans I could not read.

## People and record
- **Mian Asad Hameed**, CEO and director since the 1991 incorporation (AR 2026). Re-appointed for three years from 28 Mar 2026. Wife **Saira Asad Hameed** is chairperson; son **Mian Muhammad Ali Hameed** is COO, cars; CFO is Muhammad Atif Rao (CBS 283385).
- Record: revenue Rs4.0bn (FY21) to Rs191.7bn (FY26) by localising the GWM Haval franchise. Before that it was thin: EPS Rs0.70 to Rs3.04 in FY19 to FY21 (CBS 231998).
- Control: family holds about 63% (AR 2026: Asad 37.43%, Saira 6.41%, Ali 11.61%, Saeed Khan 4.48%, plus spouses). Three of nine directors are independent. CEO pay is Rs5.8m a month net, rising 15% a year (Mettis, 30 Mar 2026), trivial against Rs23.6bn profit.

## Big capital decisions
- **2020 rights issue**: 65.84% at Rs50 (Rs710m), mostly taken up by sponsors (doc 143146, scan, unread). It funded the car plant. Equity went from Rs1.95bn (Jun 2022) to Rs43.0bn (Jun 2026). The best decision on record. Haval localisation cost little: PP&E Rs2.6bn to Rs2.9bn while profit rose 8x.
- **Now**: Rs22bn paint shop (54,000 units a year against 19,179 sold in FY26), Rs4bn for 900 kanals, Rs1.1bn plot, Rs868m land (6 Oct 2026, doc 284740, unread): about Rs28bn against Rs23.6bn profit. Stock in trade tripled to Rs42.1bn and Rs6.8bn of new debt was drawn (AR 2026). **No return earned yet.**

## Minorities
- Clean: no related-party trades with associated companies ("Nil", AR 2023, 2026); small director fees; no rights issue since 2020.
- Low payout: 24% (FY23), 15% (FY24), about 19% (FY25), 18% (FY26), while cash and land accumulate.
- Sponsor sold: Asad's stake fell from 25.30m to 22.63m shares (Jun 2024 to Jun 2026) and free float rose 31.7% to 35.0% (AR 2024, 2026). Part went to relatives. Buyer and price **unverified**: insider_trades.csv has no SAZEW rows and the 5.6.1(d) filings are scans.
- Unprovided contingencies: stayed additional customs duty on CKD kits, a section 5A undistributed-profit case, sales tax demands (AR 2026 note 17); amounts unquantified.

## Disclosure
Good: quarterly results 3 to 4 weeks after quarter end, monthly unit sales, slides filed before briefings, candid policy warning. Weak: blank Q&A, scans, the Sep 2026 briefing revoked and reset, and auditor Saeed Kamran & Co. removed in June 2024 for Crowe Hussain Chaudhury with no reason found (AR 2024). Reports unmodified. **FY25 AR (258831) not read.**

## Growth engine
Three-wheelers and parts are small. The engine is Haval, Tank and NEV volume. Doubling FY26 EPS (Rs390.51) by FY31 needs about 2x units at a 24% gross margin while hybrid tax is higher (see SAZEW.md). I would not assume it. It breaks on hybrid tax parity, a GWM franchise change, PKR, and new Chinese entrants.

## Red flags
1. Capex plan went Rs4.5bn, Rs11.5bn, then Rs22bn plus land, with no stated return target.
2. Rs42bn stock, and operating cash flow of Rs9.6bn on Rs23.6bn profit.
3. 18% payout funds expansion while the sponsor sold shares.
4. One franchise, and one family in the chair, CEO and a COO role.
5. Exports promised yearly, none delivered.

## Scores (0 to 5)
- Promises vs delivery **4**: core promises met early; exports and capex size missed.
- Capital allocation **3**: rights issue and Haval bet excellent; Rs28bn programme unproven.
- Minority treatment **3**: clean on related parties, low payout, sponsor selling.
- Disclosure **3**: timely and candid, but scans, blank Q&A, unexplained auditor change.

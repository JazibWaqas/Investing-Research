# OGDC: Oil & Gas Development Company. Underwriting card

As of 2026-10-17. Price Rs299.10 (16 Oct close, ex the Rs6 final, which went ex on 8 Oct). KMI All Share member (list of 5 Jun 2026), and also KMI-30. **Verdict: WATCH. Buy at Rs275 or below.** The business is sound, but at today's price the expected return of about 7% is below the 6-month T-bill.

## The story

OGDC is Pakistan's largest E&P. It is state controlled, with about 85% held by the government after the BESOS shares were transferred back in July 2026 (doc 279735; the exact percentage is unverified). It earns dollar-linked revenue from oil and from wellhead gas priced under the 2012 policy, so earnings move with Brent and the rupee. FY26 revenue was Rs449.2bn (+12%) and profit Rs242.4bn, EPS Rs56.35 (+43%). Both are records, and so is the DPS of Rs17.

Why now: Brent is about USD106 with Hormuz shut. OGRA raised wellhead gas 8-10% for Jul-Dec 2026. New wells (Baragzai, Bobi Deep, Sahito, Lundali-1 on 6 Sep) add volume. Topline expects 1QFY27 EPS of Rs12.91 (+45% YoY, -56% QoQ) with a Rs5 DPS. A further upside is the Rs1.49tr gas circular debt plan, which would turn part of OGDC's Rs508bn of overdue receivables into dividends. The government's indicative proceeds from OGDC are about Rs197bn.

Several things are already in the price. Consensus already includes the +44% Q1 print. The stock is down 14% since 6 July (KSE-100 -11%) and down 9% since the 4 September results (KSE-100 -5%), so the market is not paying for USD106 oil. Over 1 year OGDC is +16% against KSE-100 +1%. The market treats today's oil as temporary, and that is a reasonable view.

## What kills it

1. **Oil normalises.** Trigger: Brent's 4-week average falls below USD80, or a durable Hormuz reopening is confirmed.
2. **Collections slip.** Trigger: trade debts rise above Rs650bn in a quarterly report, or the collection ratio falls below 90%.
3. **Expected credit loss provisioning starts.** Trigger: SECP does not extend the exemption past Dec 2026 and OGDC books an impairment on its Rs592bn of government receivables.
4. **Payout cut.** Trigger: Q1 DPS below Rs4, or Q1 EPS below Rs10.
5. **The tax reverses again.** Trigger: the FY28 Finance Bill (June 2027) brings back super tax at company level or raises the windfall levy.
6. **Curtailment.** Trigger: OGDC gas output down QoQ on RLNG surplus, as reported in the quarterly production figures.

## Red team (17 Oct 2026)

Checked against data/prices_raw/OGDC.csv, data/announcements.parquet, data/fundamentals/quarterly_eps.csv, data/macro/brent.csv, tests/commodity_pkr_sensitivity.md and ledger/calls.csv.

**What held up.**
- Share count (4,300.93m) and EPS basis match the data (StockAnalysis shows 4,301m; the four FY26 quarters sum to 56.35).
- The 52-week low of Rs240.15 on 12 Nov 2025 is correct.
- Liquidity is a non-issue. Median traded value over the last 120 sessions is Rs966m a day, and the minimum is Rs283m.
- The 2026 announcements (45 rows) contain no suspension, unusual-movement letter, non-compliance notice, auditor change or pledge. The only governance items are two director appointments (2 Feb, 13 Mar), one director/executive interest disclosure (21 May, size not shown in the title) and the BESOS transfer back to the government.
- Six-month price is Rs314.71 to Rs299.10 (-5%), in line with PPL -7% and MARI -4%. The stock has not run, so the catalyst is not obviously priced on price alone.

**Three strongest objections.**

1. **The probabilities lean on a base rate the repo's own tests contradict.**
   - tests/commodity_pkr_sensitivity.md finds E&P goes the wrong way after Brent spikes. Median net return is -4.85% at 1 month with a 29% win rate, and -2.6 / -4.5 / -5.7% versus KSE-100 at 1/2/3 months (7 episodes, "fails everywhere"). The September 2026 onset (Brent +64.5% over 3 months) is still pending.
   - Our own frozen prediction records OGDC rising after only 29% of 7 results windows, median -1.8%.
   - The 25% bull case needs a CCoE/IMF decision that has slipped before, plus a rerating to 7.5x. The bear case ignores a 1.5 to 3x P/E tail (OGDC traded at 1.5x in 2023) and the expected-credit-loss impairment, which is listed as a killer but not priced anywhere.
   - A fairer set is bear 30% / base 55% / bull 15%. That gives Rs290.75 plus Rs17 of dividends, or about **+2.9%** (about +2% after roughly 1% round-trip costs and the 15% capital gains tax; the card includes neither). That is well below the ~11% T-bill.

2. **The cheapness measure is mixed.**
   - Historical relative P/Es use OGDC's reported P/E. The "now" row uses normalised EPS (6.7x) against a reported-basis screener median (8.4x) from a different source. On a like-for-like reported basis it is 5.3 / 8.4 = 0.63, versus 0.68 at FY-end. So "top of its range" is overstated, and equally the base-case multiple (0.75 relative, 6.3x) rests on a proxy with no KSE-100 series.
   - "Normalised" Q4 is Rs17.75 per share (127.1bn less 50.8bn, over 4,301m shares) against Rs8 to 10 in each of Q1 to Q3. Year-end true-ups are not ruled out, and Topline's Q1 estimate of 12.91 is 27% below it. The Rs44.55 mixes three pre-spike quarters with one spike quarter.
   - Rs82/share of net cash includes Rs269.9bn of "other financial assets", and its composition (government instruments, restricted balances) is not shown. A state-controlled company does not obviously hand that cash to minorities.

3. **Dividends are covered by the cash pile, not by free cash flow, and the special dividend is treated inconsistently.**
   - FY26 operating cash flow was Rs160.5bn (66% of profit), against capex of Rs108bn plus Rs33bn into associates. Free cash flow of about Rs19bn does not cover Rs73bn of dividends, and Rs86bn at Rs20 a share. The payout is affordable only while the Rs353bn cash holds.
   - The bull case (Rs390) omits the roughly Rs46 net per share special dividend, which is unverified and a pure inference from Rs197bn. If the special dividend is real, the bull is understated by about 12 points of return. If it is not, the bull's rerating story has no source. Either way, the bull return of +36% is not computed on one basis.

**Smaller flags.**
- The card says Brent is about USD106, but data/macro/brent.csv (FRED spot) shows USD126.55 on 14 Oct. The source and basis are unreconciled.
- The 12-month dividend count includes the final due about 7 Oct 2027, which falls just inside the window.
- The proposed Rs275 limit gives about +12% on the corrected distribution, not the card's +17%. Rs17 net of dividends plus a Rs290.75 exit needs a buy near Rs263 to restore +17%.

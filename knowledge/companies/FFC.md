# FFC: Fauji Fertilizer Company. Underwriting card

As of 2026-10-17. Price Rs537.87 (16 Oct close). KMI All Share member (list of 5 Jun 2026), also KMI-30. **Verdict: WATCH. Buy at Rs485 or below.** A high-quality, cash-generating urea franchise, but the market already pays a market multiple for it. On my numbers the expected 12-month return is about +5%, about half the T-bill yield.

## The story

FFC is Pakistan's largest urea maker and, since absorbing FFBL in Dec 2024, the largest DAP producer. In H1 2026 it held 56% of urea offtake (48% a year earlier) and 66% of DAP. Its two Mari-network plants buy feed gas at a regulated price below the SNGPL rate, and that cheap gas is the core of the earnings. The group adds Askari Bank (Rs7.4bn share of profit in H1), Thar Energy, PMP Morocco, wind farms, Fauji Foods, FCCL and Agritech. Since Jun 2026 it also holds 33.99% of PIA Equity Limited, the SPV that owns PIA.

Why people like it now: H1 2026 consolidated EPS was Rs29.05 (+11%), the 2026 dividend run-rate is Rs41, urea inventory is lean (87k t at end-Sep against EFERT's 519k), and the sector has lagged the market since June. Why I am cautious: the stock already re-rated, the cash pile is spoken for, and the PIA bet starts to hit the P&L from Q3.

## What kills it

1. **Gas repricing.** Trigger: an OGRA or ECC notification raising Mari-network feed gas for fertilizer, or an IMF review document naming it. Each Rs100/mmbtu on feed gas costs roughly Rs2/share after tax if not passed on (my estimate).
2. **PIA drag.** Trigger: share of loss from PIA Equity above Rs1bn in a quarter, an impairment, or a payout cut to fund the second tranche.
3. **Payout cut.** Trigger: Q3 DPS below Rs9.
4. **Share loss.** Trigger: monthly FFC urea offtake falling y/y for two months while industry offtake rises (Q3 already shows -9% against industry +2%).
5. **GIDC.** Trigger: the SHC stay vacated, which makes Rs85bn payable plus late-payment surcharge.
6. **Winter curtailment** at Port Qasim (RLNG/SSGC). Trigger: Qatar force majeure extended past 6 Nov.

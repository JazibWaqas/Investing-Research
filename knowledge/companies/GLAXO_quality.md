## Capital decisions

No acquisitions or disposals in five years. Capex Rs2.6bn (2025), Rs2.8bn (2024); no debt. ROE 19.3 / 25.5 / 11.7 / 2.5 / 23.1 / 29.8% for 2020-25: above a roughly 15% cost of equity (my assumption) in four years, far below in 2022-23. Shingrix and the finance hub are the only new bets; returns not yet disclosed. GSK still makes Rs1.3bn of OTC goods for Haleon Pakistan pending DRAP transfers.

## Minorities

- Share count flat at 318.5m; no rights, no bonus (AR2025 table; corporate_actions.csv shows only cash since 2025: 10, 5, 12, 7).
- Dividends paid when profitable except 2022-23 (EPS 7.73 and 1.68 unpaid, board cites "investing in growth" and working capital). Payout about 50% in 2024-25.
- Group dealings: purchases from affiliates Rs4.5bn (7% of sales), royalty Rs0.70bn (1.1%), expense recoveries Rs1.4bn, promotional allowance from group Rs0.6bn (Rs2.1bn in 2024) (AR2025 note 35). Transfer-price fairness unverified.
- Insider dealings: insider_trades.csv has no GLAXO rows; director-interest filings exist (Dec 2018 [123310], Nov 2020 [156942]) but were not read. Unverified.

## Disclosure

Annual report out in early April each year, AGM April/May, Q4 results 26 Mar 2026; best corporate report in pharma 2022 (CB 2023). One briefing a year (Nov or Dec) plus Apr 2026, slides only, video from 2026. Revocations: 2014, 2016, Oct 2017 (Q3 results), Feb 2020, Sep 2022 (Panadol info), Oct 2024 (9M results): about six in twelve years. AGM extension requested Mar 2023. Weak: no forward numbers, 2026 turnover, some scanned filings.

## Growth engine, next five years

Little that doubles EPS. 2025 profit rose 53% from margin (25% to 37%) after deregulation, not volume: antibiotics flat at Rs31.4bn, derms +21% (AR2025 note 21). Underlying sales growth slowed to 4.4% in H1 2026. Plausible: 8-10% nominal sales, Shingrix and vaccines, tender business; EPS 1.3-1.6x on my estimate (unverified). Breaks it: the 28 Aug 2026 order to review DRAP pricing, hardship rules and margins near 40% (ProPakistani); a rollback of deregulation; super tax; stock already -20% since Dec 2025 (Rs389.8 to Rs312.45, stockanalysis.com 16 Oct 2026).

## Red flags

1. Earnings quality depends on regulation: margin 7% to 37% in two years.
2. No dividend in 2022-23 despite Rs8.5bn cash at end-2021.
3. 83% parent, affiliate purchases and royalty set by group.
4. Leadership and CFO churn mid-2026; inventory days up to 105 in 2025 from 91.
5. Many tax appeals pending since 2009 (AR2025 note 20).

# ISL (International Steels Ltd): management and business quality (18 Oct 2026)

Sources are PSX documents saved in data/raw/filings/quality/ISL/ (doc ids in brackets). AR = annual report, CB = corporate briefing. AR2025 [258751] has a broken font layer (text unreadable), so FY25 facts come from CB2025 [259851] and AR2026. No briefing transcripts exist, only slides (2023 to 2025) and a 2026 video notice.

## People

- **Sponsor:** International Industries Ltd (IIL, Amir S. Chinoy group) 56.33%; Sumitomo 9.08%; JFE about 4.8%; free float about 31% (135m of 435m shares) (AR2026 p.203). Share count has stayed 435m since listing in 2011.
- **Chairman:** Kamal A. Chinoy, director since 2007, also chairs IIL (AR2026). Sponsor family is chair and CEO.
- **CEO:** Samir M. Chinoy since Oct 2024 [237992]; at ISL since 2009, previously COO and a family member. Predecessor Yousuf Mirza, CEO 2015 to Oct 2024, now IIL CEO and an ISL director. AR2026 says Mirza's tenure ended in 2023, but he signed PSX letters as CEO in Jan and Apr 2024 [224399, 229101]: the bio is wrong or loose (unverified which).
- **Record under them (EPS, FY June):** 17.16 (2021), 12.44, 8.10, 8.40, 3.58, 8.44 (2026) (AR2026, quarterly_eps.csv). Price Rs93 (Dec 2020) to Rs79 (16 Oct 2026); dividend-adjusted about -9% in six years. Auditor A. F. Ferguson throughout.

Pattern: near-term operating promises are kept (some late); strategic ones (hot-rolled mill, "strategic growth initiatives" in CB2025) are dropped or unquantified. No volume or earnings targets are ever given.

## Big capital decisions

- **CCM-2 cold mill:** Rs5.6bn, announced Feb 2017, started 21 Jun 2018 (Business Recorder, Pakistan Today; web). It doubled cold-rolling capacity to 1.0m t. Sales peaked at 539 kt in FY19 and were 435 kt in FY26; production was 447 kt (about 45% utilisation) after 264 kt in FY25 (CB2023, AR2026). Capacity is paid for, so FY26 shows operating leverage, but ROE was only 13.7% in FY26.
- **Solar:** Rs1bn, on budget (CB2025).
- **CECL:** small, good return, but ISL also guaranteed Rs1.5bn of CECL bank debt and a joint Reko Diq performance guarantee (AR2026 note 7.4); release on exit is unverified.

## Minorities

- Cash dividends every year: DPS Rs10, 6.5, 5.5, 5.5, 2.5, 5.0 for FY21 to FY26, payout 52 to 70% (AR2026 six-year table, partly garbled; corporate_actions.csv). No rights, no bonus, no dilution. The interim was nil in Jan 2025 and the FY25 payout was still 70%.
- Purchases from related parties Rs38.8bn in FY26, mostly the HRC supplier Sumitomo (9.08% holder), "commercial terms" (AR2026 note 40). Price fairness is unverified.
- Insiders: directors sold 4.5m shares in FY25 and 126k (bought 3k) in FY26; sellers unnamed in the AR. insider_trades.csv holds only one 2019 filing (14k shares).

## Disclosure

Annual report in early September every year, a briefing every year since 2019, slides from 2023, prompt material-information letters, A+/A1 VIS rating (Nov 2025). Weaknesses: Jan 2025 half-year result was revoked on 29 Jan and reissued 6 Feb [246495, 246938], reason not stated; letters are scanned; AR2025 text layer is unusable; no transcripts.

## Growth engine, next five years

Volume on a built plant. FY26 earnings per tonne were about Rs8,400 PAT; 650 kt at that rate gives EPS near 12.6 (1.5x), and 2x needs about 870 kt or higher margins (my estimate, unverified). Levers: domestic demand 1,250 kt with imports 52% of it, anti-dumping enforcement and FATA/PATA tax alignment, exports (91 kt, doubled), and 11.5% policy rate. Breakers: duty collection stays stayed or circumvented; HRC price swings; the unprovided CCP penalty of Rs914m (about Rs2.1 per share, under appeal, AR2026 note 23.1.2); the SRO 565 HRC concession dispute (Rs5.7bn post-dated cheques); Sumitomo supply dependence.

## Red flags

1. Cash conversion: operating cash flow about -Rs4bn in FY26 against Rs3.67bn profit; short-term borrowings Rs10.7bn from Rs4.5bn.
2. CCP found price coordination among steel producers (Rs914m, no provision).
3. Earnings are cyclical and protection-dependent: FY21 EPS 17.16, FY25 3.58.
4. Half the cost base buys from one related shareholder.
5. Guarantees for a sponsor-group affiliate; sponsor family holds chair and CEO.
6. Hot-rolled mill idea dropped silently.

## Scores (0 to 5)

- Promises vs delivery: **3**. Operational items done, solar late, strategic items dropped, no targets to test.
- Capital allocation: **3**. Cheap, on-budget small projects; the big mill still runs at under half capacity.
- Minority treatment: **4**. Steady 52 to 70% payout, zero dilution; offset by related-party supply and guarantees.
- Disclosure quality: **4**. Regular and prompt; one unexplained revocation, scanned or garbled files, no transcripts.

Overall **14/20**.

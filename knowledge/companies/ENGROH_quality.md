## Disclosure
Good. Text reports; briefings yearly since 2019 and now quarterly with video; buy-backs reported next morning; the one-off is split out (EPS 46.2 reported, 24.1 underlying) [271352]; the EPCL offer was disclosed within three days. Auditor A.F. Ferguson throughout. Negatives: some 2024 to 2025 letters are scans; one director-interest filing was revoked then revised (Jan 2025).

## Growth engine
Underlying owners' profit was Rs29bn in 2025 (EPS 24.1); our last four quarters sum to Rs32, partly one-off. Doubling in five years needs tower profit to roughly double, fertiliser back to 2024 levels (PEF due Q3 2026) and Thar Phase III (COD Sep 2026). Plausible, not likely. Buy-backs add 3 to 4%. What breaks it: the gas-cost gap, EPQL take-and-pay cuts, super and minimum tax, inter-corporate dividend tax trapping cash below ENGROH, Jazz as main tenant, and liabilities of Rs755bn against equity of Rs319bn at an 11.5% policy rate.

## Red flags
EPS inflated by a Rs26.6bn reversal; no 2025 dividend; buy-back timing; lending authority to a sponsor-linked firm; debt-funded tower bet; family control via a holding layer; thermal buyer exit unexplained.

# GHNI (Ghandhara Industries): management and business quality

Written 18 Oct 2026. AR = annual report, CBS = corporate briefing slides (no Q&A text published). Numbers are PSX document ids in data/raw/filings/quality/. Appointment and material-information filings (229082, 164583, 113425, 266557, 281196) are scans I could not read.

## People and record
- **Chairman Lt Gen (R) Ali Kuli Khan Khattak** (ex-Chief of General Staff). **CEO Ahmad Kuli Khan Khattak** (ex-PAF Wing Commander, past chairman of APTMA and PAMA). **Muhammad Kuli Khan**, Deputy CEO, is the next generation (AR 2026, doc 283092). Filings in 2018, 2021 and 2024 suggest three-year terms (titles only).
- Control: the Bibojee Group holds about 57%: Bibojee Services 39% and sister company Ghandhara Automobiles Ltd (GAL) 17.91%. Directors hold 0.12% in their own names. Three of nine directors are independent.
- Record: loss in FY20 (EPS -30.11), then EPS 162.57 in FY26; sales Rs15.0bn (FY21) to Rs60.7bn (FY26). Number one in trucks and buses throughout. Bought in the 1992 privatisation (Wikipedia, 18 Oct 2026).
- Insider trades are trivial (7 shares in 2019, 100 in 2022, one filing revoked).

## Promises against delivery

## Capital decisions
- No acquisition, rights issue or large expansion since 2018. Capex was Rs1.5bn in FY26.
- The main decision was to repay debt (Rs1.35bn at FY24 to nil) and hold the surplus: Rs15.4bn in cash and Islamic income funds against Rs18.4bn of customer advances (AR 2026 notes 14, 24). Other income was Rs2.15bn against Rs11.5bn pre-tax profit.
- Property: Rs630m of advances for offices and a showroom, and a floor in a project built by related party Business Vision, sold back to it in FY26 for Rs554m (gain Rs104m; AR 2026 note 6.8.4). Small, but off-core and with an insider.
- Zhongtong line (Rs741m advances) and solar (Rs183m): no return yet.

## Minorities
- Dividends: nil for FY24 despite Rs781m profit, Rs10 for FY25 (9% of EPS), Rs12 for FY26 (7%). Earlier years not checked.
- No rights issue in the record; a 2018 bonus (rate inferred, unverified).
- Assembly is outsourced to GAL, a 17.9% holder: FY26 purchases Rs2.5bn, Rs4.7bn of GIL stock held there. GAL also sells JAC trucks, a rival (CBS 265610). Conflict is plausible, not shown to cost GIL.
- Essar Asset Management: dividend and bonus withheld by a Sindh High Court order since 16 Nov 2018 (AR 2026 note 25).

## Disclosure
- Annual reports on time (17 to 21 Sep 2026; early October before) with a six-year summary. Auditor ShineWing Hameed Chaudhri unchanged FY21 to FY26, no qualification found in the OCR text.
- Briefings are slides only with boilerplate outlook, no guidance. Isuzu agreement terms undisclosed (unverified). Material filings are scans.

## Growth engine and what breaks it
- FY26 is a peak: 8,424 market units against 6,498 in FY22, gross margin 23% against 12 to 14% in FY21 to FY22. A 2x (EPS about Rs325) cannot come from Isuzu volume alone. Candidates: Zhongtong, UD Trucks, D-Max (795 units from 295), income on cash. None proven. My view: no clear 2x.
- Breaks it: rates (policy rate 11.5%, inflation 11.07% in June 2026), yen cost, customer advances unwinding, Isuzu relationship, JAC and Hino.

## Scores (0 to 5)
- Promises vs delivery 3: leadership kept, share lost twice, diversification late.
- Capital allocation 3: debt-free, small capex, but no proven return on new lines.
- Minority treatment 2: 7 to 9% payout on a large cash pile, a nil year, related-party dealings, withheld-share dispute.
- Disclosure 3: timely and clean audit, no Q&A or guidance, scanned filings.
- Overall 11 of 20.

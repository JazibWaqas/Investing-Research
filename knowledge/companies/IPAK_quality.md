International Packaging Films (BOPP/CPP/BOPET film). Sources: PSX docs in data/raw/filings/quality/IPAK/ (AR = annual report; CB = briefing slides; ids in brackets), quarterly_eps.csv, press. Price Rs31.72, P/E 4.7 (stockanalysis, 16 Oct 2026).

## People

- **Naveed Godil, CEO and founder (2015), 13.14% holder.** Ex-financial markets, in packaging since 2010; re-appointed for 3 years from 31 Oct 2025 [263323]. Lead sponsors on the board (Tejani, Pirani, Raja, Iqbal, Fazal ur Rehman) with spouses hold about 43% (AR2026). Free float 29%.
- **Muhammed Amin, chairman**, non-executive, ex-CEO Gillette Pakistan and Mondelez Pakistan (AR2026). Three of ten directors are independent (Saad Amanullah Khan chairs audit; Sarfaraz Rehman; Saira Nasir).
- CFO changed Aug 2025 [257432]; Mohsin Anwer, CFO in AR2024, is now Chief Strategy Officer (AR2026).
- Record: two lines built roughly on schedule (PETPAK BOPET 5 Feb 2024, GPAK BOPP April 2024; press, unverified against the prospectus), then two years of financing and ramp-up pain.

## Capital decisions and returns

- **GPAK and PETPAK expansion** (most of Rs27bn consolidated PP&E, debt-funded when the policy rate was 20%+): consolidated debt Rs15.0bn by Mar 2025, finance cost Rs1.8-2.2bn a year. EBIT of Rs3.8bn on that base in FY25 earned roughly the cost of debt; FY26 EBIT Rs8.1bn earns about 20%. One year of returns above cost of capital, and not yet proven repeatable.
- **PETPAK rights:** group (IPAK 47%, GPAK 53%) took the whole Rs2.13bn at par although book value per share was below par; the 48% partner paid Rs141.6m (AR2026 cash flow). Stake rose to 61.6%. Fairness unverified.
- **IPO:** 70.1m fresh shares at Rs25.16, retail discount to Rs24 (AR2024). Allottees are up about 40% including bonus and dividends (my arithmetic).
- **2026 adds:** Rs1.4bn metallizer [280205], Portugal subsidiary [280607]: small, value-added, sensible.

## Minorities

- Fresh issue, not a sponsor sell-down. Dividends: nil FY24, Rs0.60 plus 5% bonus FY25, Rs2.00 FY26 (30% of EPS). Standalone payout is capped by a Rs3.26bn non-distributable reserve for subsidiaries (AR2025).
- Related-party sales of about Rs3.6bn a year to Universal and Saima packaging firms (common directors); Rs1.6bn receivable at June 2026, 17% of trade receivables (AR2026). Ownership of those firms is unverified.
- Rs22bn of cross-guarantees from the listed parent to subsidiaries (AR2026).
- Insider dealings: unverified. insider_trades.csv has no IPAK rows; 13 director-interest filings since 2025, nine in Apr-Sep 2026, are unparsed (no document ids).

## Disclosure

Three briefings with slides since listing (Dec 2024, Jun 2025, Feb 2026), a letter for each event, results within days of board meetings, A. F. Ferguson unchanged, Best Corporate Report certificate FY24. Against that: quarterly report [264256] and AGM minutes [267566] each revoked once in late 2025; AR2026 text is partly unreadable on extraction; results releases quote profit "excluding deferred tax" (Rs5.4bn vs 4.95bn, [282487]); utilisation definitions shifted between decks.

## Growth engine, next five years

Doubling needs more than volume. Q4 FY26 earned Rs2.4bn, 48% of the year, on revenue only 7% above Q3 (quarterly_eps.csv); cause unverified (one-offs, an Iran-war price spike, or both). On Q1-Q3 run-rate, EPS is about Rs4.7. Levers: utilisation 75% to 90%, metallised and specialty mix, exports from 25% towards 40%, PETPAK profit, falling interest. My estimate: 1.3-1.6x on the run-rate by FY31, unverified. Breakers: the company's own slides show domestic BOPP demand about 78kt against saleable capacity of 158-196kt; tax holidays end (CPAK credit expired Mar 2026; GPAK and PETPAK exemptions will lapse); polymer and freight swings; Rs22bn guarantees if PETPAK stumbles.

## Red flags

1. Profit concentrated in one quarter; operating cash flow Rs2.2bn against Rs4.95bn profit, receivables up Rs1.8bn and inventory up Rs2.1bn.
2. Structural domestic overcapacity (own slides).
3. Heavy associate sales and a 48% PETPAK partner that did not follow its rights.
4. Two of three prospectus EPS targets missed by over 50%.
5. Float only 29%.

## Scores (0 to 5)

- Minority treatment: **3**. Fair IPO, rising payout; associate sales, guarantees and thin float.
- Disclosure quality: **4**. Frequent and timely; marred by revocations.

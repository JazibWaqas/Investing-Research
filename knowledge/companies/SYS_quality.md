## People
- **Aezaz Hussain**, founder (1977) and non-executive Chairman; also Chairman of Visionet Systems, a related party (AR25 p.8).
- **Asif Peer**, CEO and MD, 30+ years in the group, Sitara-e-Imtiaz, holds 7.06% (AR25 note 36.3). Under him USD revenue went from $94m (2021) to $286m (2025), 32% CAGR (Q1'26 deck).
- **Arshad Masood**, non-executive director, holds 13.09%, ex-Visionet (AR25). CFO Roohi Khan (ex-PepsiCo), COO Asif Akram.
- Directors and CEO hold 26.1%; foreign holders 16.1% (AR25, 31 Dec 2025). Auditor A.F. Ferguson throughout (AR22 to AR25).

## Capital decisions
- **NDC (Jul 2022)**: banking software, now a BFS vertical. Price not retrieved (unverified). It sold the CLOS licence for $8m plus about $7m royalties, covering its $15m revalued cost (AR22), and added Temenos rights costing $15m over five years. NDC goodwill is Rs4.9bn (AR25 note 8.4); its earnings are not disclosed separately, so the return is unverified.
- **Minority bets**: Jomo, Jugnu, Retailistan, EP Systems. Small, loss-making, partly written off. Systems' loan to EP Systems is being converted to preference shares; Hussain, Peer and Masood are on the EP B.V. side (AR25 s.199 notice).
- **BAT SAA Services (Oct 2025)**: Rs3.43bn cash, Rs2.56bn booked as customer relationship (AR25 note 8.2.1). Shared-services contract, with Accenture as partner. Unproven.
- **Confiz (merger, effective 1 Jan 2026)**: share swap, court sanction 6 May 2026 (PSX notice 277865). Shares outstanding rose from 1,473m to 1,531m (CSV), about 4%; I link this to Confiz without a filing, so unverified.
- ROE is high but drifting down: 44% (2021), 38%, 31%, 21%, 25% (2025) (sa_ratios.csv).

## Minorities
- No rights issues in `corporate_actions.csv`. Bonus 15% (2019), 10% (2021), 100% (2022), 5-for-1 split (Jun 2025).
- Payout is low: Rs2 per post-split share on Rs7.52 EPS, about 27% (AR25 note 43.2). Cash is reinvested, including in the weak bets above.
- **Related-party concentration**: two customers were 48.1% of standalone revenue in 2025 (47.9% in 2024), and the note says no concentration risk "as these customers are related parties" (AR25 credit-risk note). Visionet Systems Inc alone paid Rs12.8bn (Rs11.5bn in 2024), a company with common directors (AR25 note 36). Arm's-length pricing is unverified.
- **CEO sale**: Peer sold 10m shares at Rs172 (Rs1.72bn) on 8 Jan 2026, about 9% of his stake, near the 52-week high of 174.40. Price is now 118.37 (stockanalysis, 16 Oct 2026). An executive sold smaller amounts in Sep 2026.

## Disclosure
Strong: a briefing and deck after most results; detailed related-party and goodwill notes; text-searchable reports. Weak: repeated REVOKED filings (H1 2019 report; Oct 2024 deck; four revoked briefing notices and video recordings in May to Jul 2026), AGM time extensions in 2023 and 2026, and the FY25 briefing held 3 Jun 2026 versus 10 Apr the year before.

## Growth engine and what breaks it
Export IT services through partners (Microsoft, Temenos, SAP), 91% of revenue in foreign currency, Saudi/UK/APAC expansion, M&A, AI. Earnings were Rs11.0bn in 2025; doubling in five years needs about 15% a year. Breakers: rupee appreciation or wage inflation (cost is 57% PKR), AI cutting billable hours, failed integration of Confiz/BAT, and concentration on related parties.

## Red flags
1. 48% of standalone revenue from two related parties.
2. CEO sold Rs1.7bn near the high; price fell about 30% afterwards.
3. Start-up write-offs; director-linked EP Systems financing.
4. "No new markets" stated twice, then new markets entered.
5. Net margin 33% (H2 2021) to 11% (2024), only 14% in 2025.

- **Capital allocation: 3.** Organic build earns 25%+ ROE; start-ups lost; BAT/Confiz untested; NDC return unverified.

# EPCL (Engro Polymer & Chemicals): underwriting card

Written 17 Oct 2026. Price Rs31.77 (16 Oct close, data/prices_raw/EPCL.csv). KMI All Share member (data/kmi_allshare_2026-06-05.csv). Verdict: **avoid at Rs31.77**. Buy only below Rs27, or if the offer terms improve.

## The story

EPCL is Pakistan's only integrated PVC producer. About 80% of revenue is PVC, 19% caustic soda and 1 to 3% hydrogen peroxide (H1 2026 briefing, doc 281225). It earns the "core delta", PVC price less ethylene and EDC. That delta fell to $273/t in Q2 2026, down 18% on Q1, because ethylene rose 35% while PVC rose only 18%. Management says PVC prices peaked in March and drifted lower through Q2. The cycle has been brutal. EPS ran 12.46, 9.67 and 7.39 in FY21 to FY23, then -0.40 in FY24 and -4.29 in FY25. H1 2026 turned back to a Rs1.6bn profit, but Rs1.3bn of that was one remeasurement gain.

The interest now is the change of control, not the PVC cycle. Lotte Chemical Pakistan, renamed Noventra on 5 Oct (doc 284637), signed an SPA on 31 Aug to buy Engro Corp's 510.73m shares (56.19%) at Rs38.60, Rs19.7bn in total (Engro Holdings, doc 282058). Liberty Daharki Power is a person acting in concert under the same owner, Shaheryar Chishty, and is buying 8.8% from Mitsubishi. That is why the minority public offer is only **159.09m shares, 17.5%** (PAI, doc 272303, 10 Mar). The deadline for the Public Announcement of Offer was extended once, to **7 Dec 2026**, because regulatory approvals are pending (doc 282317, 7 Sep).

On fundamentals EPCL is expensive. It has trough-cycle earnings, 1.3x book on a 4% ROE, and Rs40bn of net debt at an 11.5% policy rate that may rise. Interest of about Rs5 per share a year eats most of a mid-cycle profit. Today's price rests on the deal, so the right question is what one minority share is worth when the deal is paid out pro rata.

**Pro rata math.** Shares that can be tendered: 908.92m less Engro's 510.73m less Liberty Daharki's about 80.0m = 318.2m. The offer buys 159.09m, which is exactly 50% of that pool. If everyone tenders, acceptance is 50%. Nadeem Nisar holds 91.2m (10.03%) and may not tender (his intent is unverified). Seagreen bought about 2.2% from Mitsubishi. If neither tenders, acceptance rises to about 77%. I use 60% in the base case. The rest of the holding stays in a company with a leveraged new parent and about 17.5% free float. If all of it were tendered at Rs38.60 the gain would be 21%. Pro rata, the realistic payoff is 0.6 x 38.60 + 0.4 x (post-offer price).

## What is coming and when

- **20 Oct 2026:** Q3 results, 2:30pm (doc 284997). Frozen prediction: 55% that EPS comes in below the Q2 figure of Rs1.04. Noventra's Q3 gross profit fell to Rs0.50bn from Rs2.70bn in Q2 (doc 285750).
- **26 Oct:** SBP MPC. Topline's survey has 61% expecting a hike, which hurts a Rs40bn net debt balance sheet.
- **About 23 Nov:** the provisional PVC anti-dumping duty on the US and Indonesia lapses unless it is finalised.
- **By 7 Dec:** PAO deadline. A PAO sets the offer price, needs funding backed by a bank guarantee and opens the acceptance window. Whether a second extension is allowed is unverified.
- **14 Dec:** MPC.
- **Dec 2026 to Mar 2027:** offer acceptance and settlement if the PAO is made (timing inferred). Noventra has Rs2.2bn of cash and short-term investments against Rs25.8bn needed (Rs19.7bn SPA plus Rs6.1bn offer). Its authorised capital doubled to Rs40bn on 22 Sep, so a rights issue is likely (inferred).
- **About mid-Feb 2027:** FY26 results.
- **Dividends:** the last cash dividend was Rs1.0, around March 2024 (data/corporate_actions.csv). PSX payouts show nothing since, and H1 2026 was nil. I expect nothing at the next four likely ex-months: Feb/Mar 2027 final, Apr 2027, Aug 2027 and Oct 2027 interims. That is Rs0 net of 15% WHT. A leveraged new parent could push for upstream dividends, but net debt makes that unlikely within 12 months.

## How much it can move

- **Bear Rs23, 25%.** The deal is delayed past 12 months or collapses: the PAI is withdrawn, funding fails or approvals are refused. The stock trades on fundamentals at about 0.95x book of 24.2, close to the 27.3 to 29 range it held in late 2025 before the deal, less a Q3 and rates drag.
- **Base Rs33.5, 50%.** The deal completes at Rs38.60 with 60% acceptance. The remaining 40% trades at Rs26, about 1.07x book, typical of a thin-float subsidiary of a leveraged parent. 0.6 x 38.6 + 0.4 x 26 = 33.56.
- **Bull Rs37, 25%.** 75% acceptance, the rest at Rs32, helped by a final PVC duty or a lower float. 0.75 x 38.6 + 0.25 x 32 = 36.95. A competing bid under Regulation 17 would push this higher, but none has been seen.

Probability-weighted value: 0.25 x 23 + 0.5 x 33.5 + 0.25 x 37 = **Rs31.75**. With no dividends, the expected 12-month return is **-0.1%**. The 6-month T-bill yields 12.4%. Even the base case gives only +5.4% over about five months, before 15% CGT, with a quarter of the probability on -28%.

## What kills it

1. **7 Dec passes with no PAO, or the PAI is withdrawn.** Watch PSX EPCL and Noventra announcements. The price goes toward the bear case.
2. **Noventra cannot fund the deal.** Watch for no rights issue or bank guarantee before the PAO, or a rights issue that is undersubscribed. Noventra's 9M pre-tax profit of Rs7.9bn included Rs4.1bn of other income, and Q3 operating profit was only Rs0.14bn.
3. **Q3 loss on 20 Oct with the core delta under $250/t.** This lowers the post-offer fundamental value.
4. **The PVC anti-dumping duty lapses on about 23 Nov, or the MPC hikes 100bp.**
5. **On the upside, the avoid call is wrong if** the PAO carries a price above Rs38.60, a larger offer, or a rival bid. Watch the PAO text.

# HALEON quality (management and sponsor), scored 18 Oct 2026

Haleon Pakistan Ltd (ex GSK Consumer Healthcare Pakistan). Sponsor Haleon plc via Haleon Netherlands B.V. holds 85.79% (100.4m of 117m shares), so free float is about 14% [AR2025 Directors' Report, 7 Apr 2026]. No prior card exists.

## People
- CEO Qawi Naseer: joined 28 Apr 2025, CEO from 1 Jun 2025 [PSX 251293, 254639]. Described as a consumer-goods growth leader; prior record unverified. Under him: EPS 2025 Rs54.45, TTM Rs56.41, Jamshoro line expansions announced (Jan 2026).
- Predecessor Farhan Haroon: GSK 12+ years, CEO by the 2022 report [AR2022], transferred to Haleon South Africa and now Chairperson [PSX 254640]. Presided over the margin recovery (gross margin 20% in 2023 to 39% in 2025).
- CFO Babar Sharif (11 years in company) replaced Dilawar Meghani on 15 Sep 2025, who moved to a group role [PSX 259082]. Head of Internal Audit has turned over repeatedly (interim in 2025, replaced again 27 Aug 2026) [PSX 281744].
- Pattern: seconded group executives rotate every 2 to 3 years. Continuity is the brands and plant, not the people. Board has 3 independents; chair is now a group nominee (an independent chaired until Mar 2025).

## Capital decisions
Capex was Rs1.8bn (2023) and Rs2.0bn (2025), roughly 4 to 5% of sales, funded from cash; no debt. ROE 43% in 2025 [AR2025]. The Panadol insourcing (feasibility 2019, approved 2023) is the one big bet; return unproven until volume moves in-house. There were no acquisitions. Cash Rs7.2bn, about 9% of market cap, sits idle. Earnings quality: operating cash flow 2025 was about Rs5.6bn against profit Rs6.4bn [quarterly_eps.csv].

## Minorities
- Zero dividends for 2021 to 2023 (EPS 18.2, 2.8, 8.5) citing macro risk and Rs1.5bn of unpaid foreign-holder dividends blocked by SBP registration [AR2022]. The parent was the owed party, not a minority loser, but minorities waited three years.
- Payout now about 55% (Rs30 on EPS 54.45). The parent also received Rs3.0bn of 2025 dividends pro rata.
- Related-party goods purchased from the group doubled to Rs2.2bn (2025) from Rs1.05bn [AR2025 note 37]. No royalty line is visible; unverified in full. OTC products still run through GlaxoSmithKline Pakistan against a service fee, as DRAP marketing-authorisation transfer remains pending since 2022 [AR2025 note 1.2].
- No buyback, no rights issue. A shareholder asked at the 28 Apr 2026 briefing about "maintaining its listing"; the answer is not in the filing. Delisting risk is unverified but is the main tail for an 86% parent.

## Disclosure
Annual reports are text-searchable and on time (Feb to Apr). Two briefings a year (Nov and Apr) with slides and advance analyst questions; answers appear only as video, no transcript. Material events are filed promptly (capex, CEO change in 6 weeks' notice). Weaknesses: the 2025 Directors' Report prints "compared to Rs39.11 in 2025" (typo), and internal-audit churn. KPMG has been auditor throughout.

## Growth engine (next 5 years)
EPS doubling needs about 15% a year. Levers: Panadol and CaC-1000 are 82% of OTC; non-essential (deregulated, 45% of OTC) pricing; Sensodyne and Parodontax FMCG +38%; Centrum; 8bn-tablet capacity. Revenue grew 17% a year 2020 to 2025. Breakers: gross margin has already doubled from 20% to 39%, so most recovery is done; paracetamol and fuel costs (briefing flagged oil, war surcharge); DRAP price controls on essential medicines; rupee; parent extracting cash. Q2 2026 EPS 14.32 was only +3% year on year. Price fell from 1,010 (52-week high) to 684 (16 Oct 2026) [stockanalysis.com]; cause unverified.

## Red flags
1. 86% parent, 14% float; listing question unanswered.
2. Export and project promises missed or late.
3. Management rotates; chair is a group nominee.
4. Related-party purchases doubling; service dependence on GSK Pakistan since 2022.
5. Pending tax demands (Rs392m, Rs287m, others), no provisions; mostly won at first appeal.

# FATIMA quality score (Fatima Fertilizer), 18 Oct 2026

Sources: PSX filings (ids in brackets, saved under `data/raw/filings/quality/FATIMA/`): annual reports 2021 to 2025 (text, readable), corporate briefing decks Dec 2022, Dec 2023, Dec 2024, Dec 2025 and May 2026, material-information letters; `quarterly_eps.csv`, `sa_ratios.csv`; stockanalysis.com (16 Oct 2026); Nukta (26 Dec 2025). No FATIMA card existed. Consolidated figures unless stated.

## People
- Joint venture of the Fatima Group (Mukhtar family) and Arif Habib Group. Arif Habib is chairman, personally 12.33% and Arif Habib Corporation 15.19% [AR 2025 p.347]. The Fatima side holds the rest of the sponsor block (exact split unverified).
- Fawad Ahmed Mukhtar: CEO and director, "35+ years" building the family business [AR 2025]. Both men re-appointed for three years from 22 Sep 2026 [284818]. Board of seven, four of them sponsors, two independents [283183]. Arif Habib's group sits on the board of FATIMA and of the counterparties in the REIT and PIA deals.
- Management bench: Asad Murad (COO, and CFO until the change of 24 Jan 2025 [announcements]); CFO also changed Feb 2021. Continuity is high; this is a family-and-partner enterprise, not a hired-CEO company.
- Record: built the Sadiqabad plant (US$750m greenfield, 2006 to 2009), bought Pakarab (2005) and DH Fertilizers (2015) cheaply, and lifted capacity to 2.57Mt, 30% of national urea-plus-NP capacity [Dec 2022 deck].

## Capital decisions
- Pakarab (2020 take-over) and Sheikhupura gas: the core earned well, ROE 14% (2022), 20%, 28%, 27% (2021 to 2025) [sa_ratios].
- Since 2022 the cash goes elsewhere: Rs15bn into three REITs run by Arif Habib Dolmen (a related party), 33% of NRL and 32% of Globacore (copper/gold exploration, 3 to 4 years to the next phase), 25% of Buraq/KT digital bank, Agritech instruments, and E&P stakes (Marwat, Harnai South, Ratana, Peshawar, two offshore blocks with Mari) [AR 2025, 257019, 260576, 266787]. None has reported earnings; management itself said none is material [Nukta, Dec 2025].
- PIA: Arif Habib-led consortium bid Rs135bn for 75%, SPA signed 29 Jan 2026; Fatima approved up to Rs50bn (about 17% of market cap, 1.2 years of profit), funded from liquidity or credit lines, with a further Rs45bn option at 12% a year premium [267715, AR 2025 note]. Returns unknowable until 2027.
- Finance cost +56% in 2025 from "an arbitrage opportunity": borrowing to hold securities; other income Rs16.2bn is 38% of PAT, while operating profit fell 1%.

## Minorities
- No rights or bonus issue in the record seen (`corporate_actions.csv` covers only 2025 to 2026; earlier unverified). Share count flat at 2.1bn.
- Payout: 62% (2022), 48%, 32%, 39% (2025, Rs6); H1 2026 cash dividend nil, citing "capital commitments" [281401, press]. Dividend is being traded for the sponsor-led PIA bet.
- Related parties: REITs and PIA with Arif Habib entities, loans to sponsor-group Reliance Commodities (Rs1.2bn at KIBOR+1.25%), a "loan from directors" line (size unverified), non-voting preference shares to Pakarab holders. All disclosed and priced; none dilutes ordinary holders.
- Insider file has one 2019 line; the many director-dealing notices are unparsed (unverified).

## Disclosure
Annual reports are text and detailed; results within two months; December briefing each year with slides, a second one in May 2026 with a video. Material-information letters are prompt (PIA, blocks, Samba). Negatives: reasons for the Samba withdrawal and Multan reversal not given in the filings seen; no Q&A transcript; the Q4 2025 slide deck calls the diversification list "all set" while the CEO calls it immaterial.

## Growth engine
Fertilizer is capped near 2.57Mt, with 111% utilisation in 2025. Earnings 2x in five years needs the new businesses: PIA turnaround, E&P and mining. Stand-alone, EPS of Rs20 (2025) rising to 25 needs gas pressure fixed and share gains held. What breaks it: PEF slipping again, a PIA loss or a cash call beyond Rs50bn, dividends staying nil, inventory build, and Rs1.1bn-a-year donations.

## Red flags
Cash conversion: OCF about Rs5bn in 2024 and Rs28bn in 2025 against profit of Rs36bn and Rs42bn (quarterly file, unverified in the statements); reversed restructuring; borrowing to run a securities book; dividend cut for a related-party airline deal; Arif Habib on both sides; Q2 2026 EPS 8.19 after Q1 1.54 (gains? unverified).

## Scores (0 to 5)

- Capital allocation 2: core excellent, new Rs50bn+ of capital in unproven sectors without disclosed returns.
- Minority treatment 3: no dilution and priced related parties, but payout cut to zero.
- Disclosure 4: timely and detailed text reports; reasons for reversals missing.

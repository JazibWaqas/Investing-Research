# LUCK: Lucky Cement. Underwriting card

> Kept as knowledge. LUCK and all Lucky group companies are not eligible for Jazib (declaration duty through his father's employer, 21 Oct 2026).

As of 2026-10-17. Price Rs400.56 (16 Oct close). KMI All Share member (list of 5 Jun 2026), also KMI-30. **Verdict: WATCH. Buy at Rs345 or below.** It is a good group, not a cheap one. The holdco-discount and PIA angles in the starting brief do not hold up.

## The story

Lucky is Pakistan's largest cement maker: 15.6 Mtpa after the Karachi upgrade (doc 279395, 1 Jul 2026) and 19% of national despatches. Around the cement business it owns a group: 55% of Lucky Core Industries (LCI), the 660 MW Thar-lignite plant Lucky Electric (LEPCL), Lucky Motor (KIA, Peugeot, GAC, Samsung phones), cement joint ventures in Iraq and Congo, and 33% of National Resources (copper and gold exploration in Chagai).

FY26 was a record year. Consolidated EPS was Rs60.78 (+15.7%). Standalone EPS was Rs31.83 (+41%), helped by a 37.5% gross margin and Rs15.8bn of dividends from subsidiaries, Rs12bn of it from LEPCL (doc 280930). The stock has not followed. It is down 12% over a year and 22% from its Rs512.7 high, because cement now trades as a proxy for oil and interest rates (cement sector note).

Fact-check on the brief. **The PIA deal is dead.** The Lucky-led consortium lost on 23 Dec 2025, bidding Rs134bn against Arif Habib's Rs135bn. The live deal is the power-distribution sell-off. With Hub Power Holdings, Kohat Cement and Metro Ventures, Lucky is pre-qualified for FESCO (doc 281942, 28 Aug) and GEPCO (doc 284989, 7 Oct), with no binding commitment yet. Both are capital sinks, not value unlocks.

The rough part is the multiples, not the inputs. The real discount is the 8% payout. Minorities get 1.25% a year while the cash goes into PIA bids, DISCOs, mining and Congo. That discount is deserved.

## What kills it

1. **Overpaying for a DISCO.** Trigger: the consortium wins FESCO or GEPCO and Lucky's board approves an equity commitment above about Rs50bn (a board or material-information filing).
2. **Iraq JV earnings collapse.** Trigger: the quarterly share of JV and associate profit falls below Rs3bn (it was Rs2.72bn in 3QFY26).
3. **Cement margin squeeze.** Trigger: standalone gross margin below 30% in 1Q or 2QFY27 (37.5% in FY26), or Newcastle coal above US$140.
4. **Rates.** Trigger: SBP hikes 100bp or more on 26 Oct or 14 Dec.
5. **Payout stays at 8%.** Trigger: the FY27 final is below Rs5, which confirms minorities fund the expansion.

## Red team (17 Oct 2026)

**Final: WEAKENED. Corrected expected 12-month return about +3% (range +2% to +5%), not +8%. Verdict stays WATCH; the Rs345 entry still works (about +20% on the corrected values).**

**What survived.** Share count and EPS basis are right (1,465m shares x Rs400.56 = Rs586.8bn; consolidated Rs60.78, not the screener's standalone figure). The Rs5 dividend is trivially affordable: about Rs7.3bn against Rs15.8bn of subsidiary dividends alone, and the cash dividend was credited on 9 Oct 2026. Liquidity is no issue: median traded value since 16 Apr is Rs571m a day (minimum Rs89m), so Rs12k does not move it. `data/announcements.parquet` has 26 LUCK items in 2026 and none is a suspension, unusual-movement, non-compliance or auditor-change notice, and none mentions a sponsor pledge. Only the AGM resolutions are filed on the standard schedule. Lucky Exim (an associate) bought 140k shares at Rs440 on 19 Aug and is now about 9% under water, which is mild insider conviction the card does not weigh. Six director or executive trade disclosures were filed between 11 and 29 Sep, with direction unread (already listed as unverified).

**Price check (split and dividend adjusted panel).** Since 16 Apr LUCK is -6.9% against a cement median of -7.7% (DGKC -7.5%, MLCF -4.5%, CHCC -9.6%, KOHC -11.1%, FCCL +2.2%) and a KMI-universe median of -0.5%. It is down 18% from Jan. The 20 Oct to 28 Oct catalysts are not priced in, but the sector is in a downtrend and LUCK is not cheaper than peers, so there is no dislocation to harvest.

**Three strongest objections.**

1. **The probabilities are not honest; the bull is a conjunction.** Rs560 needs Brent below US$90, SBP cuts and a market re-rate at once. Our own rate test (`tests/rate_cycle_episodes.md`) has the 6M bill 90bp above policy, every cut-signal variant OFF, and a frozen 97% prediction of no cut on 26 Oct. The 27 Apr hike starts a hike cycle, and complete first-hike cycles show EW -15.6% at 120 days (n=2, descriptive only). The sector test (`tests/sector_momentum_regime.md`) calls the regime-list link "not supported", so the cement-likes-cuts story is unproven in both directions. Bull +40% at 25% against bear -23% at 25% gives a favourable skew only if both are equally likely. A 30/55/15 split gives Rs409 and +3.2% with dividends.
2. **EPS quality is flattered.** Normalising only the tax rate (Rs58) leaves in the 4Q Iraq JV rebound and about Rs21bn of other income, which is largely interest on Rs182bn of cash. That is roughly Rs11 to 14 a share after tax, up to a quarter of EPS (my inference, not in the filings). A cut lowers it, and a DISCO equity cheque (Rs50bn plus) consumes the cash that earns it. The bull case needs cuts and keeps the income. A base of Rs62 is above the card's own normalised Rs58.
3. **No test supports a results or news pop.** `tests/calendar_effects.md` finds no results-season edge, and the random-entry placebo is -1.5% median with a 43% win rate (`tests/seed_audit.md`). Sector momentum is weak (t about 1.3). So the 27-28 Oct results are not a reason to buy early; they are a data point for the Rs345 plan. The base rate for a single stock holding up after a 12% 12-month drop is poor, and the card's own best argument is the entry price, not a catalyst.

Not objections: no auditor, pledge or compliance flags; no results-date risk to the dividend. Unchanged unverified items: consensus Rs72, 4Q one-off size, ownership percentages.

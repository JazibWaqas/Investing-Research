## Big capital decisions

- **Apparel master project (Rs29bn) and Plant 6:** drove sales 3.3x in five years, but equity and assets also tripled (assets Rs61bn to Rs188bn, FY21 to FY26) while net profit only about doubled (Rs6bn to Rs13bn). ROE fell from 46% (FY23, helped by Rs8.4bn FX gain) to about 22% (my estimate from CBS2026). Debt-funded: interest cover 2.0x in FY25, 4.8x FY26.
- **Top Circle (US/China socks), 64% from 1 Nov 2023:** cost Rs1.73bn, booked a Rs857m purchase gain (AR2024 [238254]). FY26: revenue Rs5.7bn vs Rs8.7bn, loss Rs703m (AR2026). Poor so far.
- **Bonnie Doon brand (June 2025):** bought by Textile Hub B.V., described as an associate of ILP [255237]. ILP's stake and price unverified.

## Disclosure

Annual report 8 to 12 weeks after year end, results about six weeks after quarter ends, annual briefing every year since 2020 with slides, auditor Kreston Hyder Bhimji since at least FY22 (no change), VIS rating upgraded 2024 [234221]. Weaknesses: no transcripts; "Questions received in advance: Nil" (CBS2026); gearing for FY23 is 39.5% in the 2023 deck and about 56% in the 2024 deck (definition change, unverified); share-volume clarifications (27 Sep 2024 [237631], 19 Jun 2026 [278868]) are scanned; a sponsor-side associate buying a brand.

## Growth engine, next five years

Earnings doubling is plausible, not given. Levers: apparel ramp (1.1m to 1.6m garments by end FY26, CBS2025), 7 more denim lines, Plant 6, a margin return from FY25's 3% net toward FY24's 10%. If revenue grew 8% a year at a 9.5% net margin, EPS would be about Rs19 (2x FY26, my estimate). Breakers: US tariffs and consumer demand, a stable or rising rupee against Pakistan's "highest cost in the region", normal tax regime, EFS removal, Hormuz energy shocks, 57% gearing.

## Red flags

1. FY25 profit down 66% after Rs29bn apparel build; apparel only 17% of FY26 sales.
2. Top Circle losing money.
3. Gearing 56 to 63% for five years.
4. 69.6% sponsor holding, thin float, group of 23 outside companies trading with ILP.
5. No post-2025 strategy published.
6. Associate-level deals disclosed as ILP news.

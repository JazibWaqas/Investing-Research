# MARI (Mari Energies, ex Mari Petroleum): management and business quality (18 Oct 2026)

Sources: annual reports FY22 to FY26 (PSX docs 191752, 214502, 236240, 258895, 282238), briefing slides Oct 2023 to Jun 2026 (CBS; 217941, 239675, 255501, 262102, 279232), PSX notices by id. Files in data/raw/filings/quality/ (prefix MARI_). Slides only, no transcripts. Year ends 30 June. No MARI.md card exists.

## People

- **Ownership:** Fauji Foundation 40% with management rights; Government 20%; OGDCL 20%; public 20% (CBS 30 Jun 2026). Thin float.
- **Chairman:** Lt Gen Anwar Ali Hyder (Retd), also MD/CEO of Fauji Foundation, since 5 Apr 2024 [228640], reappointed 24 Jun 2025 [255578]; replaced Waqar Ahmed Malik.
- **MD/CEO:** Faheem Haider, petroleum engineer (Imperial College), 27+ years at Union Texas, OMV, BG, Neptune; appointed 12 Aug 2020 [150765], reappointed one year in Aug 2023 [213030], then three years from Aug 2024 [233447]. Not a Fauji officer. Record: capacity 104 to 136 kboepd, reserves and resources 568 to 1,029 MMBOE, EPS 26.2 (FY21) to 72.5 (FY26) (AR2026).
- Insiders: insider_trades.csv shows no sponsor or CEO trades; executives bought about Rs48m (2024) and Rs23m (2025). Not a signal.

## Big capital decisions

- **Exploration and fields:** capex Rs72.6bn in FY26 against operating cash Rs95.8bn (AR2026). It bought the reserves, but earnings did not rise (below).
- **Abu Dhabi (PIOL, 25%):** US$100m fully invested (Rs24.8bn) by Jun 2026. No revenue; return unknown.
- **Minerals, data centres, GHG capture:** Rs12.5bn into subsidiaries in FY25, Rs4.1bn in FY26, Rs7.5bn more approved for minerals. No profit shown.
- **15% extra wellhead payment** to renew the Mari lease to 2029: Rs16.5bn in 9M FY26 versus Rs9.0bn (CBS Jun 2026).

## Minorities

- Payout fell from about 60% (FY21) and 50% (FY22) to 40% (FY24, FY25) and 37% (FY26, Rs27 on EPS 72.5) (AR2026 ten-year table). Retained cash funds the bets above.
- 800% bonus Sep 2024 (cosmetic); no rights issue since 2018 (corporate_actions.csv); no purchases from the sponsor. Gas sales to Fauji group (FFC Rs38bn) are at notified prices; Rs1.6bn interest income from Askari Bank, a group lender.
- FY26 profit of Rs87.1bn includes Rs17.2bn super tax reversal (court judgment 27 Jan 2026) and Rs5.7bn Rule 35 reversal under a stay (AR2026 note 34). Pre-tax profit fell 5.6% to Rs83.2bn. Underlying profit about Rs64bn (EPS 53.5): flat on FY25 (54.3), 17% below FY24 (64.4). My arithmetic.

## Disclosure

Results within five weeks (7 Aug 2026), annual report early September, two or three briefings a year, prompt discovery notices, denials of false news (Aug 2024, Jan 2025), award-winning report. Weak spots: no transcripts (video from Oct 2026), half-year result revoked and revised 27 to 28 Jan 2025 [246386, 246466], annual-report notice revoked Sep 2022 [191730], no credit-loss charge on government debt under an SECP exemption (AR2025). Audit opinion text unverified.

## Growth engine, next five years

Volumes are rising (Ghazij to 222 MMscfd for three fertilizer buyers, Shams, Spinwam, Pateji, Soho), yet earnings have not, because the state takes more per unit. Ghazij needs customers to build processing plants (over US$200m, press, Jan 2026). A doubling of EPS is not visible; 1.3 to 1.5x is plausible (my estimate, unverified). Breakers: trade debts Rs86bn (45% of sales, Rs38bn over 90 days), curtailments, oil price and rupee, Shewa security, tax rulings, more wellhead charges.

## Red flags

1. Rs22.8bn of tax reversals flatter FY26; Rs5.7bn is stayed.
2. Trade debts 45% of sales, no credit-loss charge.
3. Dated projects late; the plateau fix (compression) unfinished.
4. Payout cut while cash goes to Abu Dhabi, minerals and data centres.
5. Sponsor chief chairs the board; 80% state-linked holders.

## Scores (0 to 5)

- Promises vs delivery: **4**. Reserves and production beat plan; milestones slipped 1 to 2 years.
- Capital allocation: **3**. Exploration pays; Abu Dhabi and diversification unproven; equity return fell from 39% to 26% (FY24 to FY25).
- Minority treatment: **3**. No dilution or sponsor deals, but payout down to about 40%.
- Disclosure quality: **4**. Fast and frequent; one revoked result, no transcripts.

Overall **14/20**.

Written 18 Oct 2026, before the 22 Oct Q3 result. Price Rs91.49 (close 16 Oct 2026, data/prices_raw/FABL.csv). KMI All Share member (data/kmi_allshare_2026-06-05.csv, row 288). **Verdict: WATCH. ## The story

Faysal converted to a fully Islamic bank on 1 Jan 2023 and runs 900 branches. Ithmaar Bank (Bahrain) owns 66.78%. It is the walk-forward's mechanical bank pick (the highest ROE/PB among Islamic banks with ROE of 15% or more). ROE/PB is simply E/P, so the rule is a low-P/E screen with an ROE floor. That is the trap in lesson 6.

H1 2026 had one real change. Management swapped financing for sukuk: financing fell 7.4% to Rs807bn, investments rose 11.6% to Rs708bn and ADR dropped from 61.1% to 51.7%. Funding also got cheaper. Current accounts rose 21% YoY to Rs646bn, CA mix rose from 37.5% to 41.4% and CASA from 81.9% to 86.8%. Reported earnings were flat: consolidated EPS was 6.82 against 6.87. But H1 needed Rs3.37bn of securities gains (Rs0.21bn a year ago), and net spread fell 3.9% to Rs33.1bn. Q2 alone shows the turn: net spread was up 1.5% YoY (Rs17.49bn against Rs17.24bn), with the 28 April hike only partly in.

**P/B against ROE.** Justified P/B = (ROE − g) / (CoE − g). With CoE 18%, g 9% (55% retention × 16.5%) and core ROE 16.5%, I get 0.83x. At ROE 17.5% it is 0.94x, or Rs65 to 73 on spot book. On the CoE implied by MEBL's own price (about 16.9%), FABL's justified P/B is 0.95 to 1.1x, or Rs74 to 85. Either way FABL trades 1.1 to 1.4x its justified multiple, and MEBL trades about 1.15x. FABL is not cheap against MEBL. Its higher E/P (16% against 9.4%) pays for slower book growth.

**The share-issue application (30 Jun 2026) is not a dilution risk.** The SECP approved up to 107m shares (7.05% of today's count) to be issued other than as rights, under a special resolution at the 26 Mar AGM. They go only to holders of the Rs7bn Tier II Mudaraba sukuk (issued 25 May 2026, 10 years, callable after 5, rated AA). They convert only if SBP declares a point of non-viability. The conversion price is variable and not disclosed; Rs7bn / 107m implies a floor near Rs65. If SBP pulls that trigger, the equity is already impaired. The real signal is the thinner capital (CAR 14.84%).

## What kills it

1. **Easing cycle.** Trigger: the 6M T-bill falls below the policy rate, or SBP cuts. Islamic banks lagged by 48 to 49 points after the 2020 and 2024 first cuts.
2. **Core erosion.** Trigger: Q3 consolidated EPS below 3.0 without securities gains, or net spread down YoY again.
3. **Book and capital.** Trigger: CAR below 13.5%, or another Rs5bn FVOCI hit. Either puts the dividend at risk.
4. **Dividend cut.** Trigger: an interim below Rs1.5.
5. **Credit.** Trigger: infection above 3%, or quarterly provisions instead of reversals. Coverage of 95% leaves less cushion than MEBL.
6. **Sponsor.** Trigger: distress at Ithmaar or DMIT, or a block sold at a discount.

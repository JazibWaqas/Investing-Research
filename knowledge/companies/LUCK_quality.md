CEO Muhammad Ali Tabba (reappointed 11 Oct 2021 and 14 Oct 2024; left the board 25 Apr 2025 but stays CEO; Hassan Tabba, next generation, joined, doc 252277). ## Big capital decisions
- Buybacks: 30.4m pre-split shares, about Rs17.3bn, 9.4% of shares. Today's price is Rs2,003 on the old share basis (Rs400.56 x 5), so they are worth about Rs61bn: 4.6x and 3.2x.
- LCI (55%): carried at Rs9.6bn (AR26 note 7); market value about Rs49bn (card, 16 Oct 2026). About 5x.
- LEPCL: Rs29.9bn equity at cost; Rs12bn dividend to Lucky in FY26 (card, doc 280930). A 40% cash yield on book, but it rests on regulated returns. CPPA-G receivable is Rs28.3bn, Rs17.5bn overdue, Rs7.1bn over 90 days (AR26 note 12.2).
- Pezu line 2 came online at a demand trough: FY26 cement output was 49.7% of capacity (AR26 note 39). Standalone ROE fell to 10.2% in FY23 and recovered to 18.5% in FY25 on lower coal and exports (AR25 ratios).
- Lotte and PIA: walked away rather than overpay.
- Unproven: Lucky Motor (Rs12.9bn cost, earnings unread), National Resources (33%, Rs1.7bn plus up to Rs1.2bn more), Congo plant 2 (due 2Q FY28), FESCO and GEPCO pre-qualification (card).

## Minorities
- Payout is low: Rs5 DPS is 8% of consolidated EPS (Rs60.78), 16% of standalone. DPS rose 3.0, 4.0, 5.0 (FY24 to FY26). Subsidiaries sent Rs15.8bn of dividends up in FY26 against about Rs7.3bn paid out.
- Directors, sponsors and associates hold about 70% and took Rs4.1bn of the Rs5.9bn paid (AR26 note 38), so the family bears the same low payout.
- No rights issue or bonus dilution in the record. The 5-for-1 split (28 Apr 2025) was cosmetic.
- Related-party trade is small (sales Rs568m, donations Rs362m), but Lucky put Rs39.6bn into associate mutual funds in FY26 (Rs24.7bn redeemed; terms unverified).
- Sponsor-side: Yunus Textile Mills bought about Rs0.5bn in Aug and Sep 2025 (Rs417 to 482); Lucky Exim bought 140k at Rs440 (18 Aug 2026); Grandcres sold 44k at Rs500 (Jan 2026).

## Disclosure
Good. Text annual reports, results about 40 days after year-end, a briefing after most results, same-day rumour reply (20 Jun 2025), unmodified opinion (A.F. Ferguson since 2017, partner changed FY23). One revoked buyback notice (18 Nov 2022). PSX letters are scans. No auditor change or non-compliance notice in 2026.

## Growth engine
Doubling EPS in five years needs about 15% a year; recent growth was +44%, +19%, +16%. Levers: Congo (1.31 to 2.91 Mtpa), a full year of the Iraq line, renewables, Thar coal at LEPCL, rate cuts. My estimate (unsourced): 1.6x to 1.9x. What breaks it: Gulf conflict hitting Iraq (plant-damage rumour, 20 Jun 2025) or coal prices; super tax and FED in the June budget; a DISCO cheque that burns the cash earning other income; solar eroding grid demand (AR26 flags it); CPPA arrears.

## Red flags
Thar coal three years late; 8% payout against 23% ROE while cash funds bids; treasury in associate funds; half-empty plants and more capacity; float only about 30%.

## Scores (0 to 5)
- Promises versus delivery 4: projects on time; misses depend on third parties.
- Capital allocation 4: buybacks, LCI, LEPCL paid well; Pezu timing, mining and autos unproven.
- Minority treatment 3: no dilution, rising DPS; low payout and associate dealings cap it.
- Disclosure quality 4: timely, clean audit, no numeric guidance.

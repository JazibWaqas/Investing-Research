# GGL (Ghani Global Holdings) quality scoring, 18 Oct 2026

Holdco with 55.96% of Ghani Chemical (GCIL: industrial and medical gases), 50.10% of Ghani Global Glass (GGGL: pharma glass) and ~56% of Ghani ChemWorld (GCWL: calcium carbide, demerged Apr 2025). Standalone EPS is Rs0.06, so only consolidated numbers matter (FY26 net sales Rs11.2bn). Price Rs19.70, market cap Rs7.0bn, 52-week 14.10 to 28.75 (stockanalysis.com, 16 Oct 2026). Read: annual reports FY22 to FY25, FY26 results (scanned, OCR), briefings Nov 2023, Oct 2024, Nov 2025, PSX notices. The FY26 annual report and Jul 2026 briefing slides are scanned and unread.

## People
Sponsor family Masroor Ahmad Khan (CEO and executive director), Atique Ahmad Khan (chairman) and Hafiz Farooq Ahmad hold 47.19% with spouses and children (AR FY25, 30 Jun 2025). Sponsors guarantee GCIL bank lines (AR FY24 note 22.8) and lent Rs439m to the group in FY26 (consolidated cash flow, FY26 results 29 Sep 2026). No pay to executive directors at holdco (AR FY25). Operating since 2007: five gas plants built; the glass plant lost money FY14 to FY19 before turning profitable (Oct 2024 briefing chart). Board churn is high: director changes in Sep 2024, May, Jul and Nov 2025, May and Jul 2026; internal audit head changed Mar and Jun 2024.

## Capital decisions
Assets rose from Rs17.4bn (FY23) to Rs31.2bn (FY26). FY26 capex Rs2.35bn and FY25 Rs2.60bn exceeded operating cash flow (Rs1.36bn and Rs2.36bn). Net debt is about Rs8.1bn (short-term borrowings Rs5.8bn, up 57% in a year; finance cost Rs768m, up 28%). Receivables jumped 53% to Rs4.46bn on 8% sales growth (about 145 days against 103). Return on capital is roughly 14% (my estimate) against debt cost near 10%: adequate, and the new plants have not run a full year. Oct 2020 rights issue (Rs1.0bn at par, 66:100), bonus shares 10% (Dec 2020) and 15% (Nov 2021). New directions: LPG plant, Mari JV, Saudi glass plant, REIT.

## Minorities
Positives: related-party dealings minimal, guarantee commission 0.10% per quarter (AR FY25 note 48); sponsors lend and guarantee. Concerns: no cash dividend from holdco in any recorded year (Cash Dividend row blank, AR FY25); the FY26 REIT scheme (approved 8 Aug 2026; court sanction pending) issues 75m GGL shares to a GGL subsidiary and 60m to partners of G3 Homes LLP, which holds sponsor real estate (SECP order 8 Apr 2026, board decision 19 Jun 2026); valuation of the injected assets unverified. Articles were altered to allow Class B tracking shares at board discretion (AR FY25), and GGL proposes to pass its GCIL rights to its shareholders (board 5 Oct 2026). Sponsors bought Rs34m in the market in Dec 2021 at Rs25 to 32; the price fell to Rs8.80 by Oct 2024 (AR FY25). 2026 director dealings are small.

## Growth engine and risks
Earnings could double by FY31 if the Hattar gas plant earns tax-exempt margins, carbide reaches the Rs8bn FY27 sales management guided, and the Rs14bn Mari project is built. It breaks if execution slips again, Chinese carbide undercuts on price, receivables keep growing, or debt-funded capex meets higher rates.

## Red flags
- 1 to 2 year slippage on every large project.
- FY25 headline EPS inflated by a bargain purchase gain; FY25 restated for deferred tax.
- Receivables and debt rising faster than sales.
- Real-estate REIT inside a gas and glass holdco, new shares to a sponsor-linked LLP.
- Scanned PSX notices; several revoked filings (Dec 2021, Nov 2022, Feb 2024, Oct 2025).

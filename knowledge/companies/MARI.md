- **Brent-sensitive share.** About 72% (FY26) to 77% (FY27E) of net sales is oil-formula-linked: about 4.5% directly (crude and condensate, Rs8.7bn of Rs191.7bn) and about 68 to 72% through Petroleum Policy 2012 gas prices (my estimate, see section 1). About 23 to 28% is the legacy Mari base-production gas, which is not shown to follow Brent. In earnings terms the linked streams make more than 100% of profit, because the fixed base gas does not cover operating costs; each linked rupee drops through at about 53% after royalty, the Rule 35 charge, profit participation and tax.
- **But the pass-through is small.** The 2012 formula passes 20% of each oil dollar between $70 and $110 and nothing above $110 (C&F basis). The elasticity of the gas price to Brent near $90 is about 0.36, with a lag of 6 to 12 months. Near $105 Arab Light the ceiling is close, so upside is capped and downside is open.
- **Lag.** July to December 2026 gas prices are set (Arab Light $92.2). January to June 2027 prices use roughly June to November 2026 oil, so a fall in Brent after November 2026 barely touches FY27; it hits July to December 2027 (FY28 first half) and January to June 2028.
- **Iran-deal probability (judgment): about 30%** that a Hormuz reopening or deal takes Brent to $75 or below by mid-2027 (range 20 to 40%).

## The story

MariEnergies (ex Mari Petroleum) is Pakistan's largest gas producer by volume and the gas source for over 90% of urea. FY26 sales were a record 41.28 MMBOE (113.1 kboepd). Gas was 305.3 bscf (836 MMscfd, 94.7% from the Mari field), crude and condensate 537,385 barrels (1,472 bpd), LPG 183 tonnes (AR2026 p.270). Fauji Foundation holds 40% with management rights, the Government 20%, OGDCL 20%, public 20%. Quality score 14/20 (MARI_quality.md): reserves beat plans, milestones slip 1 to 2 years, payout down to 37%.

FY26 EPS was Rs72.52, but Rs22.8bn of tax reversals (Rs17.2bn super tax after the 27 Jan 2026 Federal Constitutional Court judgment, Rs5.7bn Rule 35 under a stay) flatter it. Underlying FY26 profit was about Rs64bn, EPS about Rs53.5, effective tax rate 23% (arithmetic from AR2026 statement of profit or loss and notes 34.2, 34.3). Quarterly EPS: Q1 13.15, Q2 10.73, Q3 17.53, Q4 30.95 (the Q4 holds the reversals; quarterly_eps.csv).

The stock is down 17% in a year (market cap Rs757bn, 52-week range Rs544.56 to 765) while Brent is up about 70%. That is the sector note's point: E&P shares trade as Pakistan macro and circular-debt stocks first, oil second (knowledge/sectors/exploration_production.md section 5).

**The Policy 2012 gas formula (section 10.2 and annexures 7 to 9, observed from the policy text in data/raw/filings/pp12/pp12_2020.pdf).** The reference is the C&F price of the imported Arab-Gulf basket, averaged over the first six months of the seven months before the notification period. Apply a sliding scale: 100% of the first 30 dollars, 50% of the rise from 30 to 50, 30% from 50 to 70, 20% from 70 to 110, nothing above 110 (ceiling). That gives an applicable price A of 46 at 70 and 54 at 110. Wellhead price in dollars per MMBtu = zone percentage x A / 5.7, with 66.5% for Zone II (Mari), 69.7% for Zone I, 63.3% for Zone III. Check: Arab Light at 68.4 gives A 45.52 and Zone II 5.31 dollars; at 92.2 it gives A 50.44 and 5.88 dollars, a rise of 10.8%, matching Topline's 10 to 11% for Policy 2012 fields (Nukta, 4 Sep 2026). At 65 it gives 5.19 dollars, close to the 5.04 reported for Ghazij.

**The linked share is my reverse-engineered estimate, not a disclosed figure.** With 836 MMscfd sold, 525 at a base price p and 311 at about 4p, FY26 gas revenue of Rs183bn gives p of about Rs283 per thousand cubic feet and incentive gas of about Rs1,133 (about 4.0 dollars per MMBtu at 282, consistent with the formula). That puts about Rs128bn (70%) of gas revenue and Rs137bn (72%) of net sales on the formula. If the true benchmark or multiple differs, the linked share could be 55 to 75%. Not checked against MARI disclosures; the Oct 2026 briefing slides were not found.

## What else moves FY27

- **Curtailment.** FY26 volumes were hit for most of the year by surplus RLNG, SNGPL pipeline ruptures (SVPF, SGPC, Shewa) and Shewa/Spinwam suspensions, yet sales were a record (AR2026 p.271). Net sales capacity was about 131 kboepd (30 May 2026) and the daily record 132,043 boepd came in June 2026, against 113 for FY26. If curtailment ends, volumes are 10 to 15% above FY26; if it persists, my 890 MMscfd holds. Each 50 MMscfd of linked gas is about Rs10 of EPS a year.
- **Trade debts and the circular-debt plan.** Trade debts Rs86.0bn (FY25 86.6bn); overdue Rs61.7bn (66.9bn); Rs38.1bn is over 90 days and due from associated and related parties; Rs61.1bn of past-due debt is directly or ultimately from the Government (AR2026 note 21). **No expected-credit-loss charge is booked under an SECP exemption that runs to 31 December 2026.** If it is not extended, 2HFY27 could carry a provision. The Rs1.49tn gas circular-debt plan (Rs540bn from extra OGDC, PPL, GHPL dividends) was presented in August and is not approved by CCoE or the IMF (17 Oct). MARI is not in the dividend list, so the benefit is indirect, through faster collection from SNGPL, SSGC and fertilizer buyers.
- **Super tax and Rule 35.** The FY26 super tax reversal of Rs17.2bn was one-off; the FY28 budget (June 2027) can change the rate. The Rs5.7bn Rule 35 reversal is under a stay at the Islamabad High Court. Rule 35 cost Rs22.65bn in FY26 (Rs14.17bn FY25) and rises with prices, which is part of why oil gains drop through at only about 53%.
- **New volumes with dates.** Spinwam-1 on 1 Apr 2026 (up to 50 MMscfd; Waziristan 100 MMscfd, ramp-up after Shewa full-field development); Shams-1 on 19 Jun 2026 (35 MMscfd); Lundali-1 on 6 Sep 2026 (10 MMscfd gross, Mari 30%); HRL wells 127H and 128H on 20 Jun 2026; Ghazij 75 now, 222 allocated, needs buyers' plants (two to three years); Spinwam 17.5 MMscfd third-party sale pending approvals; Soho processing for 30 MMscfd; Mari lease renewed to 10 Nov 2065 (21 Aug 2026).
- **Cash uses.** Capex Rs72.6bn in FY26; exploration expense forecast +39% in 1QFY27 (Topline); Abu Dhabi, minerals, data centres (MARI_quality.md).

## What kills it

1. **Curtailment persists or worsens.** Trigger: quarterly sales below 105 kboepd, or a new SNGPL rupture notice.
2. **ECL provision.** Trigger: SECP exemption lapses on 31 Dec 2026 without extension; a charge on Rs61bn of government debt would be several rupees a share.
3. **Overdue debt rises.** Trigger: overdue trade debts above Rs70bn or over-90-day balances above Rs45bn in the 1QFY27 or half-year accounts.
4. **Tax or levy shocks.** Trigger: Rule 35 stay lifted (Rs5.7bn) or a higher super tax in the June 2027 budget.
5. **Oil-linked fall in FY28.** Trigger: Brent below 75 dollars for most of December 2026 to May 2027 (cuts the July to December 2027 price by about 10%).
6. **Cash leaks.** Trigger: payout below 35% or new commitments to Abu Dhabi, minerals or data centres above Rs10bn.

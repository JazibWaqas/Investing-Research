## Capital decisions
- Branch roll-out: 571 (2016) to 1,105 (2025) while ROE ran 31% (2019), 56% (2023), 33.8% (2025) and cost-to-income 27 to 30% (CBS Apr 2026). Strong return.
- HSBC Oman Pakistan unit (2015, one branch, Rs4.1bn assets): small, booked a bargain-purchase gain; tax dispute worth Rs1.8bn still open (AR2025 note 26.3).
- Retention: equity Rs86.6bn (2021) to Rs279bn (2025), 3.2x in four years, no rights issue found in this window.
- Rs2.6tn investments, Rs2.2tn in government Ijarah sukuk, 9x equity: a rate and sovereign bet, not operating skill.
- Al Meezan AMC: PAT Rs4.2bn, but AUM Rs724bn vs Rs718bn in a year the index rose 51%.

## Disclosure
Annual report out by 5 to 7 March each year, text-searchable, audited by A. F. Ferguson since 2022 (EY before, rotation by rule), no qualified opinion found. Briefings held and decks posted every year; quarter results on time. Gaps: no numeric outlook in the report, scanned key notices.

## Growth engine and what breaks it
Deposits at 20 to 25% a year plus 100+ branches a year, 91% CASA, digital share. At ROE 33% and 55% payout, retention alone doubles equity in five years (14.5% a year), so 2x earnings needs ROE to hold; deposit growth would have to meet it. Breaks: easing cycle (earnings followed the policy rate: PAT Rs28bn in 2021, Rs101bn in 2024, Rs89bn in 2025), tax or minimum-deposit rules, sovereign sukuk concentration, a new CEO, corporate loan book (74%, textile NPLs).

## Red flags
1. Earnings are a rate call; 2021 to 2024 was not management skill.
2. Credit card five-year slip; "stronger H2" missed.
3. Unnamed Rs30 to 33bn related-party financing (repaid); several 2026 notices unread; Q4 2025 dip unexplained.

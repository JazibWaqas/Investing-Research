## Capital decisions
- **IPO (Sep 2021)**: 90m shares at Rs71.5; 30m were sponsor shares, so Rs2.145bn of Rs6.435bn went to the family (AR23 n.1.2).
- **Select Technologies (2022, Xiaomi/Tecno assembly)**: carried at Rs8.0bn; the group earns about 30% ROE (sa_ratios: 34% FY24, 29% FY25, 33% FY26). Listed 13 Jul 2026 at Rs34, Rs3.02bn raised, 10% sold (press). This tests the group's value; Select now has outside holders.
- **Shell Pakistan bid (Jul 2023, with PRL, 77.42%)**: withdrawn 3 Apr 2024 after talks failed (notice 228397). No cost disclosed.
- **Widening scope**: Zexo (Dec 2025, Rs200m), AirFin BNPL (Jul 2026, Rs500m), e-bikes "AirV" (23 Jul 2026), Hisense TVs and ACs, Apple stores. Returns on all of these are unproven.
- **Cash conversion is the weak point**: consolidated operating cash flow was -Rs2.8bn (FY24) and -Rs8.7bn (FY25) (AR25); FY26 about -Rs5bn (quarterly_eps.csv, unverified). Short-term borrowings reached Rs29.6bn at June 2025 against equity near Rs17bn; FY26 finance cost Rs4.4bn. PACRA rates it A+/A1, but the company rolls short-term sukuk (twelfth issued Jul 2026).

## Minorities
- No rights issues or share-capital raises since IPO. A bonus issue and capital increase EGM was called then cancelled twice (Jun 2023, Mar 2025); no bonus issued.
- Dividends: Rs2.5 (FY23), Rs6.0 (FY24), Rs7.0 (FY25), then **Rs2.0 on EPS 15.75 for FY26 (13%)**, final nil (AR26 n.50). Cash is retained to fund working capital while borrowing.
- **CEO sold 7.5m shares at Rs135.25 (Rs1.01bn) on 3 Oct 2024** (notice 239086), a day before his Business Recorder statement on Xiaomi EV imports. His holding then read 100.16m; it is 96.66m in AR26 with no matching filing in the CSV (unverified).
- Related-party dealings in AR26 are with subsidiaries only; the CEO is the only paid director.

## Disclosure
Prompt notices; decks every Oct or Nov (2023 to 2025); FY22 and FY24 reports are mainly scanned. Against that: four revoked filings (annual report Oct 2021, AGM notice Oct 2022, FY23 results Oct 2023, annual report Sep 2024); deck numbers disagree (FY23 sales Rs36.9bn vs Rs21.5bn, which are consolidated and standalone; FY24 debt-to-equity "19%" with Rs13.7bn borrowings on about Rs15bn equity); two PSX unusual-movement queries (Dec 2024, Aug 2025) answered "not aware".

## Growth engine and what breaks it
Profit Rs6.2bn; doubling in five years needs about 15% a year. Drivers: local TV, AC and phone assembly in the SGSEZ with a ten-year tax holiday, Hisense and Acer, retail and BNPL. Breakers: sales-tax or import-rule changes (the 18% phone tax cut FY25 sales 19.5%), Xiaomi dependence (payable Rs7.8bn, over 10% of revenue), higher rates on Rs29bn of short-term debt, SGSEZ delays, gross margin that may be at a peak, and 10% of the best asset now owned by others.

## Red flags
1. Three years of negative operating cash flow against Rs15bn of reported profit, funded by short-term debt.
2. Sponsor sale of Rs1.0bn (and Rs2.1bn at the IPO), with about 74% family ownership.
3. FY26 payout cut to 13% while building new businesses.
4. Rolling announcements (e-bikes, BNPL, EV talk, laptops) far from the core assembly business.
5. Revoked filings and inconsistent deck numbers; Q4 FY26 profit Rs2.6bn is 41% of the year (cause unverified).

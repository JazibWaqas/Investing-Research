# SAZEW (Sazgar Engineering Works): underwriting card

Written 17 Oct 2026, before the Q1 FY27 result (no board notice yet; it must be filed by 30 Oct). Price Rs1,530.41 (close 16 Oct 2026, data/prices_raw/SAZEW.csv). KMI All Share member (data/kmi_allshare_2026-06-05.csv). **Verdict: AVOID at this price. A trailing P/E of 4 is cheap only on peak earnings, and those earnings are already falling. I would look again at Rs1,200, or sooner if the auto policy brings back the hybrid tax concession.** Expected 12-month return is about -4.5%, against 12.5% on a 12-month T-bill.

## The story

Sazgar assembles GWM Haval SUVs (H6 HEV and PHEV, Jolion) and the Tank 500 from CKD kits in Lahore. It also makes auto-rickshaws and some tractor parts for MTL and AGTL. As the first Chinese brand to localise hybrids, it went from EPS Rs1.95 in FY22 to Rs390.51 in FY26. FY26 revenue was Rs191.7bn (+76%), with 19,179 four-wheelers (+77%), and profit after tax was a record Rs23.6bn. Q4 alone earned Rs144 a share on 6,549 units, helped by the Tank 500 launch and by buyers rushing ahead of a tax change.

Why it is interesting now: the stock is Rs1,530, a 52-week low. That is 38% below the 9 Feb high of Rs2,456 and 28% below the July high of Rs2,120. The orchestrator's starting point (down 22% from the July high) understates the fall. On trailing numbers it is 3.9x earnings and 2.15x book, with a 71% ROE.

What changed is tax. Locally assembled hybrids paid a concessionary sales tax of 8.5% (12.75% at 1,800cc and above) until **30 June 2026**. From 1 July they paid 25% (PakWheels, 7 Jul). On 13 Sep, SRO 1525(I)/2026 cut the rate to 18% for hybrids of 2,000cc and below, which covers the H6 and Jolion (Daily Pakistan, 14 Sep). The Tank 500's treatment is unclear. Even after the cut, Sazgar's core products carry about 9.5 points more tax than in FY26.

FY22 is not meaningful because EPS was Rs1.95. On forward base EPS the stock trades at 5.8x against a median of about 7.8x, or 0.75x. That is above its four-year median of about 0.64x. The cheapness is in the trailing number only.

Three things the low P/E is pricing in:
1. **Volumes have turned down.** Q1 FY27 four-wheeler sales were 3,137 (July 663, then 2,474 in August and September together), against 3,557 a year earlier (-12%). Industry car sales rose 43% over the same quarter, so Sazgar is losing share. Rickshaws grew 45%, but they are a small part of revenue.
2. **Margins are being squeezed.** Gross margin was 29.1% in FY25, 24.2% in FY26 and 22.3% in Q4. Arif Habib attributed the decline to product mix and PKR weakness (via ProPakistani, 27 Aug). If Sazgar absorbs any of the tax, margins fall further.
3. **Capital is going into expansion and property.** The board has approved a Rs22bn paint shop that raises capacity to 54,000 units, against a current run rate near 15,000. It has also approved Rs4bn for land and Rs1.1bn for a commercial plot at Pine Avenue, and on 6 Oct it bought land for Rs868m. Stock in trade tripled to Rs42.1bn. Operating cash flow was Rs9.6bn, against Rs15.6bn of capex.

The probability-weighted value is Rs1,415. Adding Rs45.90 of net dividends and dividing by Rs1,530.41 gives an **expected return of about -4.5%**. The bull case needs a policy decision that has slipped since June.

## What kills it (if held)

1. **Q1 EPS below Rs50 or gross margin below 20%** (result by 30 Oct). That would confirm the tax is being absorbed.
2. **Four-wheeler sales below 1,200 a month** in the October or November filings.
3. **The auto policy is notified with hybrid parity at the full rate and a 15% CBU cap.** Protection is gone permanently.
4. **Net cash turns to net debt** in the December balance sheet, or the interim dividend falls below Rs10. That would show capex is crowding out payouts.
5. **PKR weaker than 285 per USD.** CKD costs rise, and so do prices or margins.

## Position plan (Rs10,000 to Rs12,500)

I would not buy at Rs1,530. For reference, 8 shares cost Rs12,243. Including Rs45.90 a share of net dividends, the outcomes for 8 shares would be:
- bear: Rs7,567 (-Rs4,676)
- base: Rs11,967 (-Rs276)
- bull: Rs17,167 (+Rs4,924)

**Buy at Rs1,200 or below** (10 shares, Rs12,000). That is 4.6x base EPS, and the expected return turns clearly positive there. **Upgrade sooner** if the policy restores a concession and monthly four-wheeler sales go back above 1,600. **Cut** any holding on a close below Rs1,050, or on trigger 1 or 3 above.

# QUESTIONS.md: what an experienced investor asks before any PSX call (draft, 18 Oct 2026)

Run these as a conversation, not a scoring sheet. Each item: question; why it matters; what answer changes the call; where the data or base rate lives. Weight tags: **[M]** measured (our test or audit, cited; most are weak or small-n, so read the caveat); **[P]** practitioner (source cited); **[U]** untested. A measured null means "not usable as a trading signal", not "irrelevant to understanding". Paths are under `tests/`, `knowledge/`, `seed/data/` unless stated.

## Sources
- S1 Buffett letters (berkshirehathaway.com/letters, fetched): 1987 (Mr. Market is there "to serve you, not to guide you"; "outstanding business at a sensible price, not a mediocre business at a bargain price"; borrow only what is safe on a worst case). 1991 ("able and shareholder-oriented managements"; good returns on equity with little debt; management is something "we can not supply"). 1996 (moat; "reported earnings are a poor measure of economic progress"; "you can pay too much for even the best of businesses").
- S2 Marks, Oaktree memos: cycles and the pendulum ("flawless to hopeless"); know where you are rather than forecast. Secondary summaries only (Axios 2 Oct 2018, FT interview); the primary memos were not retrieved.
- S3 Lynch, One Up on Wall Street: six types (slow growers, stalwarts, fast growers, cyclicals, turnarounds, asset plays) and the two-minute story. Secondary summaries only.
- S4 Greenblatt: good business (return on capital) at a cheap earnings yield; special situations (mergers, spinoffs) are mispriced because they are complex and thinly covered. Secondary summaries only; his backtest is contested.
- S5 Venture-style diligence (founder, strategy, why now, who else wins): general practice, not fetched. Used only where it matches the investor own checks.
- S6 `knowledge/lessons.md` (L1 to L21). S7 `tests/FACTS.md`. S8 `knowledge/HOW_TO_THINK.md` (HTT).

## A. Regime questions (what is the tide doing)

A1. **Where is the policy rate against inflation, and which way is it likelier to move?** Rates set the P/E the market pays and which sectors work. Changes: hold or hike favours power, E&P, fertilizer, Islamic banks; first cuts favour the broad market. Data: `tests/rate_cycle_episodes.md`, `tests/sector_momentum_regime.md`, `knowledge/playbooks/sector_rotation.md`. [M] first cut, 120d median +39.8% vs placebo +7.9%, but 2 cycles and 3 clusters: weak. Doublers were 2.5 to 2.8x commoner when the 6M bill sat below the policy rate (`tests/winners_case_control.md`).
A2. **Is the curve (6M bill, PIBs) pricing a move the SBP has not made?** If it is priced, the announcement is not a trade. Data: `tests/rate_cycle_episodes.md` H5. [M] "weak as a lead".
A3. **Is inflation turning, and is this a run or a blip?** Falling CPI preceded big 12-month gains. Changes: confidence in a cut-cycle tilt. Data: `tests/seed_audit.md`, rate_cycle H4. [M] 10 runs, six of them in about two episodes: count episodes (L16).
A4. **IMF status: staff agreement, board approval or cash release, and which conditions bite (tariffs, circular-debt ceiling, taxes)?** It sets default risk and policy room for everything else. Changes: a programme cliff removes the tide; a conditions headline can hit one sector (power, Oct 2024). Data: `knowledge/sectors/power.md`, `knowledge/reviews/critic_review.md` s2. [M] IMF events "not detectable" in index returns (seed_audit); sector effect [U].
A5. **Balance of payments: reserves, current account, import cover, rupee direction?** HTT lists it as a main driver. Changes: reserve stress means stay small; a stable rupee supports rate cuts. Data: `seed/data/macro/`, `tests/commodity_pkr_sensitivity.md`. [M] exporters-over-importers after devaluation "not supported" (5 episodes); reserves as a regime gauge [U].
A6. **Is the market cheap or rerated against its own post-crisis range?** 2022 to 2025 went from about 3.5x to 9x and flatters every long test. Changes: lowers the base rate and the size you accept. Data: L2, L9, `tests/value_timestamped.md`. [M] value "nothing" on four cohorts; latest cohort flat to negative. Marks on valuation as cycle position [P] (S2).
A7. **Political stability as business reads it: is the government stable, buyable and pro-business?** HTT: instability and a rising market coexisted. Changes: only a break in business confidence counts, not headlines. Data: no ledger yet; `critic_review.md` s2 proposes one. [U].
A8. **Oil: where is Brent, and which link do I actually hold (refiner margin, E&P, circular debt, import bill)?** Oil reaches PSX by links of unequal strength. Changes: act on the strong link, ignore the chain. Data: `tests/commodity_pkr_sensitivity.md`, `knowledge/sectors/refineries.md`, `exploration_production.md`. [M] Brent up then refiners at 1m: median +5.92 vs placebo p95 4.03, but 7 episodes, and +0.44 without 2020; E&P beta to Brent t=4.04.
A9. **Is the market used to this noise? What did price do on the headline day, and what did the largest holders do?** A shrug is aggregated judgment (HTT s3). Changes: if price ignored it, do not trade the headline; if price broke, ask why. Data: daily prices; no noise map yet. [U] (the investor own method).
A10. **Which past regime does this resemble, and what did the method do there?** Changes: weight evidence by regime similarity. Data: `tests/active_trading_codex.md`. [M] HIGH52 0.89x (2016 to 18), 3.34x (2020 to 22), 2.62x (2024 to 26).
A11. **If a shock (war, default scare), how far and how fast did past shocks fall, and who led the rebound?** HTT: chaos is an opportunity class. Changes: rebound plan versus sit out. Data: situation library not yet built. [U].

## B. Business questions (the core)

B1. **What is five-year earnings power on consolidated EPS, one-offs stripped?** Headline P/E is often wrong by 2x. Changes: LUCK is 6.6x not 12.6x; EPCL H1 profit was a one-off gain. Data: L1, `knowledge/companies/LUCK.md`, `EPCL.md`, `FFC.md`. [M] audit finding (HUBC consolidated EPS 38.26 vs standalone 18.86). Buffett 1996 on reported earnings [P].
B2. **What are the sector real levers (gas price and allocation, PPA terms, regulated prices), and where do they stand?** Profit follows a few regulated numbers. Changes: a lever move is the first link, so act. Data: `knowledge/sectors/*.md` s1 to s3. [P] structure from practice; the edge itself [U].
B3. **What does the business earn on new capital, and how much does it reinvest?** A compounder needs reinvestment at high returns (S1 1991; S4 return on capital). Changes: yield-only names fail the HTT growth test. Data: `tests/quality_within_sector.md`. [M] top minus bottom median +5.8, p=0.054, 3 episodes: weak.
B4. **Is the business trying to become big (adjacent businesses, capacity, new segments), with a builder culture?** The HTT LUCK model. Changes: justifies a premium multiple and a ten-year hold. Data: `companies/LUCK.md`. [U]; "quality" needs an operational definition (L21).
B5. **Who runs it, and did past promises meet delivery and past bets pay?** Management is the one input Buffett says he cannot supply; HTT says on PSX it is often the whole story. Changes: poor record caps size or vetoes. Data: annual reports and briefings, not yet scored. [P] S1, S5.
B6. **How does the sponsor treat minorities (payout, rights issues, related-party deals, capital sinks)?** LUCK pays 8% of Rs60.78 EPS while funding PIA bids, DISCOs, mining. Changes: a deserved discount, so it is not "cheap". Data: L10, `companies/LUCK.md`, `playbooks/holdco_discounts.md`. [P] S1 1991; insider-buying as a signal is [M] not supported.
B7. **Can the balance sheet survive higher rates and a bad year?** EPCL carries Rs40bn net debt at 11.5%. Changes: leveraged names drop out in a hike regime. Data: `companies/EPCL.md`, `knowledge/sectors/README.md`. [P] S1 1987 on worst-case borrowing.
B8. **Is the cash real (cash conversion, receivables, circular-debt exposure)?** Profit booked against receivables is not a dividend. Changes: discount earnings and payout. Data: `companies/NETSOL.md`, `sectors/power.md`. [M] cash conversion sat inside the quality test (weak).
B9. **Float and exit: can I leave at my size, and who holds the free float?** Thin float and lower locks trap exits. Changes: size to what can be sold. Data: L7, `tests/size_liquidity.md`, `tests/seed_audit.md`. [M] operator pump 60d median -8.5%, win 35% (n=675).
B10. **Is it in KMI All Share, and near the 33% debt threshold (cut from 37% in Feb 2026)?** A hard constraint; losing eligibility forces selling. Changes: ineligible equals not a candidate. Data: `data/kmi_allshare_2026-06-05.csv`, `critic_review.md` s2. [P] PSX notice; the index-change trade is [M] "nothing".
B11. **What is priced in: P/E relative to market and own history, P/B against ROE for banks, and what must go right?** A good business at a high price returns little (S1 1996; S2). Changes: wait for a price or take a smaller position. Data: L2, L6, `companies/MEBL.md`, `FABL.md`. [M] value alone "nothing"; inside a sector only a tiebreaker (L17).
B12. **Can I explain it in two minutes, and do I know it better than the price does?** If not, pass (S1 1987; S3). Data: the company card. [P].
B13. **Is it growing, not merely stable?** HTT: capital gains, not a 7% yield. Changes: demote yield names. Data: HTT s2; `tests/FACTS.md` (walk-forward core B2 ratio 0.86 to the index). [M] the yield core did not beat the index like-for-like; the growth core is [U].

## C. Situation questions (opportunity sleeve)

C1. **What type is this: policy decision, ignored earnings inflection, shock and rebound, deal, sponsor or buyback move, volume ignition, or none?** Types have different odds (S3 types; S4 special situations). Changes: no type, no trade. Data: `playbooks/catalyst_trade.md`, `deals.md`. [P] S3, S4.
C2. **What is the measured base rate for this type, and how many independent episodes stand behind it?** Anecdotes are not odds. Changes: sets size and whether to act. Data: deals 27 of 56 PAIs completed (48%), about 90% after the PAO; refiner reaction rule 60d median -3.4% vs placebo -6.9%. [M] for deals and the reaction rule; shock-rebound and policy-pending [U].
C3. **Who gains, who decides, and when?** HTT: everyone is playing a game. A named decider with a date (ECC, OGRA, NEPRA, cabinet) can be watched. Changes: no decider or date, no catalyst. Data: `playbooks/catalyst_trade.md`, sector notes s3. [U]; refiners and ASL were flaggable only in hindsight.
C4. **Is price confirming (new highs, rising volume) or extended?** Join confirmed trends, not stretched ones. Changes: confirmed allows size; extended means pass. Data: `tests/active_trading_codex.md`, `tests/oracle_and_good_case.md`. [M] HIGH52 beat random paths in 2 recent cohorts and lost in 2016 to 18; "a hypothesis from five windows".
C5. **Am I picking a winner from start-of-move features?** The tests say these do not work. Changes: do not pay for a "this looks like a doubler" story. Data: `tests/winners_case_control.md`. [M] top-decile precision 5.82% vs shuffled placebo 5.91%.
C6. **If a takeover: what fraction of my holding does the offer buy, and what is the stub worth?** EPCL: 17.5% offer, so about 50 to 70% of a minority holding fills. Changes: free money disappears. Data: L10, `deals.md`. [M] 11 completed offers: price a month after completion median -13% vs offer.
C7. **Is volume with upper locks demand or an operator?** SSGC 2024 was +120%; operator pumps had 60d median -8.5%. Changes: act only with a dated reason and a hard stop. Data: `tests/seed_audit.md`. [M].
C8. **Is the sponsor or an insider buying, filed and public?** ASL was flaggable from filings. Changes: supports entry only with a catalyst. Data: `tests/insider_cluster_buying.md`. [M] not supported (63d median -0.4%, p 0.473; top 3 events carry the mean).
C9. **What is the exit: stop, target, time, and the loss in rupees if wrong?** HTT: constraints are loss budgets. Changes: no stop, no entry. Data: `tests/fixed_capital_runs.md` (S3 best case drew down 35%). [P] HTT; loss limits are placeholders until sourced.
C10. **What is the trap: one episode counted as many, a one-off, a corporate-action gap, lookahead in my sample?** The usual false positive here. Changes: discard the signal. Data: L3, L5, L15, L16. [M].
C11. **Is capital stuck in a position I would not buy today?** HTT: capital is never stuck. Changes: rotate. Data: `playbooks/active_trading.md`. [M] fast rotation into winners "not supported" by case-control; the rotation rule itself is the investor own [P].

## D. Self-check questions

D1. **What would an expert find obvious that I skipped (sponsor, float, circular debt, rupee, Shariah, tax)?** Past failures were omissions. Changes: add the missing section before concluding. Data: HTT s3, s6. [M] our record (cards missed people and incentives).
D2. **What does my data not contain (delistings, pre-Nov 2024 dividends, a 2026 liquid pool, order flow)?** Survivorship inflates results. Changes: haircut. Data: `tests/FACTS.md` caveats, `system_walkforward` files. [M].
D3. **Am I intellectualizing a long causal chain? How strong is each link?** Changes: if any link is weak, drop the trade. Data: `tests/commodity_pkr_sensitivity.md`. [M] H1 and H2 fail, H3 betas pass.
D4. **Does the metric fit the goal and horizon?** The 12-month yardstick produced the yield core. Changes: re-grade. Data: HTT s3. [M] our own error.
D5. **Did I use a placebo, the total-return index and a reference class, and separate deposits from skill?** Changes: restate the result. Data: L15, L20, `tests/fixed_capital_runs.md`, `oracle_and_good_case.md`. [M].
D6. **Did the market surprise me, and have I written why?** Surprise is data (HTT s4). Changes: update the map or base rate. Data: scorecard, to build. [U].
D7. **Did I start work before the question was clear?** Changes: stop and re-ask. Data: HTT s8. [M] session record (two overruns).

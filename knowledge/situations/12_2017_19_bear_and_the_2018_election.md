# Situation 12: the 2017 to 2019 bear and the 2018 election

Written 2026-10-18. Type: regime case (balance-of-payments squeeze, hike cycle, no IMF until 2019). Sources: `data/macro/` (kse100, policy_rate_decisions, cpi, usdpkr, tbill_auctions), `data/panel_close_adj_pricereturn.parquet` (75 liquid survivors from May 2017, 55 from Jul 2018; survivors only, delisted names missing), `knowledge/sectors/tech.md`. News: MSCI inclusion (Textalks, Dawn, Arab News, asserted from search snippets); Reuters-sourced rupee and IMF items (Malay Mail, Al Jazeera, Arab News, 2018, asserted); the 2017 Supreme Court verdict and the 25 Jul 2018 election are general knowledge, not re-fetched.

## Dates and regime

- 24 May 2017: KSE 52,876, the high. Policy rate 5.75%, CPI 4.8% (Apr), USD/PKR 104.8, 3-month bill 6.0%.
- 16 Aug 2019: KSE 28,765, the low (27 months, -45.6%). Policy rate 13.25% (750bp of hikes from Jan 2018), CPI 10.4% heading to 14.9%, USD/PKR 158.6, bill 13.75%.
- In dollars (index divided by the exchange rate) the fall was 64%.

## What was knowable, and when

1. 15 May 2017 and 1 Jun: MSCI reconfirmed Pakistan's move from Frontier to Emerging, effective 1 Jun (asserted). KSE hit its high on 24 May, then fell on the news. A 10% trailing stop would have triggered on 15 Jun at 47,443.
2. 28 Jul 2017: the Supreme Court disqualified the PM (general knowledge). KSE 45,912, -13.2% from the high.
3. 8 to 13 Dec 2017: USD/PKR moved from 105.4 to 109.9 in six sessions (observed), the first step devaluation.
4. 29 Jan 2018: the first hike, +25bp to 6.0% with CPI at 4.6% (observed). A central bank hiking at 4.6% inflation was defending the rupee, not fighting prices. 14 Jul: +100bp to 7.5%.
5. 25 Jul 2018: the election. KSE 41,339 on 24 Jul, 43,557 on 30 Jul (+5.4%), and 42,447 on 17 Aug.
6. 8 Oct 2018: the finance minister said talks with the IMF would begin; KSE fell 3.39% to 37,898 (Asia reporting, asserted; the close is observed). USD/PKR 124 then 138.7 by 31 Dec (observed). In late November the rupee fell almost 5% in a day to a record low near 143 (Reuters via Malay Mail, asserted).
7. 12 May 2019: the staff-level agreement (case 01).

From 24 May 2017 to 16 Aug 2019, 75 liquid survivors had a median return of -71.5%, and 3 of 75 (4%) were up: LOTCHEM +30%, BAHL +5%, EFERT +1%. From the election day to the low, the median of 55 was -47.6% and 1 of 55 was up (LOTCHEM +22.5%). HUBC -54%, OGDC -43%, LUCK -66% from the 2017 peak. The exception that mattered: USD earners. NETSOL rose from 60 to 158 between Dec 2017 and Aug 2018 (tech.md, raw prices), +145% against KSE +4% over the same year.

## The right move

Out of equity indices from June 2017 (the stop) or no later than the first step devaluation and the 29 Jan 2018 hike, into bills (6% rising to 13.75%) and, for risk capital, USD earners. Cash returned roughly 20% over the 27 months (approximate: bill average about 9% times 2.25 years) against -46% for the index and -71% for the median stock. There was no long entry until the case 01 sequence (rate hold on 16 Sep 2019). The cost of sitting out was the three rallies (+12.6%, +5.4%, +10.6%), which a trader could have used but none was a bottom.

## The trap

Treating each event as the low. The election removed an uncertainty (KSE +5.4%) and the IMF talks opened a path, but the reasons for the fall (a current-account deficit funded by reserves, a stuck rupee, a central bank behind the curve) remained until the rupee floated and the programme started in 2019. A second trap: valuation. Names looked cheap on trailing multiples at every step down and kept falling.

## Situation type and lesson

Regime: a hike cycle forced by the external account, plus a currency step-down. Politics (a PM disqualified, an election, a new government) were the visible events, and none drove the index: Dec 2017 and Jan 2018 mark the regime shift, not the verdict or the election. Lesson: read the rate and the rupee, not the political headline. Lower highs (52,876, 45,560, 43,557, 41,113) were visible each time. In this regime, breadth was the signal: 4% of liquid names up over 27 months.

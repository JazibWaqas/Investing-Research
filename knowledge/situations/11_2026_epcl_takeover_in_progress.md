## Dates and regime

- Policy rate 11.5% since the 27 Apr hike; the next MPC is 26 Oct and a Topline survey had 61% expecting a hike (EPCL.md). 6-month bill 12.4%. CPI 10.3% (Sep). USD/PKR 277.4.
- EPCL is a PVC producer in a trough: EPS 12.46, 9.67, 7.39 (FY21 to FY23), then -0.40 and -4.29. Net debt about Rs40bn.

## What was knowable, and when

1. 11 to 12 Feb: EPCL rose 10% and 6% on 18m and 27m shares (4.5m on 10 Feb), from 32.99 to 38.48. No filing explains it. The first related filing came on 17 Feb at 13:46 (doc 270336). A run before the news.
2. 17 Feb: Mitsubishi agreed to sell its 11.0% to Liberty Daharki Power, a related buyer. EPCL closed 37.16.
3. 10 Mar: public announcement of intention (PAI): Noventra (formerly Lotte Chemical Pakistan) to acquire control from Engro Corporation; the offer covers 159.09m shares, 17.5% (doc 272303). EPCL 30.18 the day before, 33.20 that day.
4. 31 Aug and 1 Sep: the share purchase agreement for Engro's 56.19% (510.73m shares) at Rs38.60 (doc 282058). EPCL 35.64 on 1 Sep, up Rs0.04.
5. 7 Sep: the deadline for the public announcement of offer (PAO) was extended to 7 Dec because approvals are pending (doc 282317).
6. 5 Oct: Lotte Chemical Pakistan renamed Noventra. Its cash of Rs2.2bn against about Rs25.8bn needed for the SPA and the offer suggests a rights issue (inference).

## The right move

As of 16 Oct: do not buy at 31.77 (EPCL.md). Math: 318.2m shares can tender (908.92m minus Engro's 510.73m minus about 80m for Liberty), against an offer for 159.09m, so acceptance is about 50% if everyone tenders and up to about 77% if two large holders do not. At 60% acceptance, the base case is 0.6 x 38.60 + 0.4 x 26 = Rs33.56. Scenarios: bear Rs23 (25%), base Rs33.5 (50%), bull Rs37 (25%); the weighted value is Rs31.75, an expected return of -0.1%, while the 6-month bill pays 12.4%. Buy below Rs27 (weighted +18%) or if the PAO raises the price or size.

What would change the call, with dates: the 20 Oct Q3 result (the frozen call is a 55% chance that EPS is below Rs1.04), the 26 Oct MPC, about 23 Nov when a provisional PVC anti-dumping duty lapses, the 7 Dec PAO deadline, 14 Dec MPC. Cut on a PAI withdrawal, a missed 7 Dec with no extension, or a close under Rs24.

## The trap

Two. The headline spread: 21.5% on 16 Oct, but only about half of a minority holding is bought at the offer price, and the remainder is left in a company with a leveraged parent and a free float near 17.5%. The pro-rata worked example: PIOC in Feb 2026, bought at 415.94 the day before the PAO, 61.64% filled at 478.43, the rest worth 330.22 on payment day, +1.4% blended against a 15% headline (deals.md). The second trap: buying the news. The price fell 17% from the pre-news peak.

## Situation type and lesson

Deal after the offer is published. Base rate across 11 PSX takeovers 2021 to 2026 (deals.md): median spread the day before the offer 14.7%, median time from intention to payment 302 days, median price one month after completion 13% below the offer. Spreads exist because completion, funding and acceptance are uncertain. What to check each time: pro-rata acceptance, the price after completion, funding, regulators and the acquirer balance sheet. No odds exist beyond n = 11.

## Questions that would have caught it

- Q1 Regime: a possible hike on 26 Oct hurts a Rs40bn net-debt balance sheet, and the acquirer has to fund from equity or debt at 11.5%+.
- Q2 Type and base rate: deal after the offer; n = 11; median completion 302 days.
- Q3 Actors: who gains? Engro Corporation sells; Noventra's owner takes control; Liberty is acting in concert; minorities get the pro-rata offer.
- Q4 Price: 12 Feb was a run before the news and the worst entry.
- Q5 Exit and loss: bear Rs23, -28%, probability 25%.
- Q6 Traps: narrow offer (lesson 10), funding, the leftover stub.

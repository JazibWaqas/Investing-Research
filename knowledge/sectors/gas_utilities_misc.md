## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Regulated return, trued up late (SNGP, SSGC).** OGRA allows a return on net operating assets and settles it in the Final Revenue Requirement (FRR) after year end. SSGC's own plan assumed a 20.05% return on a Rs155.6bn asset base for FY26, about Rs31.2bn (corporate briefing, 17 Dec 2024, doc 244576). Profit is therefore a booking-and-true-up exercise. Q4 carries the true-up: SSGC's consolidated Q4 FY25 was minus Rs4.05bn after three profitable quarters.
2. **UFG (gas lost) against the allowed level.** Losses above the benchmark are disallowed. SSGC's plan carried 10.5% actual UFG and an annual disallowance near Rs11bn; its actual UFG was 16.6% in FY23 and 10.14% in Q1 FY24. OGRA published a new "Final UFG Benchmark Study Report" dated 3 July 2026; I could not read its percentages.
3. **Receivables and circular debt.** Gas circular debt was Rs3.61tn in June 2026 (Rs1.88tn principal, Rs1.72tn late-payment surcharge). SSGC's trade debts were Rs130.9bn at June 2025, including K-Electric Rs28.5bn and Pakistan Steel Rs21.8bn, and its auditors qualified FY25 over them. Surcharge from those two is booked only on receipt.
4. **Rates.** SSGC borrowed Rs82.8bn short term at June 2025 and paid Rs12.1bn finance cost in FY25 (unconsolidated). PAEL has Rs11.8bn of short-term debt. Policy rate 11.5%; a hike is possible on 26 Oct.
5. **Volume and supply.** Gas: RLNG diversion and winter supply. SNGP revenue fell 25% YoY in Q3 FY26. Non-gas names: PNSC earns on freight rates and fleet; PIBTL on coal and bulk throughput at Port Qasim; PAEL on appliance and transformer demand plus financing cost.

- **6 Nov** Qatar force-majeure review (fertilizer.md). Nov to Feb is peak gas demand.
- **By 27 Nov** SNGP and SSGC FY26 results, dividends and AGMs. Last year's ex-dates were 19 Nov (SNGP, Rs3) and 18 Nov (SSGC, Re0.5).
- **Unscheduled:** cabinet and IMF decision on the circular debt plan; Pakistan Services restructuring agreement (disclosed 14 Sep); FESCO pre-qualification; half-yearly gas price notice (Feb 2025 precedent).

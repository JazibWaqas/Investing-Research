## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Regulated margin times litres.** The OMC margin is fixed by the government at Rs7.87 per litre on petrol and diesel, last revised September 2023, about a 2% gross margin. An ECC-approved Rs1.22 rise (to about Rs9.09) is still not notified. My arithmetic: PSO sells roughly 7 to 8bn litres a year, so Rs1.22 is about Rs9bn pre-tax, Rs8 to 11 a share after 46 to 60% tax, roughly 15 to 20% of consolidated EPS.
2. **Inventory gains and losses.** Stock is held about three to four weeks, so a price rise earns a gain and a cut books a loss, with no compensation for losses. The 11 April 2026 diesel cut of Rs135 a litre cost the sector an estimated Rs106bn pre-tax. PSO's implied Q4FY26 consolidated EPS is about minus Rs30 (9M Rs83.93 less full year Rs54.29, my subtraction). Daily pricing from 21 July 2026 should smooth this. A windfall recovery is the political side of it.
3. **Receivables and finance cost.** PSO's trade receivables are Rs414.8bn, 86% owed by SNGPL, GENCO and PIA, funded by Rs384.3bn of short-term debt. Finance cost fell about 21% to Rs29.7bn. Each 100bp of rates is roughly Rs3.8bn pre-tax (my arithmetic).
4. **Government items.** Import premium (the formula allows a fixed $5.10 a barrel against physical premiums near $30 in the crisis), price differential claims (Rs66.7bn unpaid industry-wide), the petroleum levy, and LNG, where PSO lost Rs7.9bn in FY26.
5. **Non-fuel.** Lubricants (PSO 44,000 tonnes, +6%; HTL), convenience retail, LPG, aviation. Small now, and the reason HTL and WAFI trade differently from PSO.

## 6. What a veteran watches weekly

1. Daily OGRA price notice (ogra.org.pk, nightly).
2. Brent and Hormuz flows (tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil, daily).
3. Monthly OMC sales, 1st to 3rd of the month (propakistani.pk, Topline and AHL).
4. PSX board meeting notices (dps.psx.com.pk/announcements/companies, about 7 days ahead).
5. ECC and Petroleum Division decisions on margin and levy (Dawn, Business Recorder, irregular).
6. SBP policy rate, reserves, PKR (sbp.org.pk, reserves weekly on Thursday).
7. PBS CPI (pbs.gov.pk, monthly; 10.3% in Sep).
8. The receivables line in each PSO result (quarterly).

## Gap resolution (17 Oct 2026, Codex)

- PSO's annual report confirms FY26 standalone profit of Rs15.07bn and EPS Rs32.10. Consolidated profit attributable to PSO shareholders was Rs25.49bn, EPS Rs54.29. The group's total profit before the non-controlling-interest allocation is a different measure and should not replace attributable EPS. Source: [PSO FY26 annual report](https://financialfilings.com/filings/pakistan-state-oil-company-limited/annual-report/2026/66477424/), published 7 October 2026.
- The Rs1.22 per litre OMC margin increase was still described as pending notification in the [OCAC letter reported by Business Recorder, 25 August 2026](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40435834/). No later notification was verified by 17 October, so implementation is unverified. The proposed Section 99D windfall recovery likewise has no verified operative notification; do not model it as an enacted charge.

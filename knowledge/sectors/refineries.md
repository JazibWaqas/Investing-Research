# Oil refineries (ATRL, PRL, NRL, CNERGY)

Written 2026-10-17. Evidence is news and PSX filings dated Aug to Oct 2026 plus our own price data (raw closes, unadjusted for bonus or rights; basket is equal weight ATRL/PRL/NRL vs KSE100). Items I could not confirm are marked unverified.

## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Product cracks over crude.** Gross refining margin (GRM) is the diesel (HSD), petrol (MS) and furnace oil (FO) price minus Dubai crude. Diesel is the profit pool. FO loses money (about minus $18 to $22 a barrel in Q1FY27, Topline estimate).
2. **The government diesel crack cap.** Since 20 Aug 2026 the allowed HSD crack is capped at $41.89/bbl with a floor of $11.33 (Platts Cash Dubai, 7-day average). The market crack runs about $10 above it, so the cap is the swing factor.
3. **Deemed duty and the upgrade policy.** Refiners get a deemed duty on diesel (7.5% under the 2023 policy). Signers of the upgrade agreement drop to 2.5%, falling to zero by 15 Nov 2026, in return for upgrade incentives (Euro-V, less FO). Exact incentive terms are partly unverified.
4. **Crude cost, premiums, inventory.** Falling crude means inventory losses, rising crude means gains. Import premiums hit $20/bbl for September cargoes. ATRL runs mostly local crude.
5. **Volume and utilisation.** Industry utilisation is 59.5%. Smuggling, import substitution and OMC offtake decide throughput.

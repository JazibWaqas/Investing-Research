# Steel and engineering sector (Pakistan, KMI All Share)

As of 2026-10-17. Prices and screener = `seed/data/fundamentals/screener_20261016.csv`. The web search budget ran out mid-session, so some items are marked unverified. "SA" = stockanalysis.com quarterly data (2026-10-17).

## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Domestic construction demand and mill utilisation.** Rebar (long) is about 60% of output and tracks building and public works. PACRA (Sep 2025): long-steel utilisation was 31.6% in FY24, local output fell 14.3% to 7.2 Mt in FY25, and imports took 30.8% of supply. SteelOrbis (2026-07-08) still describes operating rates as "well below normal".
2. **The scrap-to-product spread.** Raw material is about 76% of long-steel cost (PACRA). Import scrap was $395-399/t CFR and local scrap Rs141-142k/t in July 2026. Mughal says FY26 scrap rose 3% and rebar fell 1%, so the spread narrowed 15%, yet gross margin still rose.
3. **Power cost.** Electric-furnace melting uses roughly 400-700 kWh a tonne. Mughal credits a 5% lower tariff for FY26 margins. The Finance Act 2026 adds a per-unit sales tax on melters' electricity, adjustable as input tax (Dawn, 2026-06-26).
4. **Trade protection for flat products.** ISL, Aisha and Amreli depend on anti-dumping duty against Chinese coil. Chinese HRC at about $505-540/t CFR Pakistan (2026-03-25) sets the import floor.
5. **Finance cost and balance sheet.** Sector finance cost fell 30% in FY26 (a Rs5.6bn benefit). Debt-heavy names (Agha, Amreli, Dost) are restructuring stories, not operating stories.

## 6. What a veteran watches weekly

1. PSX company announcements, daily: dps.psx.com.pk/announcements/companies (board-meeting notices).
2. SBP policy calendar and T-bill auctions: sbp.org.pk/m_policy/mp-calendar.asp (eight MPCs a year).
3. APCMA cement despatches, monthly on the 1st-3rd (construction proxy).
4. SteelOrbis Pakistan scrap and rebar notes, weekly.
5. NTC notices at ntc.gov.pk, ad hoc.
6. PBS CPI and SPI at pbs.gov.pk, monthly on the 1st.
7. PKR interbank, daily, Mettis or SBP.
8. NEPRA and Power Division tariff notices, ad hoc plus the January rebasing.

## Gap resolution (17 Oct 2026, Codex)

- The [Pakistan trade tariff portal's FY27 entry for ferrous waste and scrap, PCT heading 7204](https://tipp.gov.pk/index.php?id=19541&r=tradeInfo%2Fview&sort=rate.desc), shows a 4% regulatory duty valid from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. This confirms one component of the landed import burden, not the combined customs, additional duty, sales tax and withholding burden. The note's FY26 all-in percentage should not be carried forward as the FY27 all-in rate without PCT-level reconciliation.

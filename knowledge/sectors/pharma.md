## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Regulated price against imported cost.** Essential medicines are price-controlled. Increases come through "hardship" cases, decided by a cabinet committee headed by the Finance Minister (pkrevenue, 25 Aug 2026). GSK says about 50 to 60 hardship cases are pending (6 May 2026). Gross margin is price list minus dollar-linked input cost. GLAXO gross margin was 17.4% (2022), 7.1% (2023), 25.2% (2024), 37.0% (2025); ABOT 29.5%, 21.4%, 29.0%, 35.1% (PSX ratios).
2. **PKR and API cost.** Roughly 85 to 95% of raw materials are imported and China supplies 85 to 90% of APIs (pkrevenue, 22 Jul and 14 Aug 2026; the percentages differ by source). USDPKR went 177.9 (Dec 2021), 226.0 (Dec 2022), 280.7 (Oct 2023), 276.8 (13 Oct 2026) (data/macro/usdpkr.csv). The rupee has been flat for three years, so the margin recovery is already in earnings.
3. **Volume and brand mix.** GSK's 2025 revenue rose 9% on price while volume fell 10 to 12%. Its top five brands are 58% of revenue and tenders 7 to 8%, being cut back (pkrevenue, 6 May 2026). Haleon sells over-the-counter and consumer health, so it follows household spending.
4. **Taxes and refunds.** 1% sales tax on registered drugs with no input adjustment, 18% on packaging, and FASTER Pharma refunds that should pay in 72 hours but are reported unpaid (pkrevenue, 25 May 2025 and 15 Jan 2026). The FY27 budget outcome was not found.
5. **Leverage and payout.** Searle and Citi Pharma carry debt, so rates and PKR reach net profit (Searle lost Rs3.3bn in FY24 and made Rs4.0bn in FY26). The multinationals pay out about half of earnings (ABOT Rs40 on EPS 81).

## 5. Past big moves, 2017 to 2026

Index returns are the pharma sector series against the equal-weight market (n=3 big episodes in ten years, so treat conclusions as soft).

- **Oct 2023 to Nov 2024, up.** Pharma +194% against +81% (+63% relative). GLAXO 75 to 365, HALEON 156 to 867, ABOT 395 to 1,092, HINOON 416 to 929. GLAXO EPS went from 1.68 (2023) to 20.52 (2024). Knowable early: the rupee stopped falling (280.7 in Oct 2023, 277.1 in Feb 2024), CPI rolled over (31.5% Sep 2023, 20.7% Mar 2024, 6.9% Sep 2024), and the trough was visible in the numbers. The signal was quarterly gross margin turning up. Trailing P/E hid it: GLAXO was about 45x on trough EPS. The pricing steps behind it are unverified.
- **Oct 2022 to Oct 2023, down.** Pharma -25% against +19% (-36% relative). USDPKR went 177.9 to 226 to 280.7, CPI hit 35.4% (Mar 2023), GLAXO gross margin fell from 17.4% to 7.1% and ABOT EPS fell from 30.7 to 2.7. The rupee fall was visible through 2022, and the SBP had said in March 2019 that the pricing policy has no currency adjustment (pkrevenue, 27 Mar 2019). A fast PKR fall with no price relief was the warning.
- **Aug to Nov 2019, up.** Pharma +87% against +45% (+28% relative, just under the bar; GLAXO 82 to 188, HINOON 252 to 555). It followed a regulatory shock: DRAP allowed up to 15% on 11 Jan 2019, ordered cuts of up to 75% on 3 May 2019, and GSK stock was seized in April (pkrevenue). The sector had fallen 35% from Mar to Aug 2019, so this was a rebound from a political reset. The cause of the Q4 rally is unverified.
- **Ongoing, down.** Jan 2025 to Oct 2026: -13% against +20% (-27% relative). Earnings stopped growing while CPI and rates rose.

# Chemicals, petrochemicals and polyester (EPCL, LOTCHEM/Noventra, LCI, ICL, SITC and others)

Written 2026-10-17. Evidence: PSX filings Feb to Oct 2026, NTC site, sunsirs spot prices (17 Oct), screener of 16 Oct, repo price data. The web search tool hit its limit and Business Recorder, Dawn and broker research were unreachable, so there is no broker view. Unverified items are marked.

## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Spread over imported feedstock.** EPCL's "core delta" (PVC less ethylene and EDC) was $273/t in Q2 2026, down 18% on Q1, as ethylene rose 35% against PVC's 18% (EPCL briefing, 18 Aug). Noventra earns PTA over paraxylene. Lucky Core (LCI) earns soda ash over energy and polyester staple over PTA.
2. **Trade protection.** Anti-dumping duties (ADD) decide whether local producers can pass on cost. NTC notices are the best public signal here.
3. **Energy.** EPCL's captive gas levy fell from Rs1,406/MMBtu (Jan) to Rs365 (May), and it holds an Islamabad High Court stay.
4. **PKR and interest.** Noventra calls its PTA margin "dollarised", so a weaker rupee helps. EPCL has about Rs40bn net debt, so rates matter.
5. **Demand and imports.** PVC goes 53% to pipes. Polyester follows textiles. LCI blames Chinese soda ash and PSF imports.

## 5. Past big moves, 2017 to 2026

Raw closes, ex-dividend not adjusted, against KMI All Share.

- **EPCL, 27 Oct 2016 to 22 Jun 2017: +309% (9.71 to 39.71), market +20%.** Knowable: the 18 Oct 2016 filing showed gross profit Rs614m against Rs291m (nine-month EPS 0.05). FY2016 EPS was 0.99 (7 Feb 2017), so Q4 earned about 0.94. Q1 2017 EPS was 1.27 against 0.03 (18 Apr). Signal: gross profit doubling on a leveraged plant priced under Rs10.
- **EPCL +304% (22.47 to 90.73) and LOTCHEM +327% (8.23 to 35.13), 24 to 26 Mar 2020 to Apr/Aug 2022; market +76% and +60%.** Knowable: the low followed a SEPA production stop on 9 Mar 2020 (pkrevenue). FY21 EPS was 12.46 (EPCL) and 3.07 (LOTCHEM) and USDPKR went 158 to 186 to 216. Signal: EPS far above price.
- **EPCL, 23 Jun 2022 to 23 Jun 2023: -53%, market -6%.** It traded at 3 to 4x earnings at the peak. 1Q23 profit fell 75% (20 Apr 2023) and 1H23 fell 61% (10 Aug 2023) (pkrevenue headlines). EPS then ran 12.46, 9.67, 7.39, -0.40, -4.29 (FY21 to FY25). Signal: falling quarterly margin.
- **LOTCHEM, 12 Aug 2022 to 20 Sep 2024: -55%, market +70%.** PAT ran 10.1bn, 5.1bn, 2.6bn, 1.1bn (2022 to 2025). The first lower annual PAT was the signal.
- **Sector, Jan 2024 to Oct 2026:** the seed chem index fell from 1.56 to 0.82 against the market. EPCL -48% (Dec 2023 to May 2025), LCI -50% (30 Jul 2025 to 14 Oct 2026). LCI's 5-for-1 split is inferred from price on 21 Jul 2025.

A cyclical looks cheapest at peak earnings. Three up and three down moves in ten years is too few for a rule.

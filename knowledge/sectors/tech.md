The tradeable sector is SYS and NETSOL, then AIRLINK.

## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **USD IT export volume.** SYS earns 94% of revenue in foreign currency. Pakistan IT and telecom exports were $4.6bn in FY26 (+20.6%) and $435m in September 2026 (+18% YoY).
2. **PKR against a PKR cost base.** About 56% of SYS costs are in rupees. Gross margin holds at 25-26% only if the rupee is stable. It has risen 257 straight days (277.01 on 8 Oct) while wages and fuel inflate (CPI 11.1% in August).
3. **Tax regime.** The 0.25% final tax on IT export proceeds is now extended to Tax Year 2029. Telecom is the opposite: operators pass 35-40% of revenue to the state.
4. **Gulf demand and collections.** SYS gets 57% of revenue from the Middle East and Africa. KSA collections slowed in Q1, and the Iran war and Hormuz disruption keep that risk live.
5. **Handset and telecom policy.** AIRLINK earns on assembled handset volume times the duty gap between imported and locally assembled phones. Telecom profit (PTC) depends on ARPU, tax, 5G capex and the PTCL-Telenor-Ufone merger synergy.

Overlay: the 6M T-bill is 12.4% (9.9% in January), which compresses growth multiples.

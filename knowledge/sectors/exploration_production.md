# Oil and gas exploration and production (E&P)

As of 2026-10-17. Prices and screener data to 2026-10-16. The web search budget ran out mid-research, so a few items are marked unverified.

## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Crude and condensate price (Arab Light/Brent) times PKR.** Oil sells in dollars. OGDC's realised oil price was USD 60.83/bbl in FY25 against 68.67 in FY24 (Nukta, OGDC FY25 note).
2. **Wellhead gas price.** OGRA resets it twice a year. Under Petroleum Policy 2012 a sliding scale passes only part of an oil move into gas: a 35% rise in Arab Light (USD 68.4 to 92.2) implies about +10-11% (Topline via Nukta, 2026-09-04).
3. **Volumes and curtailment.** Surplus RLNG and pipeline ruptures throttle domestic gas, which hurts MARI most. New fields (OGDC Baragzai, MARI Ghazij/Shawal) add volume.
4. **Tax and levies.** Super tax, windfall levy on crude, royalty. The Federal Constitutional Court's 2026-01-27 order sent super tax back to a per-concession calculation. That produced a Rs54.9bn sector reversal in 4QFY26 (OGDC Rs44bn). It is one-off.
5. **Cash collection and dividends.** Sui companies pay late. OGDC is owed Rs533bn (Rs122/share), PPL Rs579bn (Rs213/share) (Insight, via Nukta). OGDC and PPL are state-controlled, so dividend policy is set in Islamabad.

1. Brent and Arab Light, daily (ice.com, Platts).

3. PSX announcements for board notices, material information and ex-dates, daily (dps.psx.com.pk/announcements).
4. CCoE and IMF news on the gas debt plan (Business Recorder, Dawn, ProPakistani).
5. SBP reserves (Thursdays) and the rupee (sbp.org.pk).
6. Production and curtailment stories (ppisonline.com, Topline daily).

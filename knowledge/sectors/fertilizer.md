## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Gas allocation and gas price.** Urea is a gas business. Mari-network plants paid a subsidised ~Rs580/mmbtu while SNGPL-network plants paid Rs1,597/mmbtu (feed and fuel) after OGRA's Feb 2024 reset (S&P Global and Fertilizer Daily, Feb 2024; current rate unverified). Plants on imported RLNG are the first switched off when LNG is short. Volume lost to curtailment matters more than the price.
2. **Industry offtake and inventory.** Capacity is about 6.6 Mt a year across 10 operating plants (Profit, 2026-07-09). Pricing power depends on whether stocks are below or above roughly 0.5 to 1.0 Mt.
3. **Retail urea price and discounting.** The domestic price is held by informal industry agreement and political pressure, about Rs4,400 to 4,750 per 50 kg bag in 2026, far below import parity. Margin comes from how much discount is withdrawn.
4. **DAP margin.** FFC (ex-FFBL) and Engro import or make DAP. International DAP and sulphur cost against a farmer-affordability ceiling set the margin. DAP is above Rs15,000 a bag in 2026 and volume has fallen.
5. **Other income, payout and tax.** FFC earns a large share from associates (Askari Bank, power, PMP, TEL). Dividend policy, 5 percent FED on fertilizer, GIDC and super tax decide what shareholders receive.

- **Oct 19:** EFERT board, nine-month results and entitlement (confirmed, PSX notice 2026-10-02). Q3 urea offtake estimate 711k t, +175% q/q (Sherman, via Bol News). Watch the payout, Q2 was cut to 61 percent.
- **About Oct 22 to 28 (estimated):** FFC nine-month results and third interim dividend. Last year the board met Oct 23 and declared Rs9.50. Ex-date likely first week of Nov (2025 was Nov 3). Fatima about Oct 27 and ENGROH about Oct 28 (stockanalysis estimates, no PSX notice seen).
- **Early Nov:** October offtake from Topline and Arif Habib (about the 3rd to 5th).
- **Nov 6:** QatarEnergy force majeure review. An extension means winter RLNG curtailment, which hits Fatima and Agritech first.
- **Nov to Feb:** Rabi urea season. Winter gas diversion to homes and power is the main supply risk.
- **Unscheduled:** India tender outcome, ECC gas decisions, GIDC bill, urea export approval (floated by Intermarket in 2025, unverified for 2026), next SBP MPC (date not found).
- **FFC final dividend:** ex-date about early March (2026 was Mar 6).

## 6. What a veteran watches weekly

1. Monthly offtake and inventory (Topline, Arif Habib, Sherman via propakistani.pk and bolnews.com), 3rd to 5th of month.
2. MNFSR / NFDC Fertilizer Review, mnfsr.gov.pk (Publications), monthly.
3. PSX board meeting notices (7 to 14 days ahead), dps.psx.com.pk/announcements/companies, daily.
4. LNG cargo status and ECC gas decisions (Business Recorder, Profit, propakistani.pk), weekly.
5. India urea tenders and Profercy index, fertilizerdaily.com, about monthly.
6. OGRA wellhead and gas notifications, ogra.org.pk, semi-annual (Jan, Jul).
7. FFC and Engro retail price changes, propakistani.pk, event-driven.
8. SBP MPC, sbp.org.pk, about every six weeks.
9. Senate petroleum committee and ECC on GIDC, event-driven.

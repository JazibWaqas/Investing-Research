# Autos, tractors and auto parts

Written 2026-10-17. The web search tool was exhausted, so brokerage research, margins and FY27 budget measures are not covered (unverified). Price history is raw closes (jumps over 10.5% neutralised, small bonuses unadjusted), so magnitudes are rough.

## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Industry volume, driven by financing cost.** Car sales swing 2x on rates: 216.8k units FY18, 96.8k FY23, 81.6k FY24 (trough), 155.6k FY26 (PAMA monthly workbook). Auto loans: Rs407bn, +33.6% YoY.
2. **Tax and tariff policy.** The 2026-31 auto policy (CBU tariff cap, hybrid parity with petrol cars, 1% sales tax on new energy vehicles) decides whether local assemblers keep protection. Extra duties on localised parts raise cost for assemblers and protect parts makers.
3. **PKR and pricing power.** CKD kits are imported. A stable rupee lets makers hold list prices; a sharp fall means price rises and weaker volumes.
4. **Segment drivers.** Tractors follow farm income and subsidy schemes (Punjab Green Tractor Scheme). Trucks follow construction and freight. Two-wheelers follow incomes and are the steady earner (ATLH).
5. **Market share and payout.** Japanese makers hold about 56% of FY26 volume against about 80% in FY18 (Topline via ProPakistani, 17 May 2026). Chinese brands (Sazgar's Haval, Jetour, MG) take share.

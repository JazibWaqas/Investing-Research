## 1. How this sector makes money

1. **Net spread (the big one).** Return on sukuk and financing minus profit paid to depositors. Meezan H1 2026: return earned Rs222.8bn, paid Rs94.0bn, net spread Rs128.8bn, up only 2% on H1 2025 although deposits grew 13%. The policy rate averaged 10.9% in H1 2026 against 11.8% a year earlier. Cheap funding matters: Meezan's current accounts are 49% of deposits and CASA is 91%, so a rise in rates lifts asset yields faster than funding cost. My rough arithmetic, assumption-heavy: +100bp is worth about +5 to 8% on EPS.
2. **Government sukuk versus private financing.** Industry ADR is 37.7% (Sep 2026) and Meezan's is 43.8%, so most of the balance sheet sits in GoP Ijarah sukuk (Meezan investments Rs2.90tn against financing Rs1.64tn). Earnings follow the sukuk yield curve, not credit demand.
3. **Tax.** The binding constraint. Listed banks paid an effective 52.5% in 2Q26 (Topline via Profit, 29 Aug); Meezan's was 52% (Rs53.7bn on Rs102.6bn PBT, including Rs10.8bn super tax). Any super-tax relief is a direct earnings upside; I found no scheduled change.
4. **Fees, FX and cost.** Meezan fee and other income rose 36% to Rs21.6bn, FX 54%; but expenses rose 26% (93 new branches) and pre-provision profit fell 1%. Sector cost-to-income rose to 51.3% from 45.9%.
5. **Credit cost.** Small now: Meezan NPL 1.8%, coverage 152%; the sector booked a net reversal in 2Q26. Not a driver unless the oil shock turns into defaults.

## 6. What a veteran watches weekly

1. PSX board-meeting notices for MEBL, FABL, BIPL: dps.psx.com.pk/announcements/companies (about 10 days before results).
2. SBP MPC dates and statements: sbp.org.pk (eight meetings a year; fetch blocked here, use Dawn or ProPakistani).
3. Fortnightly T-bill and PIB auction yields against the policy rate: SBP and Mettis (6M bill minus policy rate is the market's hike or cut vote).
4. PBS weekly SPI (Friday) and monthly CPI (first working days): pbs.gov.pk, Dawn.
5. Monthly SBP deposits and ADR (AHL via Profit, mid-month).
6. Government Ijarah sukuk auction results (SBP): these set asset yields.
7. Topline and AHL results previews, about a week before each results day.
8. IMF board calendar and SBP weekly reserves (Thursday).

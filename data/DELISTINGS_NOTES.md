# Delistings and survivorship (built 2026-10-18)

Files: `delistings.csv` (169 rows), `prices_delisted/{SYM}.csv` (daily OHLCV from the portal, 315 files, including dormant names that never traded in the window; 151 of the 169 delistings have a price file), `prices_delisted_log.csv` (every symbol probed), `prices_listed_missing/` (see below), `delisted_last_year_returns.csv`. Scripts: `scripts/fetch_delisted.py`, `scripts/build_delistings.py`, `scripts/survivorship.py`.

## How the list was found
The key finding: the portal `/symbols` list (`data/symbols.csv`, 1,044 rows) still contains most delisted names, and `historical_full` still serves their daily history from Oct 2016 up to their last trade. 302 non-debt, non-ETF symbols in `symbols.csv` had no price file anywhere in the repo, plus announcement-only symbols not in `symbols.csv`. All 493 candidates were probed: 370 returned history, 123 were empty. psxdata fallback was not needed and not tried. The derived KMI All Share and KSE100 lists contain only current symbols (they were built from today's list), so comparing them with `symbols.csv` finds nothing; the real dropouts are the 302 above.

Reason is a keyword classification of announcement titles (voluntary delisting > merger/amalgamation/scheme > defaulter/compulsory > winding up/liquidation), and the date is the last day with volume on the portal (announcement date for names with no price history). Names with no notice but history ending before 1 Sep 2026 are `suspension/unknown`: the data shows they stopped trading, not why. Rights letters, preference shares, TFCs, sukuk and funds are excluded.

## Counts (n = 169)
By reason: voluntary delisting 46, merger 44, suspension/unknown 60, liquidation/winding up 18, default/compulsory 1 (SERF; the other defaulter-segment names, WTL and IML, still trade on 16 Oct 2026 and are excluded).
By year (all reasons): pre-2016 27 (no prices; ULEVER, PMCL and similar), 2016 8, 2017 15, 2018 14, 2019 16, 2020 7, 2021 11, 2022 12, 2023 16, 2024 20, 2025 14, 2026 9. Full year by reason table: recompute with `pd.crosstab` on `delistings.csv`.
Price history: 151 of 169 (141 of the 142 events since Oct 2016). Missing prices are all pre-Oct-2016 events (the portal history starts 2016-10-18) plus a handful of unnamed symbols.

## Survivorship estimate
Method (`scripts/survivorship.py`): add the delisted price files to the panel, compute daily returns with `prices.returns(method="crude")` for both sets (corporate_actions.csv has nothing for delisted names, so crude is used for both to stay comparable), build the equal-weight basket of names with trailing 60-day median traded value above Rs5m (`prices.ew_market`), compound 2017-01-01 to 2026-10-16, with and without the delisted names.

Result: the basket gains almost nothing from dropping them. Without delisted names: +44.1% cumulative (3.80% a year); with: +44.5% (3.83% a year); gap about 0.03 points a year, the wrong sign for the usual survivorship story. Yearly gaps are within +-2 points except 2024 (-6.6: delisted names such as FFBL, PSMC and PIAA took part in the rally). The reason it is small: only 38 delisted names ever pass the Rs5m liquidity filter and they average 7 of about 115 basket members per day. Most delisted names were illiquid textile and leasing shells that the basket never held. Per-name check: for the 97 delisted names with a last full year of data, return from 1 Jan of the last calendar year to the last price has median +19.5% (mean +173%, driven by a few illiquid prints, for example ILTM at Rs2,400). For the 11 that were liquid at the start of that year, the median is -22% (mean +1%). Buyback delistings at a premium (AKZO Rs270, PGCL Rs300, GLPL Rs700) offset collapses (BYCO, SMBL, NIB, SILK).

Caveats: the crude method zeroes large moves, which understates both the crashes and the buyback jumps; a delisted name's last portal price is not a realised payout (squeeze-outs, liquidation payouts and the SMBL/NIB swap ratios are not modelled); `suspension/unknown` names are carried at their last print with no loss assumed, which flatters them. A rough but honest conclusion: for a liquid-only equal-weight basket the survivorship bias is small (under 1 point a year), but the delisted illiquid tail is still missing from any stock-picking backtest that screens on cheapness or low price.

## Not found or not done
- psx.com.pk defaulters/delisted company lists, Wayback Machine, SECP notices and news were not searched (no web access in this run). No ambiguous PDFs were downloaded to `data/raw/filings/delist/`; reasons come from announcement titles only. The 60 `suspension/unknown` rows need that work.
- Announcements start in 2005 but sparsely, so pre-2016 events are incomplete (and have no prices anyway).
- `prices_listed_missing/` holds 55 portal histories for names still trading on 16 Oct 2026 that are not in `panel_close.parquet`, among them POL, APL, PRET, NICL, HASCOL, PASL, UNIC, PIL, NCL, WTL, IML. This is the reverse gap: currently listed names missing from the panel (seed ALLSHR coverage), worth a separate fix. Names with a delisting or merger notice that still traded on 16 Oct 2026 (CJPL, NCL, OLPL, PASM, PIL, UNIC, WTL, IML) are excluded from `delistings.csv` as pending.

## Capital gains tax (PSX listed securities)

- **Acquired from 1 July 2025:** flat 15%, regardless of ATL (filer) status. NCCPL deducts at sale (Profit, 5 Aug 2025).
- **Acquired 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2024:** progressive rates from 12.5% down to 0% by holding period. Pre-July 2013 holdings are exempt. Rates for 2013–2022 and 2024–2025 acquisitions were not verified.
- **Pending:** Finance Bill 2026 proposes removing the exclusion that keeps non-ATL rates lower. Tax experts say this would double Section 37A tax for non-ATL investors. Proposal only (pkrevenue, undated). Enactment and the FY2026-27 rate for filers and non-filers: **unverified**.

## Source conflicts

- Brokerage minimum: tickeranalysts says 0.15% for price at or above Rs 20 (max 2.5%); most brokers charge 0.15–0.50%. The 2017 Profit article says the higher of Rs 0.05/share or 0.25%. The 2017 figure is older.

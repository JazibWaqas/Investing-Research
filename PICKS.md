Buy three large, cheap, dividend-paying KMI names on Monday with 75% of the money and keep buying them every month. Hold them through a 40 to 50% fall. ## How the book is built, and why

**Core 75%, three names, equal weight.** Chosen by the walk-forward's mechanical rule (tests/system_walkforward.md, strategy S3): in the sectors that fit the current rate regime, buy the name with the highest earnings yield and a return on equity of at least 15% (banks: ROE over P/B). The regime is TIGHT (6-month T-bill 12.40% against the 11.5% policy rate, CPI 10.3%, MPC 26 Oct with 61% of brokers expecting a hike), which puts the rule in Islamic banks, E&P and fertilizer. Its picks are FABL, ENGROH and OGDC. I take FABL and OGDC as picked, and FFC in place of ENGROH because ENGROH's 40% ROE is a probable standalone-versus-consolidated artefact (lesson 1) and its holdco discount on market multiples is only 9%. Version 1 of this file overrode FABL with MEBL on franchise quality; both reviews rejected that override because the rule it cited (lesson 6, ROE over P/B) picks FABL (17/1.2 = 14 against MEBL's 34/3.3 = 10) and no measured payoff supports the premium. MEBL runs as a frozen paper position so the judgement can be scored against the rule.

Be clear about what the regime label is worth: the regime tilt has no measured edge (placebo p 0.26), and the S3 trades in these three sectors made a median of +0.3% against +5.9% for its trades elsewhere. The sector choice is context. The core is three index-like names; its expected return is the index's.

**Satellite 25%, one ticket of about Rs12,500 a month.** No stock-level signal passed a fair placebo, so the satellite has no measured edge and stays small until the frozen-prediction scorecard shows a hit rate above its pool after ten or more trades. Each ticket needs a written card, a red team, a trigger, a limit, and a frozen row in catalysts/frozen_predictions.csv before the order.

**Rules.** Four-week rule: satellite money not deployed within four weeks of its deposit goes into the core name furthest below one third. No price stop-losses (a 25% trailing stop cut the best backtested strategy's final value by a sixth by selling its winner). Event stops, acted on within a week: a KMI All Share deletion notice, a deal lapsing or the offer not being funded, an omitted dividend without explanation, an adverse related-party transfer. Cap 25% per name at full size; trim only above 35%. Sell a core name when its written killer fires or when a regime change removes its sector (the rotation rule in knowledge/playbooks/sector_rotation.md: CPI down two prints and the 6-month T-bill below the policy rate at two auctions).

**Odds and confidence.** Bear, base and bull values are the cards' scenarios after red team. The reviews are right that every card's point estimate (+2% to +10% over 12 months) sits below the 12-month T-bill; the reason to buy anyway is that the first deposit is about 1% of eight years of deposits, hike years have lost to bills but the one that did not (2023, +53%) was unrecoverable for anyone who waited, and this mandate cannot hold bills. Dividends are the next four payments from last year's pattern (catalysts/dividend_calendar.csv), net of 15% withholding tax.
